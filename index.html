Phone, tablet and desktop

Every screen here follows the three form-factor buckets: phone under 600px, tablet 600 to 1023px, desktop 1024px and up. Same single column in each, relaxed on tablet, capped and centred on desktop. Resize this window to see it.

Recipe search, four directions

Fragment

The "very intelligent" search for recipes: describe it, stock first, pick and type, or a conversation. Results show what you have and what's missing.

Open: which direction leads, whether it explains how it read you, and what a result carries into the recipe page.

Fragment

Tapping a Pantry item opens a bottom sheet to about half the screen. Four ways to lay out its details and edits. The owner chose the tile grid (option 2); it is now wired into the household flow.

Open: see docs/knowledge/item-sheet.md for the proposals the flow had to make.
