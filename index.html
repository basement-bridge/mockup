Preferences, option A: one list

Fragment

Every preference in one list. Each row shows the value in force and where it comes from; a You / Household switch shows the other level. Drawn from what the code really holds. Kept for reference: the owner chose option B on 10 October. Chips say App, Household or You; Stock level checks; quiet hours default 10pm to 6am and can be set to none; most suggestions per day is 1 to 20, default 3. Only household admins can change the household's values.

Kept for reference (option B chosen, D15). Open: whether Recipe stars get a person level, and whether Preferences replaces Stock level checks.

Preferences, option B: Mine and Household

Fragment

One Preferences screen, two tabs. Mine shows each value in force and where it comes from (App, Household or You). Stock level checks hold quiet hours, the daily limit, level and how often by place (milk once a week, proteins in the freezer quiet), and the switched-off list. Household and personal notes sit side by side. Kitchen role and assistant moved to the profile. Household is managed on its own tab, and only household admins can change it.

Chosen direction (D15), 10 Oct. Open: which rule wins between category and location, a person's broad rule against the household's narrow one, notes that disagree, where the household name lives, what "per day" counts. Locked 10 Oct: household values are changed by household admins only (the founding member is admin and grants it), personal values are ungated, cap 1 to 20 (default 3, D20).

Preferences, option C: propose for another member

Fragment

One housemate notes something about another ("Sam avoids peanuts"). It lands on Sam's own list, is used at once, and Sam keeps, changes or removes it at the next login. The proposer is never told what Sam decided, and nothing expires. Not an approval gate. Built on option B's layout.

Awaiting approval. Locked 10 Oct: any member may propose (not gated by role), a reject never rewrites a meal already planned, the proposer is not told, nothing expires. Open: who sees a pending record, take back, a conflicting record, which statements, where the entry lives.
