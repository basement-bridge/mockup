Plan it: the day rows

Just the day rows of option F, on their own. Mockup only, nothing is saved. A rolling list of the next seven real days, today first (not locked to Monday to Sunday). Each row: a square label with the day name on the left, what is already planned that day on the right, read only and in full, no date. Click or use the arrow keys to move; Enter picks. In option F, picking commits at once.

Sample: today is Wednesday 14 October 2026, so the list runs Wed to Tue. Thursday is the usual day off, Friday holds your own meal, Sunday holds two meals, Monday has a long name. Wed · Mon · Sat (start day).
