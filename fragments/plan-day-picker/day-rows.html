Plan it: the day rows

Just the day rows of option F, on their own, with the scenarios. Mockup only, nothing is saved. A rolling list of the next seven real days, today first. Each row: a square day-name label on the left (no date), what is planned on the right, read only and in full, and under it what picking the row does. Picking a row commits at once in option F (here it only says what it would do).

Mockup only
The scenarios in this list (today is Wednesday)
  • Thu: a usual day off, not locked (owner). Tue: off this week only. Under “Off days refuse” both say what the code answers today (day_blocked).
  • Fri: a meal you planned yourself (yours). Sun: another person’s own meal. Sat: a household meal. Mon: a household meal where Jo has her own plan too: the row shows the household plan with a peek behind it; Show (the strip under the row, or the right arrow key) opens that night’s full picture, Hide (or left arrow) folds it.
  • Change Who is it for to see what a row shows and does for each person. Turn on the Hard avoid to see the stop and the warning (Leave Jo out is the action in the flyout).

Arrow keys move between rows, Enter picks, Right and Left open and fold a night. The scenario table, with what matches today’s code and what is new, is in docs/knowledge/plan-day-picker.md.
