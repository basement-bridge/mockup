Plan it: how to choose the day on desktop

Seven live, clickable options for Kitchie issue #757. Mockup only, nothing is saved or sent. Each one is the desktop Home page with the Mandarin Mango Yogurt Bowl open in the right-hand pane: press Add to plan (or P) and try it. The phone sheet is not changed in any option. The owner has not chosen among A to G.

He wants to choose by looking at working options, not by prose. Raised the same day, typed: “raise an issue about the best way to do the selection of day of week to plan on the desk..”.

Owner rule, 16 October 2026 (voice), in every option

Under Cooking ideas, when the primary recommendation is the meal already planned for tonight (the “Planned for today” card) it has no Plan button, on the card or in the right-hand pane: the person has already made that plan. If nothing is planned for tonight the primary recommendation does have Plan. The Other ideas rows always keep Plan, so a person can override what they planned (Replace on the desktop Home card). Open both states: nothing planned · planned.

Rules every option keeps (from the issue)
  • This week and up to four weeks ahead; past days greyed; a usual day off marked and still allowed (“cooks this week only”); a day with a meal gets a second meal; Hold on by default; the Hard avoid guard is asked live when the day or the person changes (SW-74 to SW-82); on the desktop Home card a day with your own meal is replaced (owner, 12 October 2026).
  • Undo: the same 14-second chip as today (here the Home toast, with Z). Undo puts a replaced meal back.
Proposals (the owner was silent)
  • PD-P1. Today is preselected in A and C unless it is past, a usual day off, or already has a meal; then the next free day in view; if none, nothing. A day that would take a second meal is never preselected, so Enter cannot add a second meal by accident (the issue says “a past or full day never preselected”; reading “full” as “already has a meal”). Wrong guess: the usual case on a busy evening takes a click.
  • PD-P2. Letters and digits jump to a day in A and C: 1 to 7 is Monday to Sunday; M T W F S jump to that day, and T and S pressed again cycle to Thursday and Sunday. In C they act within the week of the chosen day. Wrong guess: letters clash with a later single-key shortcut (Kitchie #594).
  • PD-P3. C is four rows (this week and three ahead, 28 days); the strip today goes to four weeks ahead (five weeks). The grid is one week short of the cap. Options: a fifth row, or keep four. Needs the owner.
  • PD-P4. C and the Plan page show the meals of all weeks, so C needs the plan for four weeks when it opens (one range read; the sheet today reads this week only, and later weeks in the strip say Free). A, B and E are drawn as the sheet draws them today: Free for later weeks.
  • PD-P5. B: a click on Tonight on a free day plans it at once (one click, Undo available); a day with a meal or a usual day off is chosen and waits for Plan. Who it is for and Hold sit above the quick row and are used as set. Wrong guess: a click that plans is too quick; make it two steps.
  • PD-P6. D: a drop is always a second meal; Replace stays a Home-card rule. After a drop the page stays on the week with the meal in its day and a Back to Home button.
  • PD-P7. Esc closes any of the pickers without planning (plain Esc closes nothing on Home otherwise, so this is new for an open picker only). Focus returns to Add to plan.
  • PD-P8. The full-width bottom sheet in A, B and E is the best reading of the code and the description of the owner's screenshot; the screenshot itself was not attached. The width of the sheet's content on a wide screen is a guess.

Loads (DESIGN.md section 6)

Nothing is loaded by any existing page: this is a new fragment. The host is the Home mockup (home-detail-panel styles and scripts, reused, not copied), plus picker.css (about 9 KB), data.js (sample), common.js (shared rules, the guard, planning) and exactly one option file: pick-c.js (grid, with grid.js), pick-f.js (flyout, grid.js), pick-g.js (curtain, grid.js), pick-d.js, or sheet.js with pick-a.js, pick-b.js or pick-e.js. All of it is desktop-only and none of it is in a shared bundle. In the build, fetch the chosen picker on the first press of Add to plan (or first focus of it), like the sheet today. This host loads the recipe view up front instead of on first use (scaffolding). No images, no fonts added. Where it lands: in place, over Home (DESIGN.md section 6a); D is the one option that leaves the page, drawn in place here for the mockup.
