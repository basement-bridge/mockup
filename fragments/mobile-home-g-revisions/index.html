Mobile Home G, revisions, round 2: urgency ranking, countdown window, drag to reveal Options A to C (D retired)

G stays as drawn (fragments/mobile-home-g). Round 1 (PR #281) is merged; this page is round 2 on the owner’s decisions of 23 Oct 2026. Same URL. Authoritative decisions for the build are in docs/knowledge/jobs-to-be-done/mobile-home-g-revisions.md; this page lets you play them.

Owner decisions (Arjan), 23 Oct 2026, applied here:
  1. Three equal chevron sections, each absent when empty and optional: More ideas (the hero’s own extension), On the way out, Running low. The hero is always first and never touched.
  2. Urgency = consequence + neglect. On the way out gets more urgent the longer it is ignored. Running low is urgent only when a planned meal needs it or it is at zero. Running low can take slot 2 ahead of On the way out. The time-of-day order is gone; urgency ranks.
  3. A global sliding countdown window in hours: anything going off inside it is urgent even if the date is tomorrow. The date label stays honest.
  4. An expired or off-today ingredient keeps the idea eligible (hero too) but with a red alert and words. Expiring stock is also its own idea in More ideas.
  5. Open by urgency and height: chevrons open in rank order until the phone’s height is spent; the urgent one always opens; scrolling never changes state. On the way out shows 1 or 2 items and “+N more”; Running low is uncapped.
  6. A manual open or close is remembered within a sitting (hops between Home, Pantry, Recipes) and reset after a real gap away.
  7. Drag up on a header to reveal, continuing into the next header as one motion; tap also toggles.
  8. Kept: search in the greeting row, takeover per G, no This week on mobile.
6 hAnything going off inside this many hours is urgent, even tomorrow. Default 6 h is a guess.
Sitting30 min is a guess.

The switches redraw all three frames. Under each frame, the report is measured from the drawn frame: a part is above the fold only if all of it is. Try it: with Short and Nothing urgent every section is closed. Press a header and drag up (finger or mouse): it opens, and if you keep going over the headers above it, each opens in turn. Open or close something by hand, then press Hop away 1 min (kept) or Away 45 min (forgotten). Move the window to 12 h on Expiring in hours and watch the ranking change; tomorrow is still called tomorrow. The clock is fixed at 14:00 on a Friday, tonight not planned. The phone height is the whole frame including the 68px bottom bar; scrolling a frame is normal, not a failure.

A section scores its worst item. 50 and up is urgent. Sections rank by score, ties On the way out, Running low, More ideas. The hero is always first. Open rule: in rank order, a section opens while it fits whole above the fold; an urgent one always opens (A, C); the first that does not fit stops the rest. Date labels come from the calendar day: Today 23:00, Tomorrow 06:00, never “today” for tomorrow.

Recommendation

C, plain chevrons. It is decisions 1 and 5 as written (three equal sections; the order and the default open carry the urgency) and the least to learn. Keep the word badge. A’s tint is a polish to add only if testing shows an urgent section being missed; B is the fallback only if a tall open section above everything else is too much on a short phone. D is retired. Not a recommendation but a risk: the drag gesture makes headers dead to a scroll that starts on them (MR-Q9); try it on a real phone before building it.

Proposals (the owner was silent; each is a guess, with the cost of a wrong guess)

Round 1 MR-P1 to P12 are carried in the doc with their status (kept, replaced or reversed). New in round 2:

  • MR-P13 Scores as in the table above (60, 50, 20, 10, 5, neglect +15 a day to 3 days). Guess. Wrong guess: a section ranks one place off; constants in scoreSoon().
  • MR-P14 Running low scores 90, 55, 50, 5. Guess. Wrong guess: “gone with nothing riding” (50) ties the lowest urgent; it can only outrank On the way out when it is 55 or 90.
  • MR-P15 More ideas scores 30; ties go On the way out, Running low, More ideas. Guess. Effect: a calm On the way out (under 30) sits below More ideas.
  • MR-P16 The window is global, default 6 h, range 0 to 48 on this page. 6 h is a guess. A plan does not remove window urgency (MR-Q7).
  • MR-P17 “Ignored” is days an unplanned item has sat in On the way out. Guess. The app needs a first-listed time and an “acted” event.
  • MR-P18 Warning: expired gives a red alert and “Check this is still OK: uses milk that went off yesterday”; goes off today (calendar day) gives the same mark and “uses chicken that goes off today at 17:00”. Tomorrow never warns, even inside the window. Whether “today” deserves the same mark is a guess (MR-Q12).
  • MR-P19 More ideas rows: expiring stock (inside 48 h, not on a plan, not the hero’s) is its own idea, worst first, then fillers; 3 rows (2 on Short).
  • MR-P20 Caps unchanged (On the way out 2, 1 on Short, then “+N more”; Running low uncapped).
  • MR-P21 Session: manual opens and closes are kept per section until a gap of 30 min or more; a data refresh does not reset them. 30 is a guess.
  • MR-P22 Drag: arms after 8 px of mostly upward movement (guess); opens the pressed section, then every closed header the pointer reaches above it; a drag only opens, a tap or Enter toggles.
  • MR-P23 Option changes (table above); D retired.
