The four options

The ladder (all four options start from it)

  1. Hero: always first, never touched, never collapses. Make it and Plan.
  2. Slot 2 is earned by urgency. On the way out, if anything is due today or already past, takes it. Otherwise (Question MR-Q1) Running low takes it only if something is at zero; otherwise slot 2 is Other ideas.
  3. Below slot 2 everything is normal scrolling content: other ideas, then the chevron sections in G’s fixed order, each open or closed by tap.
  4. Caps when open: On the way out shows the most urgent 2 items (1 on the short phone) and “+N more · View all”; Running low shows all it has.
  5. Height decides how much more opens by default. What fits whole above the fold opens; the rest stays closed and is one tap away.

Recommendation

A, with C’s height rule on the chevrons below it. A is the owner’s rule as he said it: the urgent section takes slot 2, expanded, ahead of other ideas and Running low. B is the fallback if a 190px card on a short phone is too much (it costs 53px). C is worth trying for tall phones only. D does not promote, so it is the least like what he asked for, but it never moves anything, which fits “not too jumpy”.

Proposals (the owner was silent; each is a guess, with the cost of a wrong guess)

  • MR-P1 Urgent means due today or already past (days ≤ 0). Tomorrow is not urgent. Guess. Wrong guess: a household that wants a day’s notice sees nothing promoted until the day itself; one constant.
  • MR-P2 “Critical” for Running low means none left (level or quantity zero). Guess. Wrong guess: “1 left” never earns the slot.
  • MR-P3 Caps: On the way out shows 2 items when open, 1 on the short phone, most urgent first (expired, then today, then by date), with “+N more · View all”. Running low uncapped. The 1 and 2 are guesses.
  • MR-P4 Phone heights are 600 (a small phone, or a browser with its bars showing), 740, and 844 (the owner’s 390 x 844). Guesses. The fold is the frame minus the 68px bottom bar. Other ideas shows 2 rows on the short phone, 3 otherwise. Wrong guess: the breakpoints sit in the wrong place; they are in HEIGHTS and facts().
  • MR-P5 A part counts as above the fold only if all of it is. A half-shown card is reported as peeking, and the model never opens a section that would be cut.
  • MR-P6 Urgency is words, never colour alone: “Expired yesterday”, “Today” in the row, a word badge in the header. The bare count steps aside when a badge is there.
  • MR-P7 Other ideas gives way by moving, not vanishing: in A, B and D it stays as rows and falls below the fold; in C it becomes a chevron section. Wrong guess: a person wanted it gone, not just lower.
  • MR-P8 The order of the chevrons below slot 2 is fixed (On the way out, then Running low). G’s time-of-day order() is not re-drawn here (at cooking time G puts Running low first); whether it still applies is MR-Q4.
  • MR-P9 Smart defaults are worked out once when Home opens, never re-worked while you look at it, so nothing jumps under a thumb; a tap is kept for the visit and not remembered after. Wrong guess: someone who always opens a section taps each time.
  • MR-P10 The hero must not be built on something already expired (the sample uses chicken that goes off today, never milk that is past). Food safety, not taste. The ranking in suggest_meals has to enforce it.
  • MR-P11 B’s test is “two other ideas still fit”; D’s test is “the whole open section fits”. Both are guesses.

Open questions

  • MR-Q1 Should Running low ever outrank On the way out for the second slot? Recommendation: only if nothing in On the way out is urgent and Running low has something at zero (drawn: the switch “Running low: Zero outranks”; “Never outranks” shows the alternative). Reason: going off is lost today, running low is lost tonight only if the shop is not done. Cost: on a day with a zero item and nothing urgent, Other ideas is one row lower.
  • MR-Q2 Which option? Recommendation: A with C’s fit on the chevrons. Cost: see the table.
  • MR-Q3 Is tomorrow urgent after 20:00? Recommendation: no, keep “today or past”. Cost: a late planner sees tomorrow’s going-off items only in the chevron.
  • MR-Q4 Does G’s moment-based order of the two chevrons still apply below slot 2? Recommendation: yes, unchanged. Cost: none drawn here.
  • MR-Q5 Should a manual open or close be remembered? Recommendation: no (MR-P9).
  • MR-Q6 Should an expired item be eligible to appear in the hero at all? Recommendation: never (MR-P10).
