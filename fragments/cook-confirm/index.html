After the cook: what happens to the pantry

Four ways to confirm what a cook did to stock, on the screen and in chat. Kitchie #788. Exploration only: the owner compares, nothing here is a recommendation.

Two axesThe recipe side is exact (grams, mL, counts). How Kitchie tracks the item is the other axis: level only, counted, measured, or measured with a level you pinned. The cook behaviour follows the shape.
Propose, never write silentlyEven clean arithmetic (2 potatoes minus 1) is a proposal. Nothing reaches stock until the person confirms. Override a line, then one confirm.
Low is where it asksPlenty and some are left alone. A low item is probably out, so cooking is the moment to check. The cue shows which way an item is heading.
Not the stock checkThis is a deliberate cook-time moment. The household stock-check setting is a different trigger (staleness) and is not used here.

Direction cue: which way an item is heading

Filled pips are what is there, dashed pips are what is about to be lost, the arrow and words say so (not colour alone). It is shown on every line, so a person can see it without being asked.

What the screen receives


  
One proposal, computed once in Kitchie. The version decides what is asked; the confidence decides how the surface says it.
