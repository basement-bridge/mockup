Cooking: the ingredients this step uses

Owner ask, 16 Oct 2026 (voice): while cooking step by step it is a real inconvenience not to know which ingredients the current step involves. Each step should show the ingredients and the amount that step uses (2 of the 3 eggs, 1 cup of flour), highlighted, with the full list kept neutral. Three options below; A is recommended. Data shape and schema: analysis. Job note: cook-step-ingredients.md. Nothing is built.

Sample
Serves 4

A Callout under the steprecommended

A highlighted card right under the step words lists only this step's ingredients with this step's amounts. Below it the rest of the list stays plain: still to come first, already used last, each with its step number in words.

B Chips inside the step card

Smallest footprint: the amounts sit as chips inside the step card, so the words never move down. The full list is one tap away in a disclosure. Wraps at 360; long lists collapse after five.

C Dock above the buttons

A one-line dock sits just above the step buttons (thumb reach) and opens in place to the full callout. Step words keep the whole screen. Costs one tap on a long list; on desktop the dock is simply the open side panel.

What every option does the same (proposals, see the job note)

  • Only stored links. The screen reads the links saved with the recipe and does arithmetic on them. Nothing is guessed when you open a step.
  • Split ingredient. Shows "1 of 2" with the rest named by step: "1 left for step 2". An earlier use reads "1 already used in step 1".
  • Amounts scale with the Serves control by the same rule the ingredients screen uses. Fractions round to quarters and thirds; nothing is adjusted to make parts add up.
  • Unknown amount shows a dash and "Amount not given". It is never filled in.
  • Step with no ingredients (saved as checked and empty) says so in one quiet line. A step that was never checked says nothing.
  • Not tagged yet: the step screen is exactly today's. No strip, no hint, no nag. Half tagged: strips only on tagged steps, and no still-to-come list (it could be wrong).
  • Not colour alone: a heading with a count, a left bar plus a tick glyph, and words for state ("Already used", "Step 4"). No gesture hint text.

Open questions, and what a wrong guess costs

  1. Strip first, or step words first, on a long list? A shows the strip first (capped at five). Cost: more scrolling before the step words end on 360. C avoids it.
  2. Show the still-to-come list at all? Proposal yes, only on a fully tagged recipe. Cost: clutter for people who only want the strip.
  3. "Used in step 1" for an ingredient the cook never ticked off. Proposal: it describes the recipe, not what was done. Cost: a cook who skipped a step sees it as used.

Default path and deferred (DESIGN.md section 6)

No new request: the links ride in the recipe already read for cook mode.
