Proposing a preference for another member

One housemate notes something about another ("Sam avoids peanuts"). It goes straight onto Sam's own list, is used at once, and Sam is told at their next login and can keep it, change it or remove it. Not an approval gate. Built on option B's layout.

What this option does

The owner's shape (voice, 10 October 2026), recorded as C-D1 to C-D4. It is not a hard approval gate. Option C itself, and every open question below, is awaiting approval.

  • C-D1Any member proposes for another member. Preferences > Mine > Food > Add something for a housemate. Pick the person, say what (avoids, dislikes, likes, usually) and the thing.
  • C-D2It is written on the subject's own list from the start. Not on the household list and not in a shared bucket. The record belongs to Sam and is tagged proposed by Arjan, with a pending flag (chip Waiting for you on Sam's list).
  • C-D3It takes effect at once. Recipe building and planning read it straight away, so an allergy does not wait for Sam to log in. The proposer's screen says so in three lines before the button, and again after.
  • C-D4Sam gets a courtesy notice at the next login (a banner, plus a standing row Added for you). Three answers: Keep it as it is (clears the pending flag, keeps the attribution), Change it (Sam's version replaces the value), Remove it (the record is gone).

Which option it builds on

Option B. Same Preferences screen: the Mine / Household tabs, the .sp frame and sub-screen rules copied unchanged, the source chips (Just you, Everyone), food rows, .banner, .empty, .popt radios, .btn and .btn.ghost. Option A's one-list-with-a-switch does not fit, because the point here is that the record is not on a shared level. The stock-check, notes, assistant and stops rows are not redrawn: they are exactly option B's. New controls (all tokens, no raw colours): .src.wait, .pact, .happens, .av, .sug, .hold.

The four things to check

  1. Proposer's flow. 1 Propose for Sam: who, what, how strict (avoids only), then three lines on what the button does, then Add to Sam's list now. 1 Added confirms it is in use and on Sam's list. 1 Proposer's list shows what is waiting, with Take back (a reading).
  2. Pending look and use. 2 Pending on Sam's list puts waiting records first with the attribution line. 2 What another member sees (Jo): a read-only line for each pending record, never the rest of Sam's list. 2 Planning uses it now: the peanut noodles are left out for Sam before Sam has seen anything, and a meal already planned is flagged.
  3. Next login. 3 Next-login notice (banner, with Review or Later; Later does not clear the flag). 3 Keep, change or remove shows what each answer leaves behind. 3 Remove asks once is Kitchie's existing rule for a safety avoidance. 3 Change it is Sam's edit.
  4. States. Pending, accepted, edited, rejected and nothing waiting, in the table below.

What the code holds, and what would have to change Checked 10 Oct 2026

Read again on the default branches: Kitchie main 7e6fd08, Recipe main 5cec532, platform main 409f44f. Summary and sources: docs/knowledge/user-preferences/option-c-propose-for-another-member.md.

  • Today a member can only write their own record or the household's. give_feedback takes who as "me" or "household", and the member always comes from the link, never an argument. There is no way to write on another member's list. Change: name the subject member, checked server side as a member of the same household.
  • Person-level food statements exist (context_claims, member_id set). Change: add proposed_by, proposed_at, an acknowledgment state (pending, accepted, edited) and when; rejected is the existing retired status.
  • Any member can already write a household claim and nothing addresses a claim to a member. There is a household-changes banner (joins and removals, dismissed per member, deleted after 60 days) and "new" badges, but nothing to claim back. Change: the notice can be a query over pending records on the member's own list, so no queue table is needed; only the "Later" dismissal needs storing.
  • Reads stop at the caller. get_context returns the caller's own and the household's; "another member's are never read", and plans carry for_whom but do not read anyone's claims. For "planning picks it up right away" to be true, the plan and recipe read must include the avoidances of each member the meal is for. That is a change to the visibility rule, and it also applies to Sam's own avoidances today.
  • Accept, edit, remove map to existing feedback kinds (accepted, corrected, a retire) but v1 only notes accepted and never acts on it; here it would clear the flag. Tool texts say everything returned was stated and Established: a proposal is stated by someone else, so it needs its own source.
  • Recipe stars and votes stay household only and platform stores no preferences (its token carries sub, household, products, times): option C changes neither. It is a Kitchie change, plus a web page for food statements, which does not exist yet (option B proposed one).

Open questions Awaiting approval

The shape leaves these open. The mockup draws the reading given; a wrong guess costs a redraw, and where noted a data change.

  1. C-Q1Who may propose? Reading: any member, for any other member of the same household, from a personal link. Not for themselves, not from the shared link.
  2. C-Q2What does a reject do to plans already made? Reading: Kitchie edits nothing. While the record is in force an earlier meal is flagged (Check this meal); after a reject the flag goes. A wrong guess on a safety item is the costly one.
  3. C-Q3Is the proposer told? Reading: yes, quietly, as a line on their own list (Sam kept it, changed it, removed it) until they tap Got it. No push.
  4. C-Q4How long does the pending flag last? Reading: until the subject answers, with no expiry, and dismissing the banner does not clear it. Household notices are deleted after 60 days today; the flag must not follow that. A member who never signs in (a child) would stay pending for good.
  5. C-Q5Can the subject see it before login? Reading: no. But the assistant reads the record at once, so Sam's assistant could mention it in chat before Sam opens the app. Is that the notification, or should it stay quiet?
  6. C-Q6Who can see a pending record? Reading: the subject sees it on their list; the proposer and every other member see a read-only line (person, thing, who proposed, status), and nothing else of the subject's list. Wrong guess: privacy of a personal list, or surprise at a meal being held back.
  7. C-Q7Can the proposer take it back or change it while pending? Reading: take back only, until answered.
  8. C-Q8If the subject already has a conflicting record (Sam likes peanuts, Arjan proposes avoids peanuts)? Reading (not drawn): a proposal never retires the subject's own record; both stand and the stricter is used.
  9. C-Q9Which statements can be proposed? Reading: avoids, dislikes, likes, usually, all with the same immediate effect. Not never suggest or fact.
  10. C-Q10What grade is an unacknowledged proposal? The evidence note says safety limits "come from what the person states". This one is stated by someone else. Reading: used at once (the safe direction for an avoidance), shown as proposed, never as the subject's own words, until answered.
  11. C-Q11After an edit, does the attribution stay? Reading: yes, "proposed by Arjan, changed by you", so provenance is not lost. Accepted keeps it, as the owner said.
  12. C-Q12Does a safety avoidance ask twice? Reading: yes, Kitchie's existing rule applies to Sam removing or softening it (one confirmation).
  13. C-Q13Where does the entry live? Reading: Mine > Food. Alternative: from a member's row in Settings > People.

Proposals Proposal

Choices the mockup made where the owner was silent (separate from the open questions above).

  • C-P1Pending chip words: Waiting for you to the subject, Waiting for Sam to everyone else. Once answered the chip is the ordinary Just you.
  • C-P2Pending records are listed first in the Food card so a new one cannot be missed.
  • C-P3The three answers are full-width rows, icon first, with one line each on what they leave behind. Keep is the filled one.
  • C-P4Strictness and reason can be "Not sure"; the model then treats it as none at all and for safety until the subject says.
  • C-P5A standing row Added for you keeps the review reachable after the banner is dismissed.
  • C-P6Household tab says plainly that proposals never appear there.
  • C-P7The planning stand-in flags a meal planned earlier while the record is in force.

Loads

Default path: this page, option-c.css (about 15 KB) and option-c.js (about 30 KB), no images, no network, nothing stored. option-c-600.css is for tablet and up and option-c-1024.css for desktop, each applied by a media attribute, so a phone does not use them. Nothing needed is deferred: the propose, review and plan screens are drawn when opened. In the real app the closed Mine tab needs one count (pending records on my list) for the banner; the pending records themselves, the proposer's list and the plan check load when opened.
