Recipe page, four layouts

Fragment. Egg fried rice as the example. A recipe page generally carries a picture of the finished dish, time and servings, ingredients, steps, and sometimes a picture per step. Ours adds what we know: what's in your pantry, your own variations, and nutrition per serving. Four ways to organise it. All content is sample content.

Many recipes have no step pictures, so every layout has to hold up without them. Scroll inside each phone to see the whole page.
