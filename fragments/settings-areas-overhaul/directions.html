Settings, the other areas: three directionsfor the owner to choose

Owner, 24 Oct 2026 (Kitchie #1110, #1090): the front of Settings is built and chosen (settings-shakeup). The other areas still feel “Java ME like”: too many nested menus and lists. Shortcuts must be the control itself. Radically simpler. This page is an overhaul of everything under the front: the profile menu, Household, the profile (my name and picture), invite, Preferences, Kitchen setup and Assistants. Phone only. Not built; nothing here is approved for build.

Three directions, every screen, both widths, light and dark, text to +30%. Each direction below shows every area it has, playable. The front is the chosen design, drawn still, only so the taps from it can be counted. What stands from the chosen design is kept: the control is the shortcut (a switch, a stepper, two times, in the row itself), double tap resets to the default, no pills, no counts, no explainer lines, 44px targets, search reaches everything (search is as decided and is not redrawn here). One phone at a time to play: A, B, C.

A

A. Flat pages

recommended

The three doors stay and each becomes one flat page. Household, Preferences, Kitchen setup (Assistants inside it), plus You under Household. Every setting is a control in its own row: a switch, a stepper, two times, chips you tap. Nothing opens another page except a small sheet to add a rule. Mine or Everyone is one two-way control. This is mostly deletion and merging of what UAT already has.

Front rows: unchanged (three). Screens below the front: 4 pages and 1 sheet. Trade-off: still a list of rows, so it is the least different from today; Preferences is long (about two screens) because it keeps every setting.

B

B. One note

One door, “Our kitchen”, and a short note in plain sentences. The underlined words are the controls, the same pattern as the History search sentence: “Kitchie may ask me to check amounts, never between 9pm and 7am, and no more than 5 a day.” Tap may and it flips. Tap 9pm and the two times open right under the sentence. No rows, no pages, no tabs.

Front rows: one, which changes the three the owner chose (SR-Q50). Screens below the front: 1 page and 1 sheet. Trade-off: the least to read and the least to learn, but words are a weaker target than a row (at least 44px tall, often narrower than 44px wide), long lists (categories, spots) are hidden behind a word, and wrapping sentences at +30% text run long. Hardest to build well.

C

C. Faces

Organised by who it is about, not by feature. Household is the household’s name, a row of faces and what is true for everyone. Tap a face for that person: your own face holds your name, picture, role, your preferences and your assistants; someone else’s holds their food, the admin switch and Remove; the plus face invites. Preferences stops being a place. The sheets open in place over Household.

Front rows: two (Household, Kitchen setup), which changes the three the owner chose. Screens below the front: 2 pages and 4 sheets. Trade-off: the most original and the best fit for “my” against “everyone’s”, but your sheet is the longest screen of the three, Assistants move away from Kitchen setup (the link acts as you), and nobody finds Preferences by name.

Recommendation: A

  • Why A. It is the only direction that keeps the owner’s three decided rows and still removes what he disliked: no tabs, no editor that opens inside a row, no Members and admins page, no Housemate pages, no picker pages, no Kitchen sub pages, no AI panel pages. Its controls are the plain ones he already approved on the front (a switch, a stepper, a row). It is the best fit for his vision needs: rows are big, steady targets that scale with the text. And it is mostly deletion and merging of what UAT already has, so it is the cheapest and safest to build.
  • What it gives up. It is still rows. If he finds A still “Java ME”, the next step is not B or C wholesale but B’s sentence for the Preferences page only (the heaviest page), with A everywhere else.
  • Why not B. It is the boldest, but words are small, wandering targets for someone who needs strong contrast and large targets, long lists hide behind a word, and it changes the three rows he chose. Worth playing before dismissing.
  • Why not C. It is the best idea about my against everyone’s and it may be where the product goes with more households, but it dissolves Preferences, which he chose to keep as a row, and it makes your sheet the longest screen. Borrow its idea if people multiply: faces on Household.

Loads

Default path: theme.css, theme.js, shared.css, ../settings-shakeup/shake.css (the chosen front’s styles, reused so the areas lift with it), areas.css, areas.js. No search engine, no index and no other theme file load: the front is drawn still and search is not part of this page. Every change happens in place (a row opens, a sheet slides over the page, a chip becomes an editor); no page loads when a control, a row or a sheet is used.

Checked, and what is not

tools/settings-areas-overhaul-check.mjs (headless Chromium) plays every screen of A, B and C at 390 by 844 and 360 by 640, light and dark, at Match my phone and +30%: no console error, no sideways scroll, nothing wider than the phone, every control at least 44px tall (words in B exempt in width), each tap sequence of the table above, the behaviour of every control (switch, stepper, times, chips, picture, role, invite kind, remove, leave, connect, default, link), that this page’s numbers equal the played numbers, that the table covers every area in all three, no hex colour in areas.css, the note and the review queue.
