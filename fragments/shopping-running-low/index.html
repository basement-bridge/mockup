Shopping: a link to what's running low

Phone Shopping screen with a way to open the Pantry's Running low view (/pantry?show=low, the view Home's tile opens). Five placements (B chosen by the owner, 23 Oct 2026), drawn on the app's real markup (web-shopping.ts), each with the hand-off card ("Is someone else shopping?") and the hint line kept. Everything here is a Proposal except the choice of B.

Owner, voice, 22 Oct 2026: "We'd still need the is someone else shopping option. We need the tick as you drop things in the basket small instruction. And we're going to need a way that's just a link to items running low. I don't know. Mock up some options for me and try to maintain as much screen space as we can on the mobile." (He had asked for "a link to running low pantry items from the shopping list"; on his laptop he could see the desktop header row, on the phone there is none.)

Other states

  • With two links out (A): the card and the links stay on top; the Running low link does not move them. Also B and E.
  • Nothing on the list: no new link. The existing "Nothing on the list yet" card already has "See what's running low" (shop-empty); it stays as it is.

Decisions and open questions

  1. RL-1 Where it goes. One of A to E. Open question 1. A wrong guess costs one small CSS and markup change; nothing stored changes.
  2. RL-2 Label. "Running low", the word Home, the Pantry preset and the Shopping empty card already use. Proposal: with the count in brackets (A) or as a badge (B, C, D, E).
  3. RL-3 The count. The number of entries the Pantry preset keeps (home.ts lowKeys), so it matches what the link opens. Sample value 3 here. Those entries are already on the shopping list; the link is for seeing them in the Pantry (levels, where to look at home). Open question 2: is that what is wanted, or should it open something else?
  4. RL-4 Empty is absent. With nothing running low, or nothing on the list, the link is not drawn (docs/design-principles.md). The empty list keeps its own button.
  5. RL-5 Where it goes. A plain link to <base>/pantry?show=low; works with no script, and the Pantry opens in the view it already has.
  6. RL-6 Desktop. Not drawn here. Proposal: the same link at the right end of the 1.5rem "N to get" header row (.shop-hd, desktop only), since the row already has room and the phone's hint-line placement is a touch wording desktop does not need. The phone placements are hidden from 64rem up (one rule).
  7. RL-7 Tablet. Takes the phone's placement (tablet is not drawn separately).

What loads, what is deferred (DESIGN.md section 6)

Nothing new on load: one anchor and, for the chosen option, a few lines in the existing Shopping stylesheet block. No script, no request, no new file. The count comes from the same list the page already works out. The tap goes to the Pantry as an ordinary navigation (a failed read is the only fallback; there is nothing to fill in place, it is a different screen).
