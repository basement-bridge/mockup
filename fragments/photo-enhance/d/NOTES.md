# Photo enhance, Option D: split slider (mockup only, nothing built in Kitchie)

Owner request (typed, 14 Oct 2026): enhance recipe photos entirely in the browser, original kept unchanged in memory, conservative auto-enhancement in the preview, instant Original / Enhanced comparison "preferably with press-and-hold", stepped levels each rendered from the original, Canvas/WebGL/WASM/Worker as appropriate, encode and upload only on confirm, no server-side processing. Follow-up: "get it mocked up" first.

**What it is.** The Enhance step is one sheet. The photo is drawn twice in the same frame: Original on the left of a draggable divider, the chosen level on the right. One strength slider, four steps (Original, Light, Balanced, Full). Press and hold anywhere on the photo (or the Hold button) shows the whole original; let go and it returns. Uses the shared engine (`../engine/enhance.js`) on the real sample photos, plus "Choose from your photos" for your own (pure client side).

Open it: `index.html` (add `?open=banner|step|cook` to jump to the picker; the Mockup button lists the failure states). Check: `node tools/photo-enhance-d-check.mjs`.

## Where it sits in the flow
Pick (camera or photos) -> **Enhance sheet** -> shrink (banner 1600 px, others 960 px, plus 240 px square) -> the existing photo queue (tile states Waiting, Uploading n%, Photo is being prepared, Done) -> Undo for 5 minutes. Same sheet markup as the app (`.sheet`, `.btn`, `.mini`, `.pq`, `.pbar`, `.cbar`-style bar); new classes are `enh*`.

## Decisions (every one is a **Proposal** unless the owner said it)
- **PE-D-O1 Before shrink, no crop in this flow.** Enhance happens right after the pick, on the full decoded photo. Crop and rotate stays a later action on the stored copy (the app's own rule: no original kept), so it crops an already enhanced photo and never re-enhances. Alternative: Enhance after crop (more to look at, but works on the framed picture only). Wrong guess costs: a person who crops after sees the enhanced look cropped, which is what they want anyway.
- **PE-D-O2 On by default at Balanced.** The owner asked for enhancement "in the preview", so the first thing seen is the split with Balanced already applied (the divider sweeps from all-original to 50/50 when it is ready). Skip is one tap, Original is the first step. Wrong guess costs: someone who never wants it taps Skip every time; PE-D-O3 softens that.
- **PE-D-O3 The level is remembered on this phone** (localStorage, optional, falls back to Balanced), including Original. Someone who chose Original last time opens at Original. Wrong guess costs: a photo uploaded at the old strength without looking; the strength word is always on screen.
- **PE-D-O4 The control.** Divider (44 px grip, keys: arrows 5 percent, Home and End) plus one slider. Original / Enhanced is the split itself, so there is no extra toggle to learn. The right label names the level; the left says Original; each hides when the divider is near its edge. Hold: pointer down on the photo shows the whole original immediately; the Hold button does the same with Space or Enter held. **A quick tap (under 250 ms) on the Hold button pins the original on and a second tap turns it off**, for people who cannot hold. Screen readers: the slider and divider have `aria-valuetext`, a polite live line says "Showing the original" and "Balanced ready".
- **PE-D-O5 Touch handling.** `touch-action:none`, `user-select:none`, `-webkit-touch-callout:none`, pointer capture on the photo, context menu and selection start cancelled, so iOS gives no long-press callout. The photo is a canvas, not an image, so there is nothing to save-as by accident. `prefers-reduced-motion`: no sweep, no shimmer, no spinner.
- **PE-D-O6 The first view is the original.** The sheet opens with a quiet "Opening your photo" shimmer, shows the Original as soon as the engine has decoded it, then swaps the enhanced side in. A "Preparing" pill shows only when a level is not cached and takes more than 120 ms. The other levels are prefetched right after the first render, so moving the slider is instant.
- **PE-D-O7 Cost of a wrong guess.** Moving the slider to Original, or Skip, gives exactly today's flow with no engine encode. Use photo is the only call to `encode()`; the Mockup panel counts it ("Encodes so far"). A bad enhancement that is saved cannot be taken back (PE-D-O13).
- **PE-D-O8 What changed.** A collapsed row, "What changed", under the slider. Opened, it lists the engine's notes in plain words and says "Balanced applies each tweak at 70 percent of its full size, worked out from your original every time". The summary shows the count and the strength.
- **PE-D-O9 Already good.** When the engine's notes say "Looks good already", the sheet says so ("Balanced hardly changes it. Slide the divider to check, or Skip") and does not pretend.
- **PE-D-O10 Big, small and unreadable photos.** 12 MP and up: an "N MP" chip, preview at 1280, and on Use photo a progress bar with "A big picture takes a few seconds" (the encode has no progress events, so it is an indeterminate bar). Under 480 px on the long edge: the sheet says the photo is small, locks the strength, and the button becomes "Add it as it is". HEIC or unreadable: the existing toast "That photo could not be read." and no sheet. Engine unsupported or its file missing: silent, the photo goes straight to the queue as today.
- **PE-D-O11 Sizes.** The sheet header names the kind ("Banner · 1600 px + 240 px square", "Step photo", "Cook photo", "Photo" for the gallery). After Use photo it shows "Ready: 1600 px long edge · 212 KB" and "Square 240 px · 14 KB, cut from this same result, not enhanced twice": the square is cut from the decoded encoded blob, not from a second `encode()`.
- **PE-D-O12 Cancel and Skip.** Skip uploads the original as the app does today. Closing the sheet (scrim, Escape) cancels: nothing is encoded or sent. Open question: should the scrim tap behave like Skip? A picked photo that is not confirmed should not go up on its own, so Proposal is cancel.
- **PE-D-O13 After confirming (open question).** The stored photo is the enhanced one, and Recipe keeps no original (locked P3), so "remove enhancement" later is impossible. The tile says so ("Kitchie keeps no original, so after that it cannot be un-enhanced"), and the sheet says "Saved enhanced: Kitchie keeps no original" while a level other than Original is chosen. **Proposal:** the phone that added it holds the not-enhanced copy (already shrunk, WebP, about 200 KB) in memory for the 5 minutes of Undo and offers "Use original" next to Undo; Undo puts the previous stored photo back. Nothing server side. Cost of a wrong guess: if the owner wants "remove enhancement" any time later, that needs keeping originals on the server, which reverses P3 and costs storage (up to 8 MB a photo).
- **PE-D-O14 Cook photos.** "Add a photo of tonight's dish" opens the same sheet, "Cook photo · 960 px". The remembered strength means each of up to three photos is two taps (pick, Use photo). A photo from a cook never becomes the banner (locked P7).

## What loads (DESIGN.md section 6)
- **Default path** (the screen behind the sheet): `theme.css`, `recipe.css`, `d.css` (about 11 KB), `d.js` (about 17 KB, unminified), the sample thumbnails of the screen. No engine, no worker, no canvas work.
- **On first Add or Replace tap** (intent, while the picker is open): `../engine/enhance.js`, then `split.js` (about 18 KB, unminified). The picker also fetches `samples.json`, which is mockup only.
- **On first `open()`**: the engine starts its Worker (`enhance-worker.js`). Nothing decodes or renders until a photo is picked.
- **On Use photo only**: `encode()`, then one decode of the result to cut the square. Closing the sheet calls `session.close()`.
- In the app, `split.js` and `d.css` would be one deferred file next to `recipes-edit-photo.js`, fetched on the first photo action like that file is.

## Deliberately not done
No separate before/after toggle (the split is the comparison); no per-tweak sliders (one strength only); no crop in this step.
