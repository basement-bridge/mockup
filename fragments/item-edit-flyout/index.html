Item edit flyout

Editing an item used to open its own full page. Here it opens as a flyout nested under the item flyout you already have open, on phone, tablet and desktop. Mockup only: nothing is saved, nothing is built. Updated 11 October 2026 (round 3) with the owner's voice answers: the battery everywhere (94), the four bands with a minimum (84), no minimum (95), a hand-set level clears only on a restock (96), Category and Unit as chips plus free text (97, 98), the shopping amount aims at twice the minimum (99), the use-by quick set (92), and a hand-set level looks the same everywhere and never touches the shopping list (100, 101) are confirmed and drawn. Decisions are numbered 63 to 101 (Plan on desktop used 49 to 62). Anything not marked confirmed is a proposal.

2. Shopping list: two states

The item has a quantity

How much to buy is a number in the item's own unit.

With a minimum it aims at twice the minimum and starts at what tops you up to it (4 tin held, minimum 4: starts at 4, the note says 8 tin is the target; confirmed aim, delta drawn as a judgment call, 99). With no minimum it starts from what you have (the amount, else what it was before it ran out, else 1). The own-words box stays out of the way; a line says to clear the quantity to use it.

3. Location, Spot, Category: the Add form's controls

Read from Kitchie uat (read only: add-form.ts, assets/form.js) and the owner's two screenshots. They are text inputs with a list of suggestions that opens when you tap or focus, filters as you type, takes a new word, and closes with Esc. A spot belongs to a location: the list shows that location's spots, or every spot with its location named when no location is set, and choosing one fills the location in; changing the location clears the spot. Same in Edit and Add here.

Category as the chip row plus free text (confirmed, 91 and 97)

The input, with the Pantry filter bar's chips under it: tag icon, chips, the chosen one outlined, a chevron for the rest.

Chips are quick picks; type anything not in the list and press Enter (or leave the box) and it is kept, and offered as a chip from then on. No "All" chip; tap the chosen chip again to clear; typing filters the chips.

What loads, and when (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path: pantry.html, theme.css (two default themes), flyout.css, the size stylesheets (each is media-gated to its own width, so a browser applies only the matching ones and fetches the rest last, without blocking), flyout.js, and exactly one host script (phone.js below 1024px, desktop.js from it). No images, no fonts. Deferred: form.js on the first Edit or Add, emoji.js (the emoji list, about 7 KB) on the first time the emoji picker opens, history.js on the first History open, and the journal itself only on that open. The suggestion lists are built when a box is focused, from the household's own data; the category chips are drawn with the form. Nothing is preloaded. Why this is reasonable: most visits open the item and leave; the form is for the minority who edit, the picker for the few who change an emoji, and History is rarer still.
