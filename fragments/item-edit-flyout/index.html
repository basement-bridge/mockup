Item edit flyout

Editing an item used to open its own full page. Here it opens as a flyout nested under the item flyout you already have open, on phone, tablet and desktop. Mockup only: nothing is saved, nothing is built. Decisions are numbered 63 to 93 (Plan on desktop used 49 to 62). Anything not marked confirmed is a proposal.

1. Level: battery or drop (the real choice)

The owner asked for a battery icon with the same colour bands as the drop. His 8 October rule (level is a drop, no visible level word) makes that a real choice, so both are drawn on the same screens. Either way there is no level word on screen, and the level is always editable.

Battery (recommended)

Four cells: 4 full and green is Plenty, 2 amber is Some, 1 red is Running low, empty and black is Out.

  • Reads as "how much is left" at a glance, and as a row of four in the picker.
  • Same four colours as the drop, so nothing is learnt again.
  • Used here in the form and in the item flyout's level tile, so the two never disagree. The item sheet, the list rows and Plan still draw drops until the owner says to change them.
DesktopPhone

Drop (the 8 October rule)

Fuller or emptier, same colours.

  • Consistent with every screen already drawn and built.
  • Cost: a drop says liquid, so it is the odd one out for rice, tins and eggs.

3. Location, Spot, Category: the Add form's controls

Read from Kitchie uat (read only: add-form.ts, assets/form.js) and the owner's two screenshots. Decisions 63 to 93

Status in brackets. "Confirmed" is the owner's, 11 October 2026; "proposed" is the design agent's, 10 or 11 October 2026.

  1. 63.(proposed) Editing is a flyout nested under the open item flyout, never a page. The item flyout stays open, in front and live.
  2. 64.(proposed) One child slot: Edit and History share it. The pencil and E, and the clock and ,, toggle their child; opening one while the other is open swaps.
  3. 65.(proposed) Desktop from 1240: a 360px column beside the item flyout, sliding out from beneath it. From 1024: tucked under the item's header inside the lane. It never covers the list or the item. History's slot goes from 320 to 360.
  4. 66.(proposed) Phone and tablet: a second sheet (84% high) stacked over the half sheet; the item collapses to a strip. Tap the strip or the dim, drag a grab handle or the title bar down, or press Esc to step back one layer.
  5. 67.(proposed) Header actions in this order: pencil, turned-back clock, round x. The open child's icon is filled. The phone sheet keeps the no-x rule of 8 October.
  6. 68.(confirmed 11 Oct 2026, owner: shown on the icons) A key cap on each icon; the shortcut line reads "E edit" and ", history"; the cue switch hides all; desktop widths only. The reveal look is no longer the plan.
  7. 69.(proposed) Close is Ctrl or Cmd Enter on desktop, Esc on a phone: the top layer first. The "step back from a field to its tiles" part went with the tiles layout.
  8. 70.(proposed) A tile tap in the item flyout lands in the edit flyout on that field, highlighted.
  9. 71.(confirmed 11 Oct 2026, owner) Layout A: one scroll in five groups. The two tabs do not survive; the tiles layout (C) is dropped.
  10. 72.(superseded 11 Oct 2026 by 82 to 86) Was: level is a drop, never a word; counted level worked out and not editable here; weighed or worded four drops. Kept as history.
  11. 73.(proposed) Minimum stock sits under Quantity in Amount, shown only when the item has a unit or a minimum.
  12. 74.(proposed; restated and refined by the owner 11 Oct, see 87 to 89) Shopping list is one spaced, ruled-off group at the end, once. Used up and Add to shopping in the item flyout stay exactly as they are (owner).
  13. 75.(proposed) "Use in a recipe" is a plain action row at the bottom; its label and target belong to the Recipe manifest; hidden without Recipes.
  14. 76.(proposed) "Mark as used up" is not repeated in the form.
  15. 77.(proposed) Every change autosaves with a short "Saved". No Save button. Errors sit on the field with Try again. A name is needed; a use-by date cannot be in the past.
  16. 78.(proposed) Add item is the same form in add mode, in the item flyout's place, with its own footer. New items are Unplaced.
  17. 79.(proposed) History shows this item only, newest first by day: what changed, when, who, from where. The last 3 days that have entries, then 5 more. Edits made on this page appear under Today. Fetched on open.
  18. 80.(confirmed in substance 11 Oct 2026: the owner asked for this tile and picker for both Add and Edit; extended by 93) The emoji is a tile ("+" when none) with a picker and "No emoji". No photos.
  19. 81.(proposed) Viewport code is in its own files: phone.css, tablet.css, desktop.css, desktop-1240.css, and one host script (phone.js or desktop.js).
  20. 82.(proposed; owner-requested 11 Oct) Level is one picker of four icons for every item. The current level is always the picked one. The level is always editable by the person. A counted item's level is worked out (automatic) by default; a weighed or worded item's is picked. Supersedes 72.
  21. 83.(proposed; owner-requested 11 Oct; recommended) The level icon is a battery (four cells: Plenty 4 green, Some 2 amber, Running low 1 red, Out 0 black) in place of the drop, in this flyout's form and in the item flyout's level tile. The drop stays available as lv=drop. Other screens keep the drop until the owner says otherwise.
  22. 84.(proposed) Automatic rules: a counted item, or any item with a number and a minimum, has its level worked out (over twice the minimum Plenty, over the minimum Some, at or under it Running low, 0 Out; counted with no minimum Plenty until 0). A weighed item with a minimum follows the same rules (proposed reading of the owner's note). A worded item, or a weighed one with no minimum, keeps the label the person picked and has no automatic mode.
  23. 85.(proposed; the owner's rule, drawn) Override: when the person picks a level on an item that has automatic rules, the pick wins over the rules and shows everywhere. When the quantity is next revised (stepper, typed, unit changed, use one, used up, cleared) the pick is dropped and the rules take over again, until the next pick. Changing the minimum or any other field keeps the pick. Out is the amount at 0; adding stock afterwards hands the level back to the rules.
  24. 86.(proposed) A small muted cue under the picker says "Automatic" or "Set by you", only where rules apply, with a small back-to-automatic icon beside "Set by you". No chip, no sentence. A tooltip explains it.
  25. 87.(proposed; refines 74) The Shopping list group has a status line first: "Not on the shopping list." or "On the shopping list: 2 L." with Open the list, then how much to buy, then the button "Add to shopping list" or "Update the shopping list".
  26. 88.(proposed) When the item has a weighed or counted quantity, "How much to buy" is a number stepper in the item's own unit, starting from what the household has (the amount, else the amount before it ran out, else 1). The own-words box is not shown; a one-line note says to clear the quantity to use it.
  27. 89.(proposed) When the item has no weighed or counted quantity (nothing, or words), the own-words box shows. Clearing the quantity (a new Clear beside the Quantity label) brings it back.
  28. 90.(proposed; owner: match the Add form) Location and Spot are text inputs with a suggestion list that opens on focus, filters as you type, takes a new word, and closes with Esc without closing the sheet. The spot list is the location's spots, or every spot with its location named when there is no location; choosing one fills the location in; changing the location clears the spot. Replaces the suggestion pills.
  29. 91.(proposed assumption) Category is the same input with its suggestions drawn as the chip row of the Pantry filter bar (tag icon, chips, the chosen one outlined, a chevron for the rest; no "All"; typing filters; Enter adds a new category). cat=list draws the plain list instead.
  30. 92.(proposed) Use by keeps the date in words and Clear, and takes the Add form's quick choices: +3 days, +5 days, +1 week, Pick date…. The earlier Today, Tomorrow, 2 weeks and 1 month pills are dropped.
  31. 93.(proposed; extends 80) The emoji picker, in Edit and Add, has a search box, a Recent row (the last 8 picked on this device), a grid of about 126 foods and kitchen things, and "No emoji" once one is set. Esc closes the picker only. The list is fetched on the first open.

What loads, and when (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path: pantry.html, theme.css (two default themes), flyout.css, the size stylesheets (each is media-gated to its own width, so a browser applies only the matching ones and fetches the rest last, without blocking), flyout.js, and exactly one host script (phone.js below 1024px, desktop.js from it). No images, no fonts. Deferred: form.js on the first Edit or Add, emoji.js (the emoji list, about 7 KB) on the first time the emoji picker opens, history.js on the first History open, and the journal itself only on that open. The suggestion lists are built when a box is focused, from the household's own data; the category chips are drawn with the form. Nothing is preloaded. Why this is reasonable: most visits open the item and leave; the form is for the minority who edit, the picker for the few who change an emoji, and History is rarer still.
