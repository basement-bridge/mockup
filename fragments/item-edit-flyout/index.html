Item edit flyout

Editing an item used to open its own full page. Here it opens as a flyout nested under the item flyout you already have open, on phone, tablet and desktop. Mockup only: nothing is saved, nothing is built. Updated 12 October 2026 (round 4) after the owner reviewed the built flyout ("this is good"): the shopping list is gone from Edit (102); the item flyout shows "How much to buy" only at Running low or Out, a stepper for a weighed or counted quantity and the own-words box otherwise (103 to 105); and the item flyout's header has a tappable emoji tile that opens the same picker (106, 107). Decisions 102 to 107 are drawn below; where they read the owner's words, they are marked proposed. Round 3 (11 October 2026) had the owner's voice answers: the battery everywhere (94), the four bands with a minimum (84), no minimum (95), a hand-set level clears only on a restock (96), Category and Unit as chips plus free text (97, 98), the shopping amount aims at twice the minimum (99), the use-by quick set (92), and a hand-set level looks the same everywhere and never touches the shopping list (100, 101) are confirmed and drawn. Decisions are numbered 63 to 107 (Plan on desktop used 49 to 62). Anything not marked confirmed is a proposal. Updated 12 October 2026 (round 6, owner by voice): the emoji is a plain text box with the same suggestion strip as Location, Spot and Category, no picker or panel (116 to 119); a unit can always be cleared, and a typed unit ("kilos", "Kg", "boxes", "2kg") is matched to the household's own (120, 121).

3. Location, Spot, Category: one suggestion strip (owner, typed 12 Oct 2026; 108 to 110)

The owner's screenshots of the built Category field: typing "da" showed a dark strip with a tag icon, an outlined "da" chip, a "Dairy" suggestion and a chevron, but a box that already had a value looked busy. Now all three boxes share one strip under the box. A box that has a value shows nothing extra (Location "Pantry"). An empty box shows one row: the pin or tag icon, one or two suggested pills and the chevron for the rest. Typing filters the pills, highlights the match, and offers the typed text first as the outlined "add this" pill; tap a pill, or press Enter or the arrow keys, to take it; Esc closes the strip only. Spot follows the location (every spot with its location when none is set; picking a spot fills the location; changing the location clears the spot). Same in Edit and Add. This supersedes the dropdown list of 90 (kept in the decisions as history).

4. Emoji: a text box with the suggestion strip, in Edit, Add and the item flyout (owner, by voice 12 Oct 2026; 116 to 119)

The owner does not want a dropdown, popup, slide-in panel or modal for the emoji. The picker (search box, Recent, grid, "No emoji") is gone. The emoji is now a plain text box with the same suggestion strip as Location, Spot and Category under it (108 to 110): empty, one row with one or two pills (Recent, which starts from the household's own emoji) and the chevron for the rest; an emoji set, nothing extra until you type or clear; typing filters the 133 foods, drinks, source animals and kitchen things by name and keyword and highlights the best match. Any emoji typed or pasted from the device is always accepted: a box holding only an emoji keeps it at once (one emoji: the newest). Clearing the box saves "no emoji" when you leave it or press Enter, and the neutral "+" tile shows. The hint is the box's placeholder, "Search, or type or paste your own emoji", with the Kitchie size rule. Pills are 44px, the strip stays one row while typing, Esc closes the strip only, Shift+Enter is left to the Add form.

Units: clear, and say it your way (120, 121)

Clearing the Unit box is always allowed: the item then has no unit. A typed unit takes the household's own spelling: "kilos", "Kg", "kilogram" are kg; "boxes" is box; "ML" is mL; "litres" is L.

"2kg", "3 kilos" or "250ml" typed in the quantity box, the Unit box or the own-words box becomes the number and the unit when that is unambiguous; "2 x 400 g", "half a bag" and "1,500 g" stay as typed. The note under the Unit box says what Enter will keep. Carrots (1 bag), spinach (own words).

What loads, and when (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path: pantry.html, theme.css (two default themes), flyout.css, the size stylesheets (each is media-gated to its own width, so a browser applies only the matching ones and fetches the rest last, without blocking), flyout.js, and exactly one host script (phone.js below 1024px, desktop.js from it). No images, no fonts. Deferred: form.js on the first Edit or Add, emoji.js (the emoji list and the field and strip code, about 14 KB) when a form is drawn (its emoji box needs the list for its strip) or on the first tap of the item flyout's header tile (round 6, 116 to 119; the item flyout does not fetch the whole form for it), history.js on the first History open, and the journal itself only on that open. The suggestion lists are built when a box is focused, from the household's own data; the category chips are drawn with the form. Nothing is preloaded. Why this is reasonable: most visits open the item and leave; the form is for the minority who edit, the emoji list for those who edit or tap the tile, and History is rarer still.
