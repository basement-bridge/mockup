Person rowwarm

Owner, 25 Oct 2026 (Kitchie #1110; phone Menu only): the person row is “too boring” after the lean pass flattened it. “Lean doesn’t mean mean”: lean cuts words and noise, never warmth or identity. The name big and editable in place; the kitchen role a swipeable set under it; the assistants under the person, not a separate menu item; each assistant with its brand mark, a symbol for its state, a star for the default.

Play it. Tap the name to change it (Enter or tapping away saves, Escape puts it back; type !! to see the error). Swipe the role sideways: it snaps to one and applies when it settles (arrow keys work too). The unlink icon asks in place. Add an assistant shows the ones not connected, greyed. With two or more connected, the star marks the default. URL aids: ?o=A|B|C, ai=none|one|two|all, role=none, long=1, ts=3 (+30%), pic=1, ask=claude, add=1.

What the owner asked for

  • Name big, editable in place (tap, no pencil). Only the name field; the picture is untouched here (the round picture opens the profile page, where it is changed). Saves on Enter or leaving, Escape cancels, errors inline under the name.
  • Kitchen role under it: a horizontal rotary of the real roles (Head Chef, Supply Runner, Full Stack Cook, Knife Hand, Pantry Marshal, Sink General), like the theme rotary but each role fills the width (no neighbour peeks), swipe only, applies when it settles. A role someone typed themselves is shown first as their own.
  • Assistants under the person: the separate Assistants menu item is gone. Connected ones only, plus one Add an assistant control for the rest. State is a symbol: linked (in colour) for connected, a plain add icon (greyed) for not connected, a small unlink icon to disconnect with the confirm in place. A star marks the default (shown from two connected).
  • Character: a big round picture in the person’s own colour (their hue comes from their name), a warm household line, a soft wash of their colour behind the card. No new words.

The three options

A: card

The person in one warm card: picture left, name large, household under it, the role rotary inside the card. Assistants are a list card directly under. Reads left to right, scales to +30% and long names best. Recommended.

B: hero

Centred: a large picture, the name big under it. The warmest, and the tallest: at +30% the rows below the person fall further down the Menu.

C: open

No card around the person: a rounded-square picture, big type, an outlined role pill, hairline assistant rows. Calmest, least like a “profile”; the person is less separate from the plain rows.

Brand marks

Monograms, not the official logos. I could not confirm the logo usage rules of Anthropic, OpenAI or Google from here, so each assistant is a clean coloured monogram (Claude C, ChatGPT GPT, Gemini G) in its brand tint, drawn from one table so the official mark can replace it where the rules allow. Inline, no fetch (the app’s CSP is 'self'). Not connected ones are greyed.

Choices made where the owner was silent

All Proposals; a wrong guess costs a small change.

  • PR-1 The round picture is the way to the profile page (where the picture, Admin and Leave are). No chevron, no extra row.
  • PR-2 The star shows only from two connected; with one connected it is the default by itself, as today.
  • PR-3 A person with no role sees Pick a role first in the rotary; swiping to a real role saves it. A role cannot be cleared here.
  • PR-4 Brand marks are monograms (above).
  • PR-5 Assistants Kitchie only knows by name (not Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) are listed as connected with a neutral monogram and no unlink (Platform manages them).
  • PR-6 The person’s hue comes from their name (stable, no setting).
