Recipe search, four directions

Fragment. The owner wants a very intelligent search button for recipes. These are four ways for it to look when someone opens it. Each phone shows the first moment, then what comes back. Results carry what we know: what's in your pantry, what's missing, what's about to go off.

Example query: "something quick with eggs, no onion". Sample data only; the real search would read the pantry and the household's recipes.

Open
  • Which direction leads? They are not exclusive: 2's stock-first start could sit under 1's box.
  • When the search understood the request, should it say how (the chips in 1), or just show results?
  • Does it search only your saved recipes, or also suggest new ones from the AI? (This mockup shows saved only, plus one marked "Ask for new ideas".)
  • "Missing one thing": show those with a clear badge, or keep them out until nothing is missing?
  • Things about to go off: should they quietly rank results higher? Today's example does, and says so.
  • Tapping a result opens the recipe page (see recipe page fragment). Does it carry the search with it, for example the missing item pre-added to shopping?
  • Voice: is the mic on the box enough, or does the voice job start search by itself?
