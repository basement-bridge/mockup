Set someone up with their own household

Fragment. Owner, by voice, 12 October 2026. Mockup only: nothing is built, and until it is, current behaviour stays as it is.

This is a growth feature. It is a way to expand the product by getting new households set up, one invitation at a time. It is not a utility tweak to the invite screen: the entry is always at the foot of the left gutter, the inviter writes a personal note, and that note is what the new person sees first when they open the link and again while they sign up.

What it is

  • An entry at the bottom of the left gutter of the settings window (desktop). It makes a shareable link for someone who is not joining your household but getting a brand-new one of their own. The link lands them on sign-up and ends with them creating their household.
  • It opens as a pop-up modal over the whole window, so the person has all the attention while they work it out.
  • A personal message field. The message stays with the link and is visible to the invited person during sign-up (drawn: the link opens, after sign-in, no message).
  • Unmistakably not the join invite. The modal says "their own household", "not joining Our kitchen", shows two separate households, and has a card with the way back to the existing Invite someone in Household.

Frames

Desktop frames are best at 1280 wide. Mobile width: open the same addresses at 360 or 390.

  • The gutter entry (foot of the left gutter, above History): desktop-profile ?open=me
  • Modal, empty: ?open=invite&inv=empty
  • Modal, filled: inv=filled
  • Modal, link created: inv=created
  • Modal, copied: inv=copied
  • The way back, where it lands: ?open=household (press the way-back card in a modal frame to see it arrive and point at Invite someone)
  • The existing join invite, not redrawn: the household flow (Menu, Household, Invite someone)
  • Recipient, the link opens: start.html?s=landing · after sign-in: s=household · no message: &msg=none

What already exists (so it is not drawn twice)

A thinner version is already in uat and in the phone flow: Me > Other households > "Invite someone to a different household" (a single-use link, 48 hours, and an "own" landing; no personal message, no gutter entry). This mockup does not change that behaviour; in the window, the Me row now opens the new modal so the window has one place for it. Details, and what is judgement versus decided, in docs/knowledge/invite-new-household.md.

Open questions (none decided)

  • Expiry and reuse: 48 hours, once is drawn (uat links); the code sheet says 24 hours. One person or many?
  • The message: length (280 drawn), required or optional (optional drawn), editable after the link exists, plain text only.
  • What the inviter sees afterwards (nothing is drawn).
  • Abuse and rate limits: a stranger gets words from a member on our page; caps, a report route, how long the message is kept.
  • Whether the inviter has any relationship to the new household (uat: none).
  • Link or six-digit code, by sign-in mode; one entry or two (Me row and gutter); who sees it; the reverse wrong-place route. All in the notes file as INH-O1 to INH-O13.

Loads

The entry is one button in the gutter. The modal's script and styles are fetched only when it is pressed. The recipient page is one small page with theme tokens, no images, no libraries and no storage. Sample only: the link is not a real address, and nothing is copied, stored or sent.
