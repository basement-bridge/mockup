Plan on desktop: four options

The desktop Plan page today is the phone layout stretched into a 60% column, with a big panel pinned bottom-right that covers the week it is about. These four options use the width instead. Same cards, same pills, same decisions as the phone Plan (Planning the week, AI-led); only the layout and the pointer and keyboard behaviour change. Mockup only: nothing is saved, nothing opens. Open any option, then press the backtick key at the top edge to pick a window size.

Recommendation

Option A, the week board, as the default. A cook planning a week needs all seven days on screen at once, and wants to know "can I cook this?" without opening it. A gives that: seven columns, drag between them, with an inspector that is always docked on the right from 1440 and never covers the board. When nothing is picked the inspector is the week summary (what to buy by when, use-by pressure, cook order), so the right side is never empty and nothing shifts when a meal is picked. Under 1280 it becomes an agenda, which is Option B's layout, so B is not lost, it is A's narrow form.

Take from the others: D's "Use first / To buy" grouping is already the summary's job; D itself is worth building later as a second view of the same week (a "Cook sheet" switch next to the week title), because it answers a different question (what do I prep and buy, by day). C's Mise en place block fits the summary lane as a per-day section. Picking C alone is the safe, small build but it shows one day at a time, which is the phone problem again.

Fallback if the build must stay small: B. One layout at every width, no collapse rule, the Pantry master-detail pattern the app already has.

The options

A. Week board with a docked inspector

Seven days side by side, drag to move, and a side inspector that pushes the board instead of covering it.

  • 1280 and up: seven columns. 1440 and up: inspector always docked (week summary when idle). 1920: cards show the missing or on-hand line. 2200 and up: the board stops at 1760 and the summary gets its own lane.
  • 1024 to 1279: collapses to the agenda; the inspector appears when a meal is picked and pushes.

Decisions I made, to confirm or overturn

Numbers continue the Plan list (last owner decision was 48). All are proposals until the owner says yes. Recorded in plan-desktop.md.

  1. Desktop Plan is its own layout, not the phone column stretched. Recommended: Option A.
  2. The inspector never covers the week it is about. It docks and pushes. The one exception is Option D below 1440, where it overlays and the sheet scrolls to keep the picked day visible.
  3. Inspector widths are 340, 380, 400, 440, matching Pantry's 400 at 1440.
  4. With nothing picked the inspector shows the week summary: to buy (by day), use-by pressure, cook order. It is never empty.
  5. Close key is Ctrl or Cmd Enter, and Esc only cancels or closes menus, as in Pantry. The inspector is also resizable (320 to 560) and dismissible, and a picked meal is in the URL (?sel=).
  6. No Move key. Decision 6 stands: moving is dragging. Desktop adds a keyboard way to drag (Space to lift, arrows, Space to drop). The brief's "M to move" is not used because M is already "focus the main column" in Pantry.
  7. Replan is X, remove and not cooking is Shift X, Hold is H. They carry decisions 13, 25 and 38 to the keyboard.
  8. Breakpoints: board from 1280, agenda below, always-docked inspector from 1440, board capped at 1760, left-anchored (free space on the right).
  9. Week step by trackpad: two-finger sideways on the header or board steps one week; the title opens a picker listing last week to four weeks ahead with meal counts (decision 44 window).
  10. Sync indicator: Live, Syncing, Offline, Updated by your assistant, in the header. Offline pauses edits and says so; it does not queue them.
  11. Undo toast for every removal, replan, move, copy; no confirm dialogs.
  12. Use-by is a drag hint: dropping a meal later than an on-hand ingredient's use-by day says so on the target; it never blocks.
  13. Hover buttons are Hold, Replan, More, and they are also reachable by focus, so keyboard and touch-screen laptops get them.
  14. Day-state looks (decisions 34 to 36) and the stable week title (46) carry over unchanged. Decision 47 (week remembered) and decision 48 (people strip) are not drawn on desktop beyond reuse of the strip.
