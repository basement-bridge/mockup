Shopping receipt: no double-add, quantity reconciliation

Kitchie issue #797, 16 October 2026. Mockup first; the build for gaps 1 to 4 follows once the owner picks. Decisions SR-1 to SR-27 are in the job note; the numbered ones below are the same. Proposal marks every choice the owner was silent on.

Owner's words (voice, 16 October 2026). While a trip is still open (the whole list not yet confirmed), a person must be able both to tick rows by hand and to scan a receipt. (1) Nothing already accounted for on this trip, that is only ticked, swiped (Bought) or finished with Done shopping, may be added to the Pantry a second time when it shows on the receipt. Such rows read Already in your Pantry, are not added again by default, and offer: same item (use the receipt amount to fix a blind swipe amount, 1 to 1.204 kg), keep the Pantry's, or another one, add again. (2) Quantity as in the Pantry receipt-add: the receipt's real quantity is shown, can fill a blank list amount or correct a different one, with a unit pill; a bare number against an exact-unit item such as kg must ask for the unit; never silently override: the person sees and confirms the number. Repeated receipt lines for one list item are combined (2 x 500 g = 1000 g). Gaps 5 and 6 (parser, shopper link) are out of scope.

Three options

A: Receipt order, receipt wins

  • One flat list in print order.
  • The stepper starts on the receipt number, with its unit pill. A List said 1 kg link switches back.
  • Every changed amount carries a flag (Changed from 1 kg).
  • Fewest taps; the risk is a wrong read going through because it was already filled in. Proposed as the lead (Proposal).

B: List order, both on show

  • Rows in the order of your list. Two chips on each: List and Receipt.
  • Where they differ, nothing is preselected: the person must choose before Add.
  • Safest for amounts; most taps.

C: Only what needs you

  • Conflicts, blanks and missing units on top.
  • Agreed rows and Already-in-your-Pantry rows fold away, with a count and what will happen to them.
  • Off-list lines (the salmon) sit in a third fold, "1 not on your list, added", each with Leave out when open.
  • Rows use A's stepper. Shortest screen; hides rows the person may want to check.

Same in all three

  • Already-in-your-Pantry rows: three buttons, Keep Pantry's is on. Same item shows only when the amounts differ.
  • A typeable box (decimal keyboard) in the stepper on every amount.
  • One Add bar: Adds 4, fixes 1, 3 not added again. Disabled with 1 to answer until every unit and choice is made.
  • Sample trip: milk (ticked, matches), eggs (blank list), rice (ticked, 1 to 2 kg), chicken (bare 2 against kg), yoghurt (2 lines), plain flour (500 g and 1 kg lines = 1.5 kg), bananas (swiped 1, receipt 1.204 kg), butter (swiped, matches), tuna (Done shopping, 2 vs 3 tin), salmon (not on list).

Open questions

  • Does a tick count as "already accounted for"? The owner's list named ticked, swiped and finished. Drawn: swiped and finished are "Already in your Pantry", a tick is not (SR-7). Wrong guess costs: if ticked should also show that label, the copy changes, but nothing is in the Pantry for a tick, so the tick row would still have to add once.
  • Same item: replace or top up? Drawn as replace (SR-8). A wrong guess double-counts or under-counts the Pantry.
  • Which option leads (A, B or C)? A is fewest taps but a prefilled wrong number can slip through; B forces a choice on every difference.
  • Unit relationships as a first-class concept (owner): a later unit switch (g to kg) must convert quantity and minimum, not relabel. Separate Kitchie issue, number to follow. This mockup only combines lines through the kg/g and L/mL relationships (SR-21).
  • Lines in unrelated units (tin and kg) are not combined (SR-26); is that right, or should the person be asked?
  • Non-food lines (bag fee, discount) go to "Not added" with their reason as before; not drawn in this sample.
  • Which answers can the scan see? A swipe and Done shopping are server-side; a tick on another device is invisible. Build question, guarded lane if the receipt commit changes.
  • Receipt line with no number at all and a blank list (gap 2): not drawn.

What loads (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path for an option page: theme.css, sr.css (about 6 KB), one of phone.css or desktop.css by media query (under 1 KB), sr-data.js, sr-core.js (about 14 KB), theme.js and the size control frame.js. No images, no fonts beyond the theme, no network call. Nothing is fetched on engagement: every tap re-draws in place (section 6a), so there is no refresh. Deferred: the other themes load only when picked. Option C's folds are drawn only when opened. This notes page loads its iframes lazily. In the build the review would load only when a scan is opened from Shopping (not on page load).

Check: tools/shopping-receipt-check.mjs (console errors, sideways scroll, 44px targets at 390, 768 and 1280, light and dark, plus a click-through of the no-double-add rule).
