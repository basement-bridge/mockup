Meal plan: start cooking from a planned meal

Phone first. The open meal detail on the Plan tab (the day card docked at the top, the detail sheet below), drawn with Kitchie's own markup and its plan.phone.css copied unchanged from uat 0.122.2. Four options; each phone below opens on tonight's made-up meal, the case that was reported. Tap Start cooking (or the … menu) to see where it goes. Mockup only, nothing is saved or sent. The owner has not chosen.

Original problem statement (owner, by voice, 15 October 2026, while cooking dinner)

“When I open up that particular day's plan, which is the detail we open, there's no place for me to start cooking, as a button.” Raised as a bug. Tonight's plan held Malatang with chicken and rice, a meal made up with the AI (no saved recipe, seven ingredients, nothing missing). Cooking was started separately through the assistant (start_cooking), which put the Cooking now banner on Home.

What the app does today (read from the code, Kitchie uat 0.122.2)
  • The detail's footer is Hold and Add only (Plan UI review decision 126, owner-stated). There is no cook action in the detail itself.
  • Make it exists only in the meal's … menu, and only when the household holds Recipe (cook mode on), the meal is a saved recipe, nothing is missing, it is not someone else's plan and not a past day (cookable() in plan.phone.js). It opens the Recipe tab's cook mode.
  • A meal made up with the AI has no recipe id, so it never gets a cook action anywhere. The household's Cooking now view (ingredients first, the banner on every tab) can only be started by the AI (start_cooking, Kitchie #307); the app itself can end it, not start it.
  • Desktop Plan has no cook action at all.

Proposals (the owner was silent)
  • SC-P1. Where: in the detail's footer row, after Hold (and Add). The footer is where the detail's actions already live and where a thumb is. Kept as well: Make it in the … menu (unchanged). Wrong guess costs: the footer gets crowded; the alternative is a small pan button on the docked day card.
  • SC-P2. Label and weight: with two buttons (nothing to add) it reads Start cooking (the owner's words) and is the filled, leading button; with three (something to add) it reads Cook and is an outline button, so Add keeps the accent. Checked at 360 and 390.
  • SC-P3. Icon: a small pan (new; the app has none). C adds the spark after the label, so it is visible that the AI app opens.
  • SC-P4. When it shows: on today's and future meals in your own or the household's plan; not on a past day, not in another person's view-only plan, not on Leftovers. Something missing does not hide it (unlike today's Make it rule): cooking with a gap is normal, and cook mode or the Cooking now view lists what is there. This differs from the “sorted” rule of Kitchie #563 (XR1), so it is an open question below.
  • SC-P5 (B only). What Cooking now carries: the meal's title and its ingredients with the planned amounts (scaled to the people count), by: user, source app, and a source line “From your meal plan, tonight. Made up in your chat.”. A second tap while it is already cooking opens the same session instead of starting another. The 45-minute clear, dismiss and Done work as today.
  • SC-P6 (C only). The prompt: “I'm cooking <meal> now, from my Kitchie meal plan for tonight. Start cooking it in Kitchie so I can see the ingredients.” through the existing assistant link (Plan decisions 42 to 45).
  • SC-P7. Lead form factor: phone, because the report came from the stove and the detail he described is the phone's docked stack. Desktop Plan has no cook action at all; it follows once the owner has chosen here (a carry-over audit, guardrails “Which form factor a mockup leads with”).
Open questions
  • Which option, A, B, C or D? (Recommended B.)
  • B reverses Kitchie #307's locked design that a Cooking now session is declared by the AI. Is a person starting it from the plan acceptable?
  • Decision 126 says the detail's footer is Hold and Add. May a third action join it (SC-P1)?
  • Does a missing ingredient hide the cook action (today's rule, #563 XR1) or not (SC-P4)?
Loads (DESIGN.md section 6)

Nothing new on the Plan page's load: the button is part of the detail markup that plan.phone.js already draws when a meal is opened, and the rules are a few lines added to plan.phone.css (here cook.css, about 1 KB). B adds one POST on the tap and then a normal navigation to the existing Cooking now view (/cooking?session=); C fetches assistant-link.js on the tap, as Ask AI does today. In this mockup app.css is Kitchie's plan.phone.css copied unchanged and cooking-view.css is the view's CSS from web-cooking.ts, so the build lifts only cook.css and the footer markup in phone.js.
