Kitchen role

Mockup only. Nothing built, nothing approved. Owner (Arjan), voice, 1 November 2026: the role screen “feels counterintuitive compared with everything else on the site”, and it greets him on every sign-in. This page draws today’s screen, then three options for phone and desktop. Every choice the owner did not state is tagged Proposal.

Why it keeps coming back (read from the code)

The welcome counts as “first” per browser, through a kitchie_welcomed cookie, not per member. Later deliberately saves nothing and sets nothing, so the welcome returns at the next sign-in; a new browser or cleared cookies bring it back too. The fix is outside this mockup (a role or a seen flag on the member, and Skip counts as seen). Every option below therefore makes Skip final.

0 · Today on UAT

Drawn from the markup and styles in skin.ts and welcome.js, not from a screenshot (UAT sits behind sign-in). The kitchen scene is simplified.

  1. Wrong job first. A new person came to get in; the biggest thing on screen is a role quiz.
  2. The main button is always “Take me to the kitchen”, picked a role or not. Nothing says the role is optional until it is too late.
  3. Six taglines of ten-plus words each, cut to two lines, hidden altogether on short phones.
  4. The layout jumps after the first tap (hero hides, editor opens).
  5. Later returns. The quiet link is the one that brings the screen back every time.
  6. Different from the Menu. The Menu person row picks a role by swiping a rotary; this screen uses a tile grid.

D · A's tiles in C's Home card Chosen direction

Owner, 4 Nov: A for the look, C for no friction. No role step at sign-in. Home carries one card: six named tiles in two rows, one tap saves (Undo for a moment). A third row is pinned: Make your own. If the presets ever grow past two rows, the extras go in that third row as a sideways loop and Make your own stays put. The cross dismisses for good; the toast says where to find it, built from the app’s own menu data, never typed in.

A · Grid and preview

Six compact tiles (icon and name only). The tagline lives in one preview card under the grid, with a pencil to make it yours. Save stays off until you pick; Skip is always there.

B · Swipe (same as the Menu) Recommended

One big role at a time, swiped like the role rotary already decided for the Menu person row (snaps to one, no peeking neighbours, one-time wiggle on arrival). Desktop has arrows, arrow keys and a strip of icons.

C · No role step; one card on Home

New sign-ups go straight in. Home shows one quiet card with the six role icons; one tap sets it (with Undo), the cross dismisses it for good. The Menu person row stays the place to change it.

Recommendation (Proposal)

B. One pattern for picking a role everywhere, a single decision per screen, and the Menu rotary is reused instead of a second idea. C is the cheapest and also fine if you would rather a new person never meets a role screen at all.

Proposals the mockup made

  1. PR-1 Title “Your role” on the phone (3-word budget), “Your kitchen role” on desktop. No helper text on the phone.
  2. PR-2 The three perks and the kitchen scene leave this screen (they belong to the welcome, not the role).
  3. PR-3 Skip and a saved role both count as seen for good, per member, not per browser.
  4. PR-4 A role cannot be cleared here (as on the Menu person row).
  5. PR-5 An icon outside the drawn set shows the sparkle (default-look principle).
  6. PR-6 Tagline and name stay editable through the pencil, with the existing 32 and 80 character limits.
