Plan it: a Hard avoid guards a hand-planned meal

Mockup only, nothing is saved or sent. When a person plans a meal by hand, a Hard avoid stops it only if that person is part of the meal. Shopping is not stopped. The guard sits inside the Plan it panel, in place, in the Plan page's own look (the lane on a desktop, a bottom sheet on a phone). Decisions SW-74 to SW-82 and questions SW-O46 to SW-O56 in docs/knowledge/settings-window.md (round 10).

Owner, 12 October 2026 (typed), answering SW-O30

“yes only meals if the person with the hard block is part of the meal (they themselves or the household itself) … if household, warn and ask the recipe to exclude the person with the hard block”

What it does
  • One rule, from the owner. A Hard avoid blocks a hand-planned meal only when the person is part of that meal: a meal for them alone, or a household meal (which includes them). A meal for only other people is not touched. The shopping list is not blocked by Hard at all. Soft never blocks.
  • Where. In the Plan it panel (the day picker the Recipe tab already opens, R-D28), under the choice that causes it, in place (SW-22): no page, no extra dialog. It checks live, as soon as the recipe, day and “Who it is for” make a conflict, and says so to a screen reader (an alert).
  • Single person: a hard stop. “Not for Jane”, the recipe has mushrooms in it (the matched word is marked in the ingredient line), she has a hard avoid on mushrooms, her Reason in her words. Hard means avoided completely, so there is no override and none is drawn (SW-O47).
  • Household: warn and offer to leave her out. Names everyone affected, the avoid, their Reason. The one action reads Leave Jane out of this meal (two: Leave Jane and Kai out of this meal) and says under it what it does: “Plans Saturday for Sam only and marks it not for Jane. Quantities are worked out for Sam.” Pressing it plans the meal in one step. There is no Plan anyway.
  • After. The card says who it is for and carries “Not for Jane”; the lane (desktop) lists who it is for and who it is not for with the avoid, not her Reason; Undo returns to the guard. Choosing Everyone again asks again: she is never quietly put back.
  • Everyone left in the meal has a Hard avoid (code path, not a state): a hard stop, “Not for anyone in this meal”, no leave out.
Proposals (the owner was silent)
  • A “Who it is for” row in the panel: Everyone, then each member, one chosen, in place of the old People counter. A Plan entry in the app is for everyone or for one member already (for_whom), and the Plan page's Mine, Everyone and per-member views (decision 48) read the same way. The people count is shown under it and follows the choice.
  • Leave out plans the meal at once (one press), with Undo, rather than only changing the selection and asking for a second press. Wrong guess: someone expected to review first; the toast and Undo are the safety.
  • “Leave out” means: the plan entry is for the others and is marked not for them; the Recipe is not changed. The owner said “ask the recipe to exclude”; uat says applying a rule never creates a Recipe variation, and Recipe has no such call (SW-O46).
  • Reason shown in the guard, avoid only afterwards. The warning shows the Reason (the owner asked for it on the single stop); the planned meal shows only “hard avoid on mushrooms” (SW-O48).
  • Soft: one quiet line in muted text with an info icon, no danger colour, no action. Wrong guess: noise; drop it (SW-O53).
  • Sizes follow the Plan page: from 1024 the rail, a left-anchored 736 column and a 400 lane that never covers the week; below 1024 a bottom sheet over the agenda (phone and tablet), toast along the bottom.
Open questions

SW-O46 to SW-O56 are written in full in docs/knowledge/settings-window.md (Open questions). The ones that matter most: how “leave out” is stored and whether it changes the Recipe (46); whether an override is ever needed (47); whether another member's name and Reason may be shown to the person planning, because uat's own check says only “another member's” (48); whether the AI planner should also leave people out instead of refusing the whole meal (49); the add paths this panel does not cover: copy last week, drag, editing who a meal is for, and a Hard avoid added after the meal is planned (50, 51).

Loads (DESIGN.md section 6)

Nothing is loaded by any existing page: this is a new fragment. Default path of frame.html: theme.css, plan.css (shared with Plan on desktop, no size rules), guard.css (about 5 KB, phone and tablet baseline), guard-wide.css (under 1 KB, fetched only from 1024 by media query), guard.js (about 19 KB, sample data and the check), the size picker script frame.js from the Plan fragment. No images, no fonts added, nothing deferred because nothing is optional: the guard is the screen. In the build the check is a call the server already makes for the assistant (checkFoodRules), so the panel adds one request when a conflict is possible, not a bundle. Check: node tools/hard-avoid-plan-check.mjs.
