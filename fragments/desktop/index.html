Pantry on desktop: keyboard and mouse (open Pantry, press ?)

What the mockup does. Shortcuts follow what Gmail, GitHub, Linear and Slack already teach: ? lists them, Esc backs out, / goes to search, J K or the arrows move, G then a letter goes to a screen, U D S act on the open item, Z undoes. They never fire while typing in a box or with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held, so the browser's own shortcuts are untouched. The list always follows the keys: moving selects, and the panel follows.

Selected row. The open row has a lighter fill, an outline, an accent bar on the left and an accent name, so it is visibly different from hover.

Edit. Tapping any tile (or Enter, E) opens every field below the tiles in the same panel, focused on the field tapped. It saves as you go, Esc closes it. Used up and Add to shopping list are no longer buttons in the panel.

Search is always on the page, dimmed, to the right of Filters and Add; click or press / and it brightens and widens.

Location pills. Drag the bar under them, or double-click the top right corner, to show more rows; when shorter than the content they scroll. Ctrl or Cmd click picks several. After 5 seconds, or when you start using the list, the picked pills slide to the left and the rest follow, wrapping below.

Use one, Used up, Add to shopping list: options. A (drawn, Proposal): three small buttons that appear on the hovered, focused or open row, plus U, D, S, with an Undo toast; nothing asks first. B: the same three in a toolbar at the top of the panel. C: a right-click menu on a row (also the context-menu key). D: tick several rows with X and act on all (the phone already has a selection bar). A is the fastest for one item; D is the only one that helps with ten. Open question: for weighed items "use one" has no meaning; the mockup opens Quantity instead. Is that right, or should it use a stored portion?

Notes

How the lanes sit (owner, 9 October 2026, after seeing the built version on a wide window: "too much gap between the centre lane and the right lane; there should be a defined amount of space between the two, and the left should be oriented to that rather than latching to the right gutter"). The rail, the column and the panel are one group anchored on the left. The panel starts where the column ends, so the space between the list and the panel is always one 48px gutter on any window, and free space collects to the right of the panel. Below about 1224px the column is the lane that gives up width, so at 1024 the panel never covers it.

Replaces DESIGN.md section 7 for desktop. Section 7 said desktop is one centred column; it now describes this layout (updated with this change). Phone and tablet are untouched. The household flow's phone frame is not used here.

Proposals (owner silent): rail 88px; column 640px plus a 48px gutter each side (736px lane); panel 400px; same Pantry data and the same four tiles as the phone item sheet; Esc closes; click or Enter on a row opens it; the first item is not auto-opened; on very wide windows the group stays left-anchored beside the rail rather than being centred as a whole.

Open questions. On very wide windows, keep the group left-anchored, centre it, or grow a left inset (the earlier "14vw" idea)? Do swipe gestures give way to hover buttons on rows (drawn here as option A)? What happens at tablet widths (not drawn)? What should the Home panel hold once Cooking ideas has real recipe data?
