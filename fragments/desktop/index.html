Desktop: locked decisions

Desktop is locked in (owner, chat, 9 October 2026): the options that were open are settled and the rejected ones are removed. It is a mode of the household flow: open the flow, then move to the top edge of the window (or press the backtick key) to pick Phone, Tablet or a desktop size. The control is hidden so the screen is shown in full. Nothing here is built in Kitchie until its issue (#402 to #409) is approved.

Locked
  1. Layout. Left rail, an off-centre column and an always-open side panel. The rail, column and panel are one group anchored on the left with a defined 48px gap between the list and the panel on any window. The top bar and drawer option is dropped.
  2. Sizes. Phone under 600px and Tablet 600 to 1023px are the household flow (tablet is the phone app in a centred frame). Desktop is 1024px and up: 1024 × 768, 1280 × 800, 1440 × 900, 1920 × 1080, 2560 × 1440. Desktop shows Home and Pantry; other screens are not drawn yet.
  3. Device control. Hidden. Move to the top edge or press the backtick key; it lists the sizes, the shortcuts, the prototype controls and the theme. Nothing else is on the page.
  4. Header. No "Pantry" title: the rail says where you are. The count, Filters and Add sit on the left and Search on the right. Search is dimmed until clicked or /; then it widens and Add steps aside.
  5. Open item. Clicking a row selects it (lighter fill, outline, accent bar). Clicking any tile expands every field below it in the panel, saved as you go; Used up and Add to shopping list are not buttons there. History opens as a column on the right for that item only: hidden until asked for (nothing loads before), the last 3 days that have entries, then 5 more days at a time; long text is cut short with the full text in a tooltip.
  6. Row actions. Hover or focus buttons on the row plus U use one, D used up, S shopping list, Z undo; nothing asks first. Use one exists only for counted items; weights, volumes and free text have no Use one and change by level. Level is worked out for counted items and set by hand for the rest.
  7. Location and Category. The pills switch between Location and Category with the pin and tag button, as in Kitchie. A plain click picks one, Ctrl or Cmd click adds more. The strip can be dragged taller, scrolls, and after 5 seconds picked pills move left.
  8. Filters. A live side panel in the right lane (not a window). Three status rows (Running low, Expiring soon, Recently added) with check circles, a window line, a ‹ value › pill (Shift < and Shift >), keys 1 to 3; Location and Category pills with icons, ticks and counts, folded to one row under a drag handle (double-click to open); a Sort row that pushes a Sort panel out to the right (a tick on each panel; the Sort tick closes Sort, the Filters tick closes both); plain click picks one, Ctrl or Cmd click adds. The tick or Ctrl/Cmd Esc closes and keeps the picks; the item panel returns. Clear and Use last filters at the bottom.
  9. Filters in use. Once filters are chosen, the pill strip is replaced by tiles for each filter, each with an x to remove it, and Clear all.
  10. Shortcuts. ? or H lists them. A switch in that list shows or hides the shortcut cues on buttons and hints.
  11. Profile. A two-pane, settings-style window that blocks the page, opened from the avatar.
