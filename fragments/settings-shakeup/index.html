Play it. Everything on the phone works. Swipe the theme sideways; tap a segment of the text stepper (double tap resets); long hold a pinned control, then drag it; tap yourself under Household to edit your name; search from any page. The whole page changes with the theme and the text size.

Owner, 24 Oct 2026, final decisions (Kitchie #1110, #1090; phone only). Base is shake-up option B. Search results are layout C. Theme is a rotary. Text size is a seven-segment stepper. Pinned controls adapt to how many there are. Double tap resets, long hold moves, everywhere. Decisions SR-Q42 to SR-Q51 and proposals SR-P28 to SR-P37 are recorded in docs/knowledge/jobs-to-be-done/settings-redesign.md; four build agents are implementing them in Kitchie uat and compare to this page.

What is decided

  • The front (B). A big search; a quick area; three plain rows with their values: Household, Preferences, Kitchen setup (Assistants live inside it). A small Looks row sits above the quick area and opens the Looks page.
  • Pinnable. Text size, Theme (with light or dark alongside) and the activity bell. Invite and Assistants are not pinnable. Pin is an icon, never a word, and lives on the Looks page. There is no Emoji setting.
  • Quick area, adaptive. 1 pinned: a full row. 2: they share a row. 3 or 4: compact chips that start small and open in place on touch, the others dim without the row jumping. Default order: Text size, Theme, bell. Move one by long hold, then drag. There is no edit button and no pencil.
  • Theme. An in-place rotary: swipe sideways through the ten themes, each drawn in its real colours with its name. The whole page re-themes live as you turn it. Light or dark sits compactly alongside.
  • Text size. Seven battery-like segments, the middle one Match my phone: three smaller (−8, −16, −24%), three larger (+10, +20, +30%). On the right (thumb) half, the label on the left. The whole page rescales live; headings grow at half the rate of list text, with a cap. In the quick area with three or more pinned it is a small Aa chip that opens in place.
  • Search results (C). A best-match card (name, a quiet line for where it lives, one plain line, then the control or Go there), then compact one-line rows. Gone: browse-by-area groups, the results count, why-it-matched labels, every pill, the AFFECTS and LIVES IN labels. Search opens from every page and this page’s matches come first.
  • Empty search. The last three searches, newest first, or example searches when there is no history: plain text with a thin dotted underline, no pill (the history-search suggestion style).
  • You. Your own name and profile live under Household: tap yourself. There is no You row on the front; searching “my name” or “profile” lands there.

Gestures are app-wide rules

Double tap resets a control to its default (text size to Match my phone, theme to the default for your phone, the bell to On). Long hold moves a thing: hold, the thing lifts, drag. Use these two everywhere in Kitchie and Recipe, so a person learns them once. Neither gesture is the only way: every control also works with a single tap or a key. Written down in DESIGN.md (“Gestures”).

Choices the mockup made where the owner was silent

All are Proposals (SR-P38 and on in the note); a wrong guess costs a small layout change.

  • The Looks page is reached from a small Looks row above the quick area; it shows the current theme and text size as its value.
  • Double tap means two taps within 340 ms and 24 px on the same control; long hold means 480 ms still.
  • A control moves while 2 or more are pinned; with one there is nothing to reorder.
  • Choosing a theme sets Light or dark to what that theme is (Marmalade shows Light). The sun and moon pick Kitchie Day or Night, the system icon returns to your phone’s setting.
  • A search counts as “last searched” when you open a result, press Enter, or use a control inside the results.
  • The plain line on a best match is written once per setting (a short list in the mockup; generated from the setting definitions in the build).

Loads

Default path: theme.css, theme.js, shared.css, shake.css, shake.js; the sub pages are drawn from the script and need nothing more. Deferred, on engagement: shake-search.js (the engine) and ../settings-redesign/search-index.js (about 7 KB gzipped), fetched on first focus of the search box, a typed letter, a preset, or when a search result opens a page. Theme files other than Kitchie Night and Day load when a theme is turned to (and are warmed the first time you touch the rotary). Everything changes in place; no page loads when a control, a page or a result is used. Check: tools/settings-shakeup-check.mjs.
