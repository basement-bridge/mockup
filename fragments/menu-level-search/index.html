Menusearch first

Owner, 24 Oct 2026 (Kitchie #1110, #1090; phone only): “everything other than Install, History and Download my data is basically settings, so why haven’t you elevated settings search to the menu level instead of hiding it under the Settings entry? And there’s a lot of duplication of the same thing.”

Play it. Search is the first thing under the bar. Type “bigger text” or “install”. The text size stepper and the theme rotary work in place. The Settings entry is gone: the rows that were behind it are the menu.

What changed

  • Search is the menu’s first item: the same search as Settings search (best match, rows, last three searches). The Settings entry that hid it is gone.
  • The quick controls come with it: text size, theme, bell, as pinned. Pins, rotary and stepper work as before.
  • The settings rows sit directly under, once each: your name (the profile), Household, Preferences, Assistants.
  • Install, History and Download my data stay their own items, in a second block. Sign out last.
  • Gone from the menu because they were repeats: Settings, Looks, Kitchen setup with Categories and Locations, the Preferences group, People, Me, the Get started strip. See the tables below.
  • One search box. The old Settings front’s own search is not drawn for a signed-in person: /settings lands on the menu.

Words

Every label is 3 words or fewer (Search settings, Household, Preferences, Assistants, History, Install, Download my data, Sign out). Each row is an icon, a label and at most one short value. No helper text, no title-plus-description rows. The one accent on the screen is the search outline.

Choices made where the owner was silent

All Proposals; a wrong guess costs a small change.

  • Your name and picture is the first row (it replaces Me and the avatar header). It opens the You page.
  • The Get started strip is gone. Its two nudges become the value on the row they point to: Link yours on Assistants (first 2 days, not linked), Invite on Household (first 5 days, nobody else in).
  • Order: settings block, then History, Install, Download my data, then Sign out.
  • The Settings front is kept only for a visitor who is not signed in (theme and text size, no household rows). A signed-in person who opens /settings lands here, with the same ?from so Back still returns to the page they came from.
  • Pages behind the menu say Menu in their Back button.

Open questions

  • Categories and Locations cost 4 taps by browsing (2 by search). Option 1: leave as drawn (recommended; the owner said these are settings and search is now one tap away). Option 2: a Kitchen setup row back on the menu (2 taps, one more row).
  • Your name row versus the Household page, which also lists you. Recommended: keep both (one is the shortcut, the other is a person in a list).
  • The You page repeats Install, Download my data and Sign out. Recommended: remove them there so the menu is their only home (another agent is editing that page).
