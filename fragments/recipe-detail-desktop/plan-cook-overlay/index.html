Plan it and Cook it on the banner

Fragment. The owner asked (by voice, 18 October 2026) for Plan it and Cook it to sit directly on the hero banner image, always visible and not shown on hover, instead of the right side of row two. He has not chosen where. Three variations of the recommended page; each changes only that. Mockup only. Kitchie issue #830. Open decision, awaiting owner

What is the same in all three
  • Buttons are solid (or dark glass with white text), 44px tall (36px on a very short banner), readable over a light, a dark and a black and white banner photo.
  • The whole banner opens the photo viewer (click or Enter), with a visible magnifier in a corner the buttons do not use. It is the first tab stop; Plan it and Cook it stay after the History icon, so the keyboard order is exactly today's.
  • Row two keeps Serves, the time badge, the version chip and History, left aligned. The hero is 4px shorter (190 instead of 194 at 1280x720): the Serves button is 40px, so row two barely shrinks.
  • No banner: nothing is overlaid; Plan it and Cook it stay at the right of row two, as in the recommended page.

Advice from Claude, not a decision

Suggested pick: A. It keeps today's buttons and today's place (bottom right of the hero), so there is nothing new to learn; it does not hide the photo's centre; both buttons keep the exact styling used everywhere else in the app. B is the safest choice if banner photos turn out to be unpredictable (it is legible over anything, but it is a new component and sits over the middle of the photo). C is the one to pick if the bottom of the photo matters more than the corner (it leaves the lower half clear, but at 1280x720 it is a row, not a stack). The owner decides; nothing here is locked.

Weak spots
  • The sample banners put the dish right of centre, so A and C cover part of the plate on the sample photos. In the app the banner is a crop with a focus point; the button corner should follow it or be chosen once for all.
  • The banner is 84px tall at 1280x720 (the page's own rule). Buttons fit with 20px to spare; under 68px they shrink to 36px (tested at 56px). Nothing in the page makes the banner shorter than 84px.
  • The photo viewer behind the banner is the Photos tab's viewer; the recommended page had no banner click before, so this is new in all three.
  • The brief said the title and share row sit above the banner; in the current recommended page the banner is on top and the title row is below it. That order is kept.
