Adaptive contrast, option C: test banners

Each row is the same recipe page (C) on a synthetic 1600x560 banner. Left: the fixed dark corner scrim and theme buttons (what C had before; also the fallback when the image cannot be read). Right: adaptive, which samples the banner behind the stack and picks light or dark buttons. The test banners carry opposite-tone bands at the top and bottom edge that the page crops away, so a wrong crop mapping would pick the wrong style. Readouts are read live from each frame. Mockup only.

How to read the numbers
  • lum / p10 / p90: relative luminance (0 black, 1 white) of the region behind the stack: mean, 10th and 90th percentile pixel.
  • text: contrast of the label on its button fill; AA needs 4.5.
  • edge: contrast of the button's outline against the worst pixel behind it (after the scrim); 3 is the target.
  • scrim: 0, 30% or 50% backing in the opposite tone, added only for mid-tone or busy regions.
