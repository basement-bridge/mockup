Recipe page, desktop layout options

Fragment. The job: read and cook from the full recipe page on a big desktop screen without scrolling where possible, with ingredients that wrap instead of wasting white space, notes close by, and history, serves, Plan it and Cook it given a place. Five options, each a live page with short, typical, long, notes and cooked states. Mockup only: nothing is built or saved. Kitchie issue #830. Draft, awaiting owner choice

The owner's words issue #830
  • Ingredients you can text wrap; a lot of white space is wasted.
  • Notes are also important.
  • Do not scroll if it can be avoided.
  • Pay attention to the history, the serves and the other bits.
The five options

Thumbnails are the typical recipe at 1440x900, dark. Each option also has light, 1280x720 and 1920x1080 screenshots in its folder.
