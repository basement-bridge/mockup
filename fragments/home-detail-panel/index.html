Home: the detail panel Fragment, proposals

Owner, 12 Oct 2026: on the desktop Home screen, picking an item from the search results, a Cooking idea, an item under On the way out or one under Running low should show its detail. If there is room, the detail displaces This week to the right. If there is not, it stacks on top of This week and leaves 1 to 2 mm of This week showing so it can still be found. The detail is the simple view, like the Pantry item flyout, with a recipe-specific variant for Cooking ideas. Mockup first: the owner confirms before anything is built. Every number and rule below is a proposal. Notes and decisions 133 to 148: job note, handover.

The rule and the breakpoint

Home on desktop is rail, an off-centre column, then the right lane (desktop layout). The detail panel takes the right lane's slot, so it is where the Pantry's flyout is. This week is displaced one lane right only if all four pieces fit side by side:

PieceWidth (px)Where it comes from

Detail panel400The Pantry item flyout's width

Needed to displace1624Proposed breakpoint for option A

Assumptions: the column keeps its full 736 (below 1224 it already gives up width, as in the Pantry); free space collects on the right, never between lanes; the browser's width includes any scrollbar, which is what a media query reads. What it means in practice: a 1440 or 1536 laptop is always in the stacked case, 1680 and 1920 and up displace. So the stacked case is the everyday case on laptops, and the peek matters more than the displace rule does. Below 1624 the lane stays at 400 and the column never moves; the panel is just peek narrower than the lane.

The peek: 6 px by default (the owner said 1 to 2 mm, which is 4 to 8 CSS pixels at 96 dpi; real millimetres depend on the screen). It is the edge of the This week card with a small grip, and it is a button: click it, hover it (it widens by 8 px), or press W, and This week comes to the front while the panel waits behind it with its own peek on the right. Nothing is closed and nothing is lost.

What this loads

Default path of Home: theme.css, flyout.css (the item flyout's styles, already shared), home.css, data.js, home.js, plus one size file chosen by media query (stack.css or displace.css; push.css only for C). No images. Deferred: recipe.js on the first Cooking idea opened, child.js on the first E or comma. A real build would also fetch an item's or idea's detail only when it is opened, never with the list (the owner's rule for desktop). layout.js exists only so one page can show three options; the build drops it.

Open questions for the owner

  • Breakpoint. 1624 (A) means a 1440 or 1536 laptop always stacks. Is that fine, or is 1448 (C, with the column narrowing) better? A wrong guess costs one number and, for C, a reflow.
  • Peek width. 6 px proposed (4 to 8 is 1 to 2 mm). Larger is easier to hit; smaller looks tidier.
  • Panel and the search dropdown. The dropdown lives inside the column, so the panel never sits over it. Proposed: choosing a result closes the dropdown and opens the panel, the box keeps what was typed. Alternative: the dropdown stays open so the next result is one click away.
  • Recipe variant content. Proposed: title, time and serves, how many are in stock, uses up, Add to plan, Open recipe, then missing or short lines with Add to list, then what is in the pantry. Is "uses up" the right fourth tile? Should the steps be here?
  • Esc. You wrote Esc to close. Desktop closes with Ctrl or Cmd + Enter and the X, because plain Esc leaves full screen. Keep that here too, or let Esc close the panel on Home only?
  • Edit and History open tucked under the header inside the panel, never as a fourth column, so the layout never needs a fourth lane. OK?
  • Phone and tablet keep the bottom sheet; this is desktop only.
