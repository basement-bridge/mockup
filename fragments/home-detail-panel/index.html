The rule and the breakpoint

Home on desktop is rail, an off-centre column, then the right lane (desktop layout). The detail panel takes the right lane's slot, so it is where the Pantry's flyout is. This week is displaced one lane right only if all four pieces fit side by side:

Assumptions: the column keeps its full 736 (below 1224 it already gives up width, as in the Pantry); free space collects on the right, never between lanes; the browser's width includes any scrollbar, which is what a media query reads. What it means in practice: a 1440 or 1536 laptop is always in the stacked case, 1680 and 1920 and up displace. So the stacked case is the everyday case on laptops, and the peek matters more than the displace rule does. Below 1624 the lane stays at 400 and the column never moves; the panel is just peek narrower than the lane.

The peek: 6 px by default (the owner said 1 to 2 mm, which is 4 to 8 CSS pixels at 96 dpi; real millimetres depend on the screen). It is the edge of the This week card with a small grip, and it is a button: click it, hover it (it widens by 8 px), or press W, and This week comes to the front while the panel waits behind it with its own peek on the right. Nothing is closed and nothing is lost.

A recipe search result (decisions 288 to 291)

A recipe in the search dropdown opens the Recipes summary flyout (recipe-browse-full option A), not the idea view; Cooking ideas keep the idea view. Dashboard: the flyout takes the lane and covers This week (opaque, no peek, no displace; closing shows This week again; Proposal, as built). Compact (the Compact view button now works here) has no gutter, so while the flyout is open the page reserves the lane and the column steps left; an item result or a row in Compact follows its link (a toast says so). Pantry item results keep the Pantry flyout. Files: flyout.js and flyout.css, fetched on the first recipe result chosen, never on page load.

What this loads

Default path of Home: theme.css, flyout.css (the item flyout's styles, already shared), home.css, data.js, home.js, plus one size file chosen by media query (stack.css or displace.css; push.css only for C). No images. Deferred: flyout.js and flyout.css on the first recipe search result chosen, recipe.js on the first Cooking idea opened, child.js on the first E or comma. A real build would also fetch an item's or idea's detail only when it is opened, never with the list (the owner's rule for desktop). layout.js exists only so one page can show three options; the build drops it.

Confirmed by the owner (12 Oct 2026, by voice)

  • Breakpoint 1624 px, peek 6 px, layout A the default.
  • Search: choosing a result closes the dropdown and opens the panel at once.
  • Recipe panel: “missing” means completely out of stock; “running low” is not missing. A short “Why this fits” blurb, no recipe steps.

  • Edit and History swap the same panel's content in place; no fourth column.
  • “Used in” on the item panel: only ideas planned in the rolling next 7 days, by weekday (“Used in: Tuesday, prawn curry”), omitted if none. Try Spring onion stalks with a planned week and Jalapeño peppers (no line).
