Plan UI review: desktop and phone

Owner ask, 12 October 2026: analyse the Plan screens on desktop and phone and amend them with better choices, mockup first. Nothing here is built in Kitchie and nothing is confirmed: every number below (63 to 84) is a proposal by the design agent. Written analysis: plan-ui-review.md. Builds on decisions 1 to 48 (phone Plan) and the four desktop layouts A to D (plan-desktop, decisions 49 to 62), which stay as drawn.

Recommended default

Desktop: the week fills the window beside the rail (no left-anchored cap); a week summary strip and a today marker lead; the week is a board of seven columns from 1760px and agenda rows below (same cards); the inspector is docked at the right edge, full height, scrolls on its own, close x always visible, actions pinned in its footer; people as Mine / Everyone plus member chips with a +N overflow; status chips with words; open nights quiet, with the add actions on hover and focus.

Phone: a sticky week header, people strip and day strip; agenda cards with labelled swipe actions (mouse drag works too); detail in a two-stop bottom sheet (half, full) with actions pinned at the bottom; "Open your assistant" in the thumb zone.

Desktop, layout

1 Week board + docked inspector recommended

Seven day columns with sticky day heads, the summary strip above, the inspector docked right. The board needs about 1150px of main column, so from 1760px; below that the same cards draw as agenda rows.

2 Agenda rows + docked inspector

One row per day, big day label, a card that spans the row reads as one line (who and what left, status right). Works from 1024 and is the fallback of 1 below 1760.

Desktop, inspector

Docked recommended

Fixed to the right edge, full height, header and footer pinned, only the middle scrolls (the cut-off On hand list is fixed). Pushes the page, so it never covers a day. Close: the x (44px, always in view) or Ctrl/Cmd Enter. Idle, from 1440, it shows the week: buy by day, use first, cook order.

Flyout

Floating card, inset 12px, shadow. Opens only on a pick and returns the width when closed. Below 1440 it overlays the page with a scrim (Esc, x, Ctrl/Cmd Enter or a click on the scrim close it); from 1440 it pushes. Pantry-like.

Decisions 63 to 84: proposed, not confirmed

Full wording in plan-ui-review.md. Other job notes reuse some of these numbers for their own lists, so always cite them with the file.

  1. The desktop week fills the window beside the rail; the header rule, summary and week span the whole main column.
  2. The Week board is seven columns only from 1760px; below, the same cards draw as agenda rows.
  3. The inspector is docked or a flyout, fixed, full height, with its own scroll; it never covers the day it is about at 1440 and up.
  4. Inspector: close x always visible; Replan and Not cooking pinned in a footer; Ctrl/Cmd Enter closes; Esc closes only a flyout, menus and popovers.
  5. Hold sits in the On hand heading row (and Add all in Missing), on one baseline above one divider.
  6. People filter: Mine / Everyone segment, member chips with initials, "+N more" overflow with search.
  7. Day rows: a large day label, a Today pill and accent border, past days dimmed.
  8. Open night: no dashed box; Ask assistant visible, Add by hand and Not cooking on hover or focus (always on phone).
  9. Not cooking is a short quiet block with an accent bar (this week) or a plain bar (usual day off), not a filled tile.
  10. Status chips carry words; icon-only and one-line are alternatives; every icon has a label.
  11. The source line ("Made up by your assistant", "not saved as a recipe") is one plain line in the inspector, not a chip.
  12. Card titles wrap, never truncate; the ... button is 40px (44 on phone); right-click opens the same menu.
  13. A week summary strip: nights cooking n of 7 with a rhythm of seven dots, to buy, use first, leftover nights; tiles highlight cards.
  14. A today marker everywhere (pill, border, ringed date).
  15. Leftover chains are shown: "Cooks double: Friday leftovers" and "Leftovers from Wed 14".
  16. Keys: arrows, E or Enter, Ctrl/Cmd Enter, X, Shift X, H, L, Z, A, T, M, ?.
  17. Phone: a sticky stack of week header, people strip and day strip.
  18. Phone swipe: labelled tint, commit at 88px, undo; works with a mouse and with X and Shift X.
  19. Phone detail: a two-stop bottom sheet with a pinned footer and a button equivalent of the drag.
  20. Phone: "Open your assistant" sits in the thumb zone above the nav.
  21. Phone M2 has no sideways page swipe; day changes by strip or bottom buttons.
  22. Phone people strip stays decision 48; a searchable menu is offered for large households.
