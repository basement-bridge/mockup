The gap under the day card (owner screenshot) fix

The screenshot shows a gap of about 90 px between the tongue and the detail. It was a defect, not the tear: a pull-down on the detail moves the detail with a pull offset, and iOS Safari ends such a gesture with touchcancel (edge gesture, rubber band, bottom bar), which was never handled, so the offset stayed. Reproduced (83 px after a cancelled pull) and fixed: every end of a touch resets it, a new touch clears a stale one, and the detail no longer rubber-bands the page (overscroll-behavior-y). The stack itself has no gap at 390 x 844, 390 x 760 (visible bottom bar), 360 x 640 and with the long list: it is fixed between the header and the nav and uses no viewport-height units, so the 100vh quirk does not apply.

Not verified on a real iPhone: Chromium's touchcancel reproduces the stuck state; the cause on iOS is inferred from the screenshot.
