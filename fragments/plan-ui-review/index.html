Rules, and what is not decided

  • Scope: the active pill's own plan for the selected week. Other members' plans are read only: disabled, with the reason. Past weeks are read only: disabled. Past nights are read only: it clears from today on (Mon 12 stays).
  • Not cooking: recommended default keeps the marks (a day off is a decision, not a meal; ?cl=all shows the other way). A "usual day off" is a rule and always stays.
  • Undo names its action ("Undo clear", "Undo copy") and lasts 14 s. Clear then Copy: recommended is a short stack: the latest action shows Undo; undo it and the earlier one shows Undo again (+1 more is shown). One level versus a stack is an open question.
  • No confirm (Undo is one tap), but never silent: the first cleared day says what went and pulses. On Mine, household meals I eat are cleared too (same set Copy last week uses); alternative in the open questions.

Surprise me, in plain words (not UI copy)

owner-confirmed It looks at timing. Earlier in the week, or when there is room to shop, it may suggest something new and may use items already on the shopping list or already being bought for planned meals. Late in the week, or when you are mostly eating from what is in the house, it favours using up on-hand stock. It never repeats a recent meal. Mock thresholds (proposal): on-hand if the night is today, tomorrow or Fri to Sun of this week; otherwise new; recent means any planned meal within 7 days.
