Mobile Home, option G: cooking ideas first, search in the greeting row

A seventh answer, drawn after the owner saw option F on his phone. Cooking ideas is always the first card, right under the greeting, and never moves. On the way out and Running low sit underneath, collapsed behind a chevron, and are not drawn at all when empty. Search is a small 48px magnifier in the greeting row, at the right, the same box in the same corner as Pantry’s; it becomes a full takeover only on tap. On the way out and Running low underneath, “some chevron or something that would then expand. By default it should not steal all the space”; “if there’s nothing running low, it doesn’t even exist”; their order follows the same situational logic as the idle search card. Search: “we don’t need a real estate hogging search screen all the time”, “I already don’t like that I had to scroll”; small, in the greeting row on the right, “that spot, right thumb, is perfect” (Pantry’s spot), the same place on all screens; full takeover only on tap; no pinned This week on mobile (“that’s not the plan”); after about two seconds idle the single most context-relevant card; typing shows desktop’s contextual dropdown. Recipes tab: parked, “we want to drive recipes through AI”.

The switches pretend the time, whether tonight (or, after dinner, tomorrow) is planned and complete, and which of the two collapsed cards has anything in it. They redraw every frame. Add ?trigger=focus to surface the card the moment the magnifier is tapped.

The frames (390 × 844)

How it behaves

  • At rest: one 56px row (greeting at the left, magnifier at the right), then Cooking ideas: the lead idea with Make it and Plan, then two other ideas. Then the collapsed cards that have something, in the order below. The greeting row, the lead idea and its buttons are on the first screen with no scroll in every state drawn (checked at 390 × 844). The greeting row sticks to the top while the page scrolls, so search is always in the same spot.
  • Cooking ideas is labelled by when it applies: “Today · Idea for tonight” (or Planned for tonight, Nothing fits tonight) until 20:00, then “Coming up · Idea for tomorrow” (as recipe-ideas/ideas.js already does from 8 pm). It never moves down the page.
  • A collapsed card is one row: icon, title, the first two names, a count, a chevron. The whole row is the button (aria-expanded), the chevron turns over when open, and the count is text, so state is never colour alone. Open shows the same .minis and View all as today’s cards. Empty means not drawn: no row, no chevron, no heading.
  • Tap the magnifier: the greeting row becomes the search field with Cancel, and every other thing steps aside with the same weight: the greeting, Cooking ideas, both cards and the bottom bar. No This week strip. Nothing else happens yet.
  • About two seconds with nothing typed: one card comes up (next sections), announced once through a polite live region; focus stays in the field. It is the same markup as the Cooking ideas lead.
  • Two characters: desktop’s dropdown replaces the card (On tonight’s plan or If plans change, Fits tonight, In your pantry, Needs a shop, On your shopping list, Left out for who is eating; at most six rows). Emptying the field brings the wait back.
  • Cancel, Esc, or tapping away with nothing typed: Home comes back exactly as it was, scroll included, and focus returns to the magnifier. Tapping away with words typed keeps the dropdown and drops the keyboard.
  • Same place elsewhere: the last two frames draw Pantry and Shopping with the magnifier as the rightmost control of the same 56px row and the same 48px box. Recipes is parked and not drawn.

Proposals (the owner was silent; each is mine, with the cost of a wrong guess)

  • MG-P1 The windows are F’s: 11:00, 16:30 and 20:00 (the first and last exist in ideas.js). Wrong guess: a household that eats at 6:30 or 8 pm sees the wrong order or lead for up to an hour; the household’s own cooking time is the better answer. Four constants.
  • MG-P2 The clock is the device’s local time. Wrong guess: someone travelling sees home time’s meal.
  • MG-P3 Two seconds of idle, counted from the tap, restarted when the field empties; ?trigger=focus compares. Wrong guess: too short is a card under someone about to type; too long is a bar that looks broken. One constant.
  • MG-P4 Content appearing unasked is polite: one live-region line per tap, focus never moves, no timer closes it; reduced motion removes the fade. Wrong guess: one extra spoken line.
  • MG-P5 Takeover hides everything with the same weight, and keeps nothing (no This week, no exception). Hidden, not dimmed, because the keyboard leaves no room behind. Home returns where it was scrolled and focus returns to the magnifier. Wrong guess: less sense of place; a dim is the alternative.
  • MG-P6 The surfaced card follows the table above. New against F: a plan that is missing things brings up what needs buying (the owner’s mid-morning example), at morning and mid-day only. It is the only purchase nudge, and it exists because the plan itself needs it. Wrong guess: an unwanted nudge; the card is one function.
  • MG-P7 The order rule above, in particular that cooking time puts On the way out last and that “plan missing things” is what moves Running low to the front (“needs buying” is drawn as Running low; the plan’s missing items are not a third card). Wrong guess: the wrong card is first of two collapsed rows, one tap from the other; cost is small.
  • MG-P8 Collapsed on every visit. The open or closed state is not remembered. Wrong guess: someone who always opens a card taps it each time; remembering per device is a few lines (localStorage in try/catch) and is MG-Q3.
  • MG-P9 A collapsed card shows its title, the first two names and a count: about 64px each. Wrong guess: 64px of a small screen per card; a title and chevron only (about 52px) is the alternative.
  • MG-P10 Cooking ideas keeps two Other ideas under the lead (hidden when nothing fits). Wrong guess: about 120px that the owner may want back (MG-Q6).
  • MG-P11 The label “Today” / “Coming up” flips at 20:00 with the rest. Wrong guess: a late cook sees tomorrow’s idea.
  • MG-P12 The greeting row is sticky, so search is always in the same spot. Wrong guess: 56px less content while scrolled; unpinned, search needs a scroll to the top.
  • MG-P13 The magnifier is the Pantry box: 48px, --r-ctl, surface fill, border, icon only, accessible name “Search”. No label, hint, microphone or coachmark. Wrong guess: someone does not know it answers questions with nothing typed.
  • MG-P14 This week is not drawn on mobile Home at all, at rest or in the takeover (the owner said it is not the plan for the takeover; at rest he was silent). Wrong guess: the plan is not visible from Home on a phone; Plan on the lead idea and the Plan screen reach it (MG-Q2).
  • MG-P15 Make it and Plan stay in both windows, including after dinner, where Plan is the useful one. Wrong guess: a Make it that means “start now” beside tomorrow’s idea.
  • MG-P16 Six rows, as F. MG-P17 F’s remembered start (homeStart) and its Settings row are dropped: G has one Home. MG-P18 Phone width only (under 600px).
  • MG-P19 Pantry and Shopping are drawn only to show position. Pantry keeps its own pull-down search and Filters; the magnifier there is already where G puts it. Nothing on either screen is changed by this fragment.

Open questions, with what a wrong guess costs

  • MG-Q1 Is G instead of F? Recommendation: yes. F’s pinned This week and remembered start are superseded here; F stays drawn and unchanged. Cost: none, both exist.
  • MG-Q2 Does mobile Home show the week at all? (a) No (drawn); (b) a third collapsed row, “This week, 3 planned”, same chevron; (c) only through Plan. Recommendation: (a) now, (b) if people miss it. Cost: (a) hides the plan; (b) another 52 to 64px.
  • MG-Q3 Remember open or closed per device? Recommendation: no, collapsed every time (“should not steal all the space”). Cost: a repeat tap for some.
  • MG-Q4 At cooking time, hide both cards? The owner says on the way out is pointless then and, for the search card, running low too. Recommendation: keep both collapsed, in the order above, because collapsed they cost one row each. Cost: two rows of low value on the busiest screen.
  • MG-Q5 Two seconds idle, or on tap? Recommendation: the owner’s two seconds, test on a phone. Cost: a flash of content versus a bar that looks broken.
  • MG-Q6 Keep Other ideas on mobile Home? Recommendation: keep two. Cost: about 120px.
  • MG-Q7 Top-right is the far corner on a tall phone. The owner chose it (Pantry’s spot). Alternative: a bottom-right floating button. Recommendation: the owner’s. Cost: a reach for small hands.
  • MG-Q8 Recipes keeps its own search for now (parked). When it follows, it should take the same corner.

What loads

Default path: this page, theme.css (two themes inline), theme.js, home.css (phone only) and home.js (markup, order(), the folds), plus mock.js (the mockup’s frames, never for the build). No images, no fonts beyond the theme, no framework, no network call. A collapsed card is one row; its body is written on the first open, in place (DESIGN.md 6a), so a closed card costs nothing more. Deferred: search.css and search.js (the sample index, rank(), surface(), the dropdown) are fetched on the first tap on the magnifier, so they arrive during the two-second wait. In the build, the one read they need (pantry, recipes, shopping, plan) is made then, not on page load. Nothing on scroll.
