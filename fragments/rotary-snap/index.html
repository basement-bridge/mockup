Quick controlssnap and wide steps

Owner, 25 Oct 2026 (Kitchie #1110; low vision; phone only; on UAT the rotary is built in 0.176 and Text size in 0.174): the theme rotary always lands on one theme, centred, never between two, and the neighbours must not peek in from the sides; swipe only changes it; a one-time wiggle says it swipes; Text size has one label and seven steps as wide as the row allows.

Play it

Menu, pinned controls

Swipe the theme sideways (or use the arrow keys when it has focus). One theme fills the row and it always comes to rest in the middle. Tapping the theme you see does nothing. The wiggle plays once, the first time this area is in view on this device; Play the wiggle forgets that and plays it again.

The wiggle, frame by frame

About 1.3 seconds, nothing else moves: the strip eases 12px left, 12px right, and settles centred. It uses a transform, so it fires no scroll, changes no theme and cannot be mistaken for a swipe. It does not play with reduced motion, while the search has focus, during a drag, or ever again after it ran or after the person swiped.

Text size, measured

One label, seven steps edge to edge. A step is a 44px tall target and a seventh of the bar wide; the coloured part is inset 1px a side. Measured live below with the page’s 20px gutter, the tile’s 1px border and 5px of padding a side.

WidthPhoneBarStep (target)Step (drawn)

At 360 a step is exactly 44px as a target, 42px drawn. Seven 44px steps need 308px; a 360px phone has 320px inside its gutters, so 44px is the most it can be unless the gutter shrinks. At 320 it is 38px (below 44; fallback in the notes).

What changed

  • Theme rotary: mandatory snap, one stop per swipe, never between two, the neighbours cut off. No arrows, no dots. Swipe only: a tap on the visible theme does nothing. Double tap still resets; arrow keys still choose.
  • One-time wiggle as drawn above.
  • Text size: the label Text size is the only word. The value line (Match my phone, Larger, +10%) and the end words are gone from the screen; the filled steps, the ringed step and the dot in the middle (Match my phone) say it, and a screen reader still hears the value. The pin sits on the label’s line, so the bar has the whole row.
  • Words: one label on the control (2 words), none on the rotary beyond the theme’s own name.

Cost of the wide bar

The text tile is one row taller than before (a label line over the bar, about 98px against 56px). With the theme under it the two rows are about 170px. The quick area is still two rows.

Not verified

A real phone (the wiggle’s feel, the snap under a thumb); VoiceOver and TalkBack activating a theme; three or four pinned controls (chips): an opened Text size chip shares its row with the other chips, so its steps are 26px at 390 and 22px at 360 (see the build notes).
