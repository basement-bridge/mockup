Removed members: a week to undo, then erased

Mockup first, nothing built Kitchie issue #85. Phone and the desktop Profile window, light and dark. Nothing is saved or sent: the buttons move a sample household between states.

What the owner asked for

Typed, 1 October 2026: a removed member can be restored for up to a week. If nobody acts, they are gone completely, like a “delete my account” under European rules: their data and the journal’s link to them are erased, and any activity they did that must stay in the transaction log is anonymised. On the open questions, the owner answered one thing: “Former member is all we need there’s no need for any other follow-ups identification any of that.” The issue carries the label approved for build; the other three questions have no written answer.

Decisions: the owner’s words
  1. RM-D1 A removed member can be restored for up to a week (owner, 1 Oct 2026).
  2. RM-D2 If nobody acts, they are gone completely, like “delete my account” under European rules: their data and the journal’s link to them are erased; activity that must stay in the transaction log is anonymised (owner, 1 Oct 2026).
  3. RM-D3 History says “a former member” after the erase, and nothing more identifying (owner, comment, 1 Oct 2026; answers issue question 3).
  4. RM-D4 The removed person gets no notice and nothing else follows (owner, same comment: “no need for any other follow-ups”). Read as the answer to issue question 4; the owner did not say “no notice” in those words.
From the issue, carried as written (label approved for build)
  1. RM-B1 Removing (the #82 danger-zone flow) sets removed_at and ends sessions, the assistant link and invites at once.
  2. RM-B2 They leave the member list and appear under a “Removed, can be restored until <date>” line with a Restore button.
  3. RM-B3 Restore needs a new sign-in (sessions stay ended) and puts them back with the same name.
  4. RM-B4 After 7 days a purge runs (app start, then hourly) and deletes the member row, sessions, assistant link, their invites, join events, notices that name them and removal records that name them.
  5. RM-B5 Journal rows they wrote keep the item and the change but lose member_id and any name in note. The append-only trigger gets one narrow, audited exception for that (RM-Q1).
  6. RM-B6 The window is exactly 7 days from removal, shown on the household page. Leaving is out of scope.
Proposals (the mockup’s own; none approved)
  1. RM-P1 One Removed card, under Members and above Invite someone, drawn only while someone is held. It replaces today’s Removals card, since a removal record that names someone is erased with them (RM-B4).
  2. RM-P2 A held row reads “Arjan removed Sam on Wed 14 Oct.”, then “Can be restored until Wed 21 Oct, 9:15 am · 3 days left”. Whole days while two or more are left, then hours, then “Less than an hour left”; the last 24 hours in the danger colour.
  3. RM-P3 Restore asks first, in place, as Make admin does (SW-86): it hands someone access back.
  4. RM-P4 A restored person is an ordinary member, even if they were an admin; an admin has to make them one again.
  5. RM-P5 A restored member reads “Has to sign in again” until they have a live session.
  6. RM-P6 Held rows are ordered by who is erased soonest.
  7. RM-P7 Whoever may remove may restore (follows SW-O63).
  8. RM-P8 At the 7-day mark Restore is refused and the row is hidden even if the hourly purge has not run yet, so the page and the server agree to the minute.
  9. RM-P9 The remove page tells the week, its exact end and what the erase covers before the name is typed.
  10. RM-P10 History says “by a former member” in the place of the name. Each erased person reads the same; they cannot be told apart (RM-D3).
  11. RM-P11 Nothing tells the household when an erase happens and nothing is logged (RM-D4).
  12. RM-P12 Erase now is drawn as an option, off by default (RM-Q2).
  13. RM-P13 Times are shown in the household’s time, as “Wed 21 Oct, 9:15 am”.
Open questions, with a recommended position
  1. RM-Q1 Approve the narrow journal exception (anonymise only member_id and note, only at purge, with the trigger allowing nothing else)? It changes a protected rule. Recommend yes, as the issue wrote it. A wrong no means a purge cannot anonymise: either the link to the person stays, or journal rows are deleted and stock history is lost. The label is not read as this approval.
  2. RM-Q2 Erase now, instead of waiting? Recommend not in version 1. It is a second irreversible path and the week already meets the owner’s ask. A wrong no costs a household up to a week when someone asks to be erased at once; drawn here so it can be added.
  3. RM-Q3 A held person redeems a new invite. Recommend it restores the same member (no duplicate, hold cleared, same name), since a member has just invited them. A wrong guess either blocks a deliberate re-invite for a week or creates a second row for one sign-in.
  4. RM-Q4 Restored as an admin if they were one (RM-P4)? Recommend no. Giving household-wide rights back is a separate, deliberate step.
  5. RM-Q5 Who may restore (RM-P7)? Recommend the same people who may remove. Open with SW-O63.
  6. RM-Q6 The one-time scrub of old “by Name” notes written before #84 rides on the same exception. Recommend a separate migration step after RM-Q1; not drawn.
