Tapping the magnifier makes a search bar fly out from right to left over the whole row, hiding Filters and +. The shallow pull-down opens the same bar. Drag the list down in the live frame to try the pull (mouse works too). The desktop docked search bar is unchanged; the no-results state is also drawn there (Kitchie #807, below).

What the owner asked (voice, 17 October 2026)
  • PS-D1 Filters anchored to the left border.
  • PS-D2 A magnifying-glass button as the rightmost item at the right border, + immediately to its left.
  • PS-D3 Tapping the glass flies a search bar out from right to left over the whole row; Filters and + hidden while it is open.
  • PS-D4 The existing shallow pull-down (behaviour preserved) opens the same full-row bar; the pull and the flyout share the "takes over the entire row" behaviour.
  • PS-D5 Text field filtering the list by item name; empty and no-results states; the keyboard's Search key submits.
Choices the mockup made where the owner was silent (Proposal)
  • PS-P1 Closing: the X at the left of the bar, the glass again, or Escape; closing clears the query. Open (PS-Q1).
  • PS-P2 An X icon, not the live word “Cancel”.
  • PS-P3 The tap fades Filters and + out while the bar grows from under the glass; the pull keeps its curtain.
  • PS-P4 The glass is a real button in both layers, so it never moves and toggles.
  • PS-P5 Matches the item name only, case and edge spaces ignored.
  • PS-P6 The mic stays inside the bar beside the glass.
  • PS-P7 The location chips keep filtering during a search; the no-results state offers “Search all places” when the item is under another chip.
  • PS-P8 Filters moves out of the right-hand group to the left; no visible item count.
Open questions
  • PS-Q1 How does the bar close, and does closing keep the query? (Assumed: X, glass, Escape; clears.) Wrong guess costs: a kept query with no visible bar reads as a bug.
  • PS-Q2 Today's live phone row has Filters and + together at the right; the owner described Filters at the left. Is moving Filters to the left border the change?
  • PS-Q3 The custom-view row (no Filters button) is not drawn: [+] [glass] with nothing at the left.
  • PS-Q4 Whether the glass needs a keyboard cue (desktop only, out of scope).
Add to shopping list from no results (Kitchie #807): what the owner asked
  • Owner, text in chat, 16 Oct 2026 (as written into #807): when a search finds nothing there is a button to add it as a new item; there should also be a button next to it that says “Add to shopping list”. Tapping it puts the searched words on the list as a hand-added entry and confirms in place, without leaving the search.
  • Stays the same: Add as a new item and its behaviour, the empty message, the Shopping screen and how hand-added entries look there.
#807 decisions (PS-S1 to PS-S15). S1 to S3 are the owner’s; the rest are Proposal.
  • PS-S1 (owner) A second button, “Add to shopping list”, next to “Add as a new item”. PS-S2 (owner) The tap confirms in place; the search stays open. PS-S3 (owner) The message, the Add button’s behaviour and the Shopping screen are unchanged.
  • PS-S4 Labels are the owner’s words, short; the typed words are in the headline and in each button’s spoken name. The words-in-each-label layout is kept at ?layout=stack.
  • PS-S5 Side by side and equal width on a phone (a label may take two lines at 390px); stacked under 380px; one line each on the desktop.
  • PS-S6 Add as a new item first and filled; Add to shopping list second, outlined.
  • PS-S7 The tap: the button becomes On your list (the item sheet’s words); a line under it says “xyz” is on your shopping list. with Undo. No toast. Undo puts the button back and returns focus to it.
  • PS-S8 The typed text is kept and the bar stays open. PS-S9 Already on the list: said in words, nothing changed, no Undo (the tap decides; no lookup while typing). PS-S10 The words match a used-up tracked item: linked to it and said, with Undo.
  • PS-S11 No quantity is asked (set it on the Shopping screen). PS-S12 The words go on as typed, trimmed. PS-S13 Shown with a place chip on too; not in the “in another place” state or with an empty field.
  • PS-S14 Desktop: the same block in the 640px column under the docked bar; the rail’s Shopping badge goes up by one and back on Undo. PS-S15 The shopper link shows the entry like any hand-added one (not drawn).
#807 open questions (recommended position first)
  • PS-SQ1 Order and weight. Recommended: existing Add first and filled, the list button second and outlined. Or the list button leads, since a miss usually means “we need to buy it”. Costs: a CSS swap.
  • PS-SQ2 Keep the typed text after the tap? Recommended: keep, bar open. Or clear and close with a toast and Undo.
  • PS-SQ3 Wording. Recommended: “Add to shopping list” and “On your list”. Or “Add to list”.
  • PS-SQ4 A used-up match: link and say so (recommended), or ask first?
  • PS-SQ5 Show “On your list” before the tap? Recommended: no, the tap decides.
  • PS-SQ6 How long does Undo last? Recommended: until the text changes or the bar closes.
  • PS-SQ7 A link to the Shopping screen in the confirmation? Recommended: not now.
  • PS-SQ8 Side by side on the phone (recommended) or stacked?
  • PS-SQ9 Order of work: the live app has the add button under the search box today; this block is built where the add button is.

What this loads (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path: search.css and search.js (small) on top of theme.css. Nothing deferred is fetched: the list is filtered in place from what is already on the page (section 6a). In the app the six refresh scenes still load on the first refresh; this mockup shows the refresh words only. The #807 block adds noresult.css and noresult.js (small); the call to the list is made on the tap only, never while typing, and the result is swapped in place. Checked at 390px, 360px and 1280px in light and dark with tools/pantry-search-check.mjs.
