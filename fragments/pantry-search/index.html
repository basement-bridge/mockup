Pantry search: a magnifier on the top row

Phone only. Mockup, nothing built.

The Pantry's top row on the phone, redrawn. Filters stays at the left border. At the right border sit + and then a magnifier, the magnifier as the rightmost item. Tapping the magnifier makes a search bar fly out from right to left over the whole row, hiding Filters and +. The shallow pull-down opens the same bar. Drag the list down in the live frame to try the pull (mouse works too). Desktop is out of scope: its docked search bar is unchanged.

What the owner asked (voice, 17 October 2026)
  • PS-D1 Filters anchored to the left border.
  • PS-D2 A magnifying-glass button as the rightmost item at the right border, + immediately to its left.
  • PS-D3 Tapping the glass flies a search bar out from right to left over the whole row; Filters and + hidden while it is open.
  • PS-D4 The existing shallow pull-down (behaviour preserved) opens the same full-row bar; the pull and the flyout share the "takes over the entire row" behaviour.
  • PS-D5 Text field filtering the list by item name; empty and no-results states; the keyboard's Search key submits.
Choices the mockup made where the owner was silent (Proposal)
  • PS-P1 Closing: the X at the left of the bar, the glass again, or Escape; closing clears the query. Open (PS-Q1).
  • PS-P2 An X icon, not the live word “Cancel”.
  • PS-P3 The tap fades Filters and + out while the bar grows from under the glass; the pull keeps its curtain.
  • PS-P4 The glass is a real button in both layers, so it never moves and toggles.
  • PS-P5 Matches the item name only, case and edge spaces ignored.
  • PS-P6 The mic stays inside the bar beside the glass.
  • PS-P7 The location chips keep filtering during a search; the no-results state offers “Search all places” when the item is under another chip.
  • PS-P8 Filters moves out of the right-hand group to the left; no visible item count.
Open questions
  • PS-Q1 How does the bar close, and does closing keep the query? (Assumed: X, glass, Escape; clears.) Wrong guess costs: a kept query with no visible bar reads as a bug.
  • PS-Q2 Today's live phone row has Filters and + together at the right; the owner described Filters at the left. Is moving Filters to the left border the change?
  • PS-Q3 The custom-view row (no Filters button) is not drawn: [+] [glass] with nothing at the left.
  • PS-Q4 Whether the glass needs a keyboard cue (desktop only, out of scope).
What this loads (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path: search.css and search.js (small) on top of theme.css. Nothing deferred is fetched: the list is filtered in place from what is already on the page (section 6a). In the app the six refresh scenes still load on the first refresh; this mockup shows the refresh words only. Checked at 390px in light and dark with tools/pantry-search-check.mjs.
