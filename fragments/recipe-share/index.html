Share a recipe outside the household

Fragment, four slices tied together. The job: send a recipe you are happy with to someone outside the household, with a link that expires and needs no sign-in, so it can also bring people to Kitchie. Each slice has three options and a recommended one (A). Mockup only: nothing is created, sent or saved. Options awaiting owner pick

The owner's words voice, 13 October 2026
  • Share a recipe he is happy with outside the household, like the shopping-list link: an expiring signed URL.
  • No sign-in needed to view, and the same if signed in. Multi-use, not single-use, but it expires.
  • The intent is growth hacking.
  • A share button at the top of the recipe, with a couple of placement options.
  • Pending URLs are visible inside the recipe, where they can be cancelled. Links can be renamed, like the shopping link.
Recommended path

Option A in every slice. Together they make one short story, and none of them covers the recipe or asks the cook a question they cannot already answer.

  1. Share icon in the top row with a count of active links, opening in place (sheet on phone, flyout on desktop).
  2. One create screen: a name, how long it works (7 days chosen), and a switch that keeps it as it is today.
  3. A Shared section after Photos lists every link with its countdown, opens, Copy, Rename and Cancel (Undo for 5 minutes).
  4. A quiet recipient page: the recipe first, one invitation card after the last note, a Save button if they are signed in.

The slices were drawn separately, so a few seams are not settled (where the link list lives, Esc, the default name). They are listed as open questions in the job note, and every decision there is proposed, none confirmed.

Where the slices disagree

Not settled; the owner or the next driver picks. Detail and the cost of a wrong guess are in the job note.

  • Where the link list lives. The Share icon opens a small list (placement), while the recommended manage option is a section in the page. Both can stay if the icon scrolls to the section.
  • Esc. Placement closes the flyout only with Ctrl or Cmd Enter, as Pantry does; the create sheet also closes with Esc.
  • The default name. The recipe title, up to 60 characters (create), or "Link, 13 Oct", up to 40 (manage).
  • One recipe or one version. The icon belongs to the recipe; the create sheet pins the version on screen.
