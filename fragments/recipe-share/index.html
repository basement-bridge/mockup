Share a recipe outside the household

Fragment, slices tied together. The job: send a recipe you are happy with to someone outside the household, with a link that expires and needs no sign-in, so it can also bring people to Kitchie. Each slice has three options; the owner has picked in all of them. Mockup only: nothing is created, sent or saved. Picks made, build in uat

The owner's words voice, 13 October 2026
  • Share a recipe he is happy with outside the household, like the shopping-list link: an expiring signed URL.
  • No sign-in needed to view, and the same if signed in. Multi-use, not single-use, but it expires.
  • The intent is growth hacking.
  • A share button at the top of the recipe, with a couple of placement options.
  • Pending URLs are visible inside the recipe, where they can be cancelled. Links can be renamed, like the shopping link.
Picked so far
Owner, 13 October 2026
  • Placement A: Share icon in the top row with an active-links badge on mobile; Share pill with key S on the full recipe page on desktop.
  • Flyout Share button: the desktop recipe summary flyout gets a Share button next to "Open full recipe", opening the share panel in the same lane. Proposed: its design.
  • Create sheet C: shares the usual version by default; a version choice only when other versions are available. The sender can optionally attach an invite: single-claim, sets up a new household, never joins one. An invite counts in the usual place of the existing invite functionality (no new allowance UI). Open: how that allowance is read.
  • Manage B: links are seen, renamed and cancelled in the share panel opened from the Share button (bottom sheet on phone, side panel on desktop).
  • Recipient bar B: a bottom bar after reading starts, shown only when the member who shared attached an invite and it has not been taken up. Otherwise the page has no growth invitation. People with an account can sign in from the page; people without one use the usual invite-only flow.
  • Process: the real feature is built with sensible defaults and merged into uat automatically (never main, never tags).

Picked path

Placement A, sheet C, manage B, recipient B. Together they make one short story, and none of them covers the recipe or asks the cook a question they cannot already answer.

  1. Share icon in the top row with a count of active links, opening in place (sheet on phone, panel on desktop; also from the desktop summary flyout).
  2. The create sheet (C): shares the usual version, a version choice only when others exist, and an optional invite.
  3. The same panel lists every link with its countdown, opens, Copy, Rename and Cancel (Undo for 5 minutes).
  4. A quiet recipient page: the recipe first, a bottom bar only when an unclaimed invite came with the link, sign-in for people who have an account.

Seams still open (the invite allowance, the sign-in return path, whether the claimed household gets the recipe) are in the job note.
