Recipe share: Share in the summary flyout

Fragment, mockup only: nothing is sent or saved, every link is a placeholder at kitchie.example. Desktop only. On the Recipes list, clicking a recipe row opens the summary flyout in the right lane. A Share button now sits right next to Open full recipe; it drills the share panel into the same lane, with the real links list and create form. Mobile has no summary flyout (a row tap opens the full recipe page, where Share is placement option A), so there is nothing to draw there.

Owner, 13 October 2026: a Share button "right next to the Open full recipe link"; tapping it "flies out a share panel" in "the same flyout lane" as the summary, not a popup. Full words, the chosen look, one alternative, proposals and open questions in notes.md.

State (every control in the frame is live; these just jump there)

Keys (focus anywhere in the window)

S opens Share from the summary. Esc goes back one step (cancels a rename first; from the form or the link made it returns to the links, from the links to the summary). Cmd or Ctrl + Esc (or Enter) closes the whole flyout. O is Open full recipe. ? or H lists the shortcuts, and S is on that list. Plain Esc on the summary does nothing, as everywhere on desktop.

What the panel does

Links: tap a name (pencil) to rename; Copy link copies the placeholder; Cancel link is one tap and the row becomes an Undo line for 5 minutes (manage, option A). Make another link (sheet option C, chosen 13 October 2026): name, a version choice only when the recipe has other versions (The usual preselected), 24 hours / 7 days / 30 days, the optional Include an invite switch (off by default; "2 of 3 invites left", disabled with a reason at the limit); then the link with Copy link and Share, an invite line when one was included, and Done. The links list now carries the allowance card and an invite line on rows, as in manage. The Share button's count is the number of links still working.

Default path and deferred

Default path loads the theme, flyout.css and flyout.js, no images, no network. The window and the keys are desktop.css and desktop.js, fetched only at 1024px and up; under 1024px nothing else is loaded (DESIGN.md sections 6 and 7). The panel is drawn with the summary and shown by a class here so opening is instant; in the build its code's code should load on the first Share press. Opening, going back, renaming, cancelling and creating change the lane in place; the page never reloads (section 6a).
