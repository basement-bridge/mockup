Shared recipe: what the recipient sees

Fragment, mockup only, nothing is built. Updated 14 October 2026 with the owner's picks of 13 October. Recipient side only. Full notes: notes.md. The page itself, with every state: page.html (parameters at the top of the file).

The owner's decisions (13 October 2026)
  • The growth invitation is option B, the bottom bar that appears after reading starts, and it appears only when the member who shared attached an invite and nobody has taken it up. In every other case (no invite, claimed, withdrawn, expired) there is no invitation at all: a clean recipe page.
  • A person with an account can sign in from the page; once signed in it shows Save to my recipes and no household claim (an invite never joins an existing household).
  • A person without an account who claims the invite goes to the usual invite-only sign-up (fragments/invite-new-household). The invite is single-purpose (the first person to activate it claims it) and sets up a new household.

1. The invitation: the bar, only with an unclaimed invite

Same recipe either way. The bar waits until the person has scrolled past about a third of the page; its x lasts the visit. On desktop it becomes a small card bottom right.

Desktop

2. No invitation at all

No invite attached, invite claimed, invite withdrawn: the same clean page. The earlier line "the invite already used" is gone (decision in the notes).

3. Signed in

A small Sign in sits in the top strip (and in the bar, for someone who has an account but was not signed in). Signed in, the page shows Save to my recipes and never the claim, even when the link carries an invite. A household without Recipes gets neither.
