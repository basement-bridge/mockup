What the person sees

One list, in print order

  • Entry is inside Add: the Scan a receipt tile (RS-73). The camera opens at once.
  • A short live picture with Shopping's boxes. Lines are read screenful by screenful and drop into the list in receipt order (RS-55). Tap a box and its row is found.
  • Every row says what it will do before you act: Updates existing (5 kg to 10 kg) or New item (RS-58). An exact match always tops up the single latest record (RS-56).
  • Swipe right accepts a recommended line without opening it (RS-59). Pick another item and New are always on the line; swipe left is New (RS-60). Desktop: Accept button, Y, P, N, X.
  • States: Exact, Check (three dots), Unsure, Ignored (RS-61 to RS-63). An Unsure line has three choices: Add as new, Match to existing, Ignore.
  • At the top of the list: the filter (All, Check, Unsure, Ignored) and 9 of 14 resolved (RS-64).
  • Discount, bag and non-food lines are listed as Ignored with a reason, never matched, one tap to put back (RS-65).
  • Add N opens the summary; Add writes once. Undo for 9 seconds, then 5 minutes in history (RS-69).

Not decided, proposed

  • New items with no place land under Unplaced, as Add item does with Location left blank (RS-70). Owner has not decided.
  • Exact lines are ready and already counted (RS-71): the receipt opens at "5 of 14 resolved".
  • No price or store field exists in Kitchie, so price is shown to check and never saved (RS-68). Purchase date to use-by is not wired: no such rule exists.

How a name is scored (no AI): the job note, with thresholds and examples: Exact is word for word; Check scores at least 0.45; dots are 3 at 0.85 and up, 2 at 0.65 to 0.84, 1 at 0.45 to 0.64; under 0.45 is Unsure.
