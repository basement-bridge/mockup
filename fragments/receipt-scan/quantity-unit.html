Receipt scan: a quantity with a unit

Revision 3 follows below (RS-92 to RS-97). Revised 15 October 2026 after the owner's live review: Option A is chosen (RS-87); Option B is kept below as the alternative not chosen. Kitchie issue #754, "Receipt scan: quantity is read differently from the shopping list". This page draws the review row of the chosen flow (Receipt order, the receipt scan hub) when a scanned quantity carries a unit. Nothing here is built; the build into Kitchie uat follows the independent design review.

The owner's words

Owner (Arjan), by voice, 14 October 2026, on #754: "when I scan an item, the quantity is not [right]. There are heuristics around quantity recognition based on what's in the free text. That's something we already use in the shopping list. Can we make sure the same technique is used as part of recognising items on a scanned-in receipt?" Decisions recorded on the issue the same day: a scanned quantity may carry a unit, read with the same reader as the shopping list (kilo and kg become one form: 2 litres becomes 2 L); in scope: a unit in the name (MILK 2L); out of scope for now: weight lines (1.234 kg @ $x/kg), whose handling stays as it is. The governance shape (mockup first, then the independent design review) is the next step.

What is drawn, and the rule that keeps it quiet

The rule (Empty is absent)

  • The unit is drawn only on a line whose unit was read from its name (MILK 2L, 2KILO, 500G, 1KG).
  • A number-only line (2 x EGGS), a line with no number, and a weight line keep today's row exactly. No empty "Amount" slot, no "no unit" placeholder, no extra pill.
  • One reader. 2L, 2 LTR, 2 litres all read as 2 L; 2KILO, 2 kilos, 2kg as 2 kg; 500GM as 500 g (the shopping list's words, quantity-words.ts). Weight and volume only: g, kg, mL, L. Pounds and ounces are not household units (RS-74).
  • The receipt never silently replaces what the person or the list said: the row says what it read, the person can change it, nothing is written until Add.

What the sample receipt shows

  • MILK 2L reads as 2 L: Updates existing (1 L to 3 L).
  • BASMATI RICE 2KILO reads as 2 kg: the folded word.
  • 2 x EGGS reads as a count of 2: no unit, as today.
  • GRK YOG 1KG and GV EVOO 1L read in kg and L; the Pantry holds them in g and mL, so the add-up converts (as built).
  • BABY SPINACH has no number: the amount box is blank for the person to type; nothing is counted as 1 (RS-88).
  • KS SMKD SLMN 400G: 400 g is the amount (RS-100). The Pantry counts salmon in packs and cannot add grams to packs, so the row says Kept separate: 400 g cannot join 2 pack (RS-74).
  • ORG BANANAS and its weight line: unchanged.

  • To correct, open the row (where the person already goes): Amount bought keeps the stepper, and its figure becomes the shopping list's box with its unit pill (wantbox, wunit, PR 157). The box is free text, as on the shopping list: tap the × on the pill to take the unit off and the box is your own words again; type 2 litres or 500 ml and the unit is read (and the pill returns) when you leave the box; other words are kept as typed.
  • Desktop: the same, in the lane. Nothing new to learn with the keys.
  • Costs: a wrong read is only seen in passing on a row that is not opened; fixing it is one tap to open, then the box.

A line where the receipt and the shopping list's words differ

This state is not in the Pantry review. The Pantry's bulk add (pantry-receipt.js) never reads the shopping list, so there are no list words to differ from. The only screen that holds both is Shopping's receipt review (receipt-scan.js, quantityFor in receipt-match.js), where a receipt line matches a list line and one box carries the amount. It is drawn here, once, because #754 asks that the receipt never silently replace the list's own words. Phone first; Shopping's review has no separate desktop layout drawn here.

As built today

  • List says 2 litres of milk; receipt says MILK 2L. Today the receipt's unit is not read, so nothing about "2 L" appears; if the receipt had a count (2 x MILK) the box is filled with 2 and the note reads receipt says 2. Only a whole number above 1, or a weight line, replaces the list's words; otherwise the note is from your list.

Proposed with a unit

  • The box starts with the receipt's reading (2 L), as the box is today. The note names both when they differ: receipt says 2 L · your list said 2 litres of milk.
  • When the two are the same after folding (2 litres and 2L are both 2 L) there is no note at all: nothing differs, so nothing is drawn (Empty is absent). The list's words are never overwritten without the note.
  • When the receipt has no amount, the list's words stand and the note is from your list, as today.

Still needs the owner's confirmation

One at a time, each with a recommended position. Nothing below is assumed settled.

  1. Size or amount (RS-83). Answered by the owner (RS-100): a weight such as 400G is an amount, the same as 2L on milk, with no special case for produce or weight lines.
  2. Chat bulk-add (RS-77). Chat says a size such as 5KG is not an amount and asks. That rule shifts if the shared reader starts reading units from names. Recommended: chat keeps asking for the amount when the only quantity is a unit in the name, until the person says it; the screen reads it and shows it.
  3. Blank amount and Add (RS-88). Recommended: a line with a blank amount is not written until the person types one (as chat), so it stays to settle and is not counted in "Add N". The mockup shows the blank box but does not yet hold back Add.
  4. Words the Pantry cannot add. 2 litres of milk is kept as typed. Recommended: as on the shopping list, kept exactly and the row says Updates existing (1 L plus “2 litres of milk”); the Pantry item keeps its exact amount and the words go to history.
  5. Counted Pantry units (RS-82). Tin, pack, bag are plain text beside the box, with no pill. Recommended: yes, as the shopping list's pill is weight and volume only.
  6. Shopping's review (RS-84). Note names both readings when they differ and is silent when equal after folding. Recommended: yes.
  7. The slim header (RS-90). The camera's prompt stays while looking; only the finished-receipt card folds into the line. Recommended: yes.

The write path (read on uat at 0ee6c08)

A unit already reaches the Pantry write today. pantry-receipt.js sends each line as item, name, quantity where the quantity is words (Q.words: a number and a unit, so a weight line goes as "1.2 kg"). POST /items/receipt runs store.pantryReceiptCommit, which cleans the words (cleanShoppingQuantity) and tops up through commitQuantity, the unit-aware add. Reading a unit from the name changes which lines carry a unit, not the shape of the request. A build would touch the reader (receipt-lines.js) and the row (pantry-receipt.js); store.ts needs no edit on this evidence, but the lane stays the owner's call.
