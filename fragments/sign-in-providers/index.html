Sign-in with five providers

Owner, typed, 26 October 2026: mock up the sign-in screen for the built WorkOS and Clerk sign-in (platform #134, merged into uat, issue #128). Updated 26 October 2026 (owner, typed): five providers. Google and Microsoft through WorkOS, Facebook, X and TikTok through Clerk. Apple is dropped: the owner will not pay the $99 a year Apple developer fee; Apple stays behind the port in Platform as a later option and is not drawn on any new frame. No email and password, no phone, minimal data. Mockup only. Nothing built, nothing approved; every decision below is proposed and none is confirmed.

Owner’s phone pick (proposed): option D

D = B’s two-across grid + C’s “Other ways”. First visit shows all five. From the second visit this browser remembers every provider used (browser only, nothing on the server) and those lead, two across; an odd last button is centred at one column width (no filler provider); the rest sit under “Other ways”, always one tap away. A provider whose keys are not set is not shown; if only direct Google is configured the page is today’s single Google button. Each button wears its provider’s official branding. See section 5 (D) first; A, B and C below are kept for comparison and now show five providers and official branding too.

Owner, typed, 26 October 2026 (SP-D9, proposed): if the last row is odd, just centre it and make it the same width as the other buttons. This supersedes the earlier “fill the odd slot with another provider” (SP-D3) and “an odd last one spans the row” rules. Scope: the remembered-providers part and the main grid, and the same inside “Other ways”.

1 · What exists today (read from code, not run, not seen on a screen)

Read on 26 October 2026 from platform uat (b53453f: pages.ts, server.ts, authn-*.ts) and kitchie uat (32f66eb: skin.ts, web-signin.ts, platform-config.ts). The built app has not been checked against this mockup. The two frames are drawn from that code.

  • The six buttons are on Platform’s page, not on Kitchie’s. Kitchie’s screen sends p=google (a fixed value, PLATFORM_PROVIDER) to Platform’s /auth/start. Platform’s own /signin page is only reached by typing its address. So today a person cannot choose Apple or Facebook from Kitchie. This mockup is Kitchie’s screen with the six buttons (SP-O1).
  • Order built: direct Google, then WorkOS (Google, Microsoft, Apple), then Clerk (Facebook, X, TikTok). Proposed: Google, Apple, Facebook, Microsoft, X, TikTok (SP-2).
  • Two “Google” buttons while the direct one stays on: the WorkOS one reads “Google (WorkOS)” (buildAuthn). They are two members (SP-O3).
  • A button is off when its key is not set. WorkOS needs two names, Clerk three; some-but-not-all stops start-up. Nothing else switches either on.
  • Errors: every failure goes back to Kitchie’s own /signin?signin_error=1 (“That sign-in didn’t work. Please try again.”). A signed-in account with no member goes to /not-a-member. There is no page for redirecting or returning: Platform answers with 303s only.
  • Not a member screen says “Use a different Google account”: Google-only wording (SP-10).
  • Own page or vendor page, as built: see section 8.
2 · Option A · one column

Five full-width buttons stacked, glyph and one word each, “Continue with” said once above them. Everything visible, nothing to learn. Cost: the column is about 290px tall on its own, so on a 360 by 640 phone the kitchen shrinks to a strip, and five bars read as a form rather than a welcome.

3 · Option B · two across recommended

Two columns, three rows. Google is first. The tick on a button means “last used on this device” (frame in section 6). On desktop the column stays 30rem wide, centred, with a taller kitchen above it.

4 · Option C · one button, the rest folded in place

One clear action: the first button, full width. “Other ways” opens the other four in place (a plain details, no script, DESIGN.md 6a). Best on the calm and on the words budget. Cost: Facebook, Microsoft, X and TikTok people always pay a second tap on a new phone, and a person who does not see their provider may think it is missing. Tap “Other ways” in the frames.

5 · Option D · remembered pairs, the rest under “Other ways” owner’s pick

The rule, in order. (1) A provider whose keys are not set is not shown at all. (2) First visit (nothing remembered in this browser): all configured providers, two equal columns, an odd last one centred at one column width. (3) From the second visit: every provider this browser has used leads, most recent first, two to a row; no filler provider is added. (4) An odd last button, in the main grid or inside Other ways, is centred at exactly one column width (SP-D9). (5) The rest sit under Other ways, a plain details, always reachable. (6) If only direct Google is configured, the page is today’s single full-width Google button and nothing else (resilience: the build must degrade to that when the others are off or their keys are missing). Memory is a browser-only store (localStorage, key kitchie.signin.providers, an array of provider ids, no names, no account); nothing is sent to or stored on the server.

Returning, 1, 2 and 3 remembered (odd, even, odd). Frames: Facebook remembered (odd: Facebook centred alone, four under Other ways); Facebook and TikTok remembered (even); Microsoft, X and Facebook remembered (odd: the third centred, two under Other ways).

8 · Can Kitchie keep its own look, or does the person land on a vendor page?

Short answer. Kitchie’s own screen is always the first thing seen, and the WorkOS buttons can go straight to the provider with no WorkOS page in between. Two screens that are not Kitchie’s remain: the provider’s own consent screen (Google, Apple, Microsoft, Facebook, X, TikTok), which can never wear Kitchie’s look, and, as built, a Clerk-hosted screen for the three Clerk buttons. Read from the vendors’ docs on 26 October 2026; each claim says where it came from; what could not be fetched is listed at the end.

9 · Decisions (all proposed, none confirmed)
  1. SP-1. Layout B (two across), A and C kept for comparison.
  2. SP-2. Fixed order: Google, Facebook, Microsoft, X, TikTok (Apple dropped, SP-D1). Built order is direct Google, WorkOS (Google, Microsoft, Apple), Clerk (Facebook, X, TikTok); Platform lists adapters by vendor, so this is a small change to its composition list.
  3. SP-3. One accent: Google, or the button last used on this device. A tick shows last used (shape, not colour alone). The order never moves.
  4. SP-4. A glyph and one word per button; “Continue with” once above (a single button reads “Continue with Google”). Words budget: button 1 to 3 words.
  5. SP-5. (Applies to D too.) A provider whose key is not set is absent: not greyed, not explained. One on: one button. None on: the built “Sign-in isn’t available right now”.
  6. SP-6. Redirecting: the pressed button shows “Opening…” and a spinner, the rest dim and stop taking taps. A small script; without it, today’s plain link.
  7. SP-7. Error stays neutral, as built; it never names a vendor or a reason.
  8. SP-8. Back from the vendor: no screen unless the answer takes about a second, then “Checking it’s you” with the dots. Built: nothing drawn.
  9. SP-9. Two buttons, two members: nothing on the main screen; last-used tick; one line on the invite-only screen (N3). N1 and N2 drawn.
  10. SP-10. Invite-only screen: “Use a different Google account” becomes “Try another sign-in”.
  11. SP-11. Desktop is the same centred column (30rem) with a taller kitchen; no side panel on a signed-out screen.
  12. SP-12. Superseded by SP-D4: the glyphs are now each provider’s own mark.
  13. SP-D1. Five providers, Apple dropped (owner will not pay $99 a year); Apple stays behind the port as a later option. The fixed order is Google, Facebook, Microsoft, X, TikTok (SP-2 without Apple).
  14. SP-D2. Option D is the owner’s phone pick: B’s two-across grid plus C’s “Other ways”. First visit shows every configured provider; from the second visit remembered providers lead in pairs, the rest fold.
  15. SP-D3. Superseded by SP-D9. (Odd slot filled by the next provider in fixed order.)
  16. SP-D4. Each button wears its provider’s official mark and colours, light and dark variants (partly verified 26 October: Google button colours, Facebook blue; all mark shapes unverified, section 5b).
  17. SP-D5. Memory is browser-only (localStorage kitchie.signin.providers, an array of ids, written when a button is pressed); nothing server-side. Cost: a press that then fails is also remembered; the alternative is to write after a successful return, which needs a script on the signed-in page. Clearing site data resets the screen to the first-visit layout. SP-O9 is answered by the owner’s rule, still to be confirmed.
  18. SP-D6. Brand-coloured buttons replace the single accent in the provider list; order, not colour, carries last-used.
  19. SP-D7. A vendor that is failing or unavailable: its button is dashed, dimmed, shows an alert shape and does not take taps; a neutral line says the others still work; the fold opens if it is inside. How the app learns a vendor is down (a health check, or the failed return carrying a provider id) is not decided and is not in the built redirects, which carry only signin_error=1.
  20. SP-D8. Resilience: with only direct Google configured the page is today’s single full-width Google button with no grid and no Other ways.
  21. SP-D9 (proposed; supersedes SP-D3 and the “odd last one spans the row” rule of SP-D2/section 5). Owner, typed, 26 October 2026, with a screenshot of D first visit at 390 dark (TikTok full width alone): if the last row is odd, just centre it and make it the same width as the other buttons. The grid keeps two equal columns; an odd last button is horizontally centred at exactly one column width, in the main grid and inside Other ways, in every state. Clarified scope: it applies to the remembered-providers part (an odd remembered set is centred, no filler) and to the main grid. No filler-provider logic remains. Not changed: the single-provider resilience page (SP-D8) and options B and C.
Open questions
  1. SP-O1. Kitchie’s screen has only Google (a fixed p=google). How does it learn which providers are on: a list in Kitchie’s own configuration, or a read from Platform? A wrong guess shows a button that fails, or hides one that works.
  2. SP-O2. Platform’s own /signin page (built in #134) is plain, with seven accent buttons. Only reached by typing the address. Restyle it to match, or leave it plain?
  3. SP-O3. Keep the direct Google button beside the WorkOS one? They are two members. If WorkOS is off the Google button vanishes (frame above).
  4. SP-O4. Clerk’s three buttons all begin at one Clerk screen. Is a second choice there acceptable, or should the button decide?
  5. SP-O5. Clerk custom flow (own buttons, no Clerk page) needs Clerk’s browser SDK and a different hand-off. Worth costing, or accept the Clerk screen?
  6. SP-O6. Accept “continue to workos.com” on Google’s screen (custom domain $99 a month, owner pays nothing)?
  7. SP-O7. Production needs a developer app and review for Facebook, X and TikTok, Apple credentials, and own Google and Microsoft credentials (owner actions in #134). Brand rules for the buttons come with them.
  8. SP-O8. The welcome line and the invite-only footer are unchanged from today. Leave them?
  9. SP-O9. A per-device cookie holding the last-used provider id (no account, no name): allowed?
  10. SP-O10. Does the owner want to see the screen on a real phone, on UAT, once the keys exist? Nothing here proves how WorkOS and Clerk behave.
10 · Design checklist (DESIGN.md 9.8), builder’s own answers

The separate senior-mobile-UX review the repo requires has not been done: this session has no agent to hand it to. These answers are the builder’s and do not count as the review.

  1. Jobs: the job is “get into the kitchen”; the accent button is the one tap most likely right.
  2. Accent: one (Google, or last used).
  3. Presentation: a set of choices is a list of buttons (9.4); state is glyph plus shape (tick, spinner), not colour alone.
  4. Words: buttons one word; one lead word; the existing welcome line kept (SP-O8).
  5. Duplication: nothing repeated; “Google” appears once.
  6. Targets: every button 52px high, 44px summary; checked at 360, 390, 768 and 1280.
  7. Text size: the names ellipsise, buttons stay in the grid; not yet checked at +30%.
  8. Dark: every option drawn in dark.
  9. Warmth: the kitchen stays on screen in B and C; A squeezes it.
Performance (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path: this page, the shared theme, and one small script and stylesheet per frame (frames load when scrolled to). The sign-in screen itself loads no image and no script except the one-screen redirecting state (about 1 KB, SP-6); the provider glyphs are inline SVG in the page, so six buttons cost no requests. Nothing is deferred because nothing here is large. Option C’s fold is a plain details: five buttons are in the page but not shown, no fetch on open.
