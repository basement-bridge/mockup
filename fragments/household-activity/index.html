Household activity: tell other members what changed

Kitchie #110. Phone and desktop. Mockup, nothing built here; Level 1 is already in Kitchie main.

When other people in the household add things or use them up, the member sees a small bell with a number in the header (a rail item on desktop). Tapping it shows who did what in one line per person and burst, then leads to History’s locked “Since you were last here” catch-up. A setting on the device can switch it off. Device notifications (Level 2) are drawn as an idea only. Tap the bell in the live frames to try it.

What the owner said (Kitchie #110, 1 October 2026) and decided
  • HA-D1 Build Level 1 (in-app) only for now; device notifications later (“build to L1”). Level 1 is in Kitchie main (PR #126).
  • HA-D2 Changes with no known person (the shared AI address) notify everybody.
  • HA-D3 Each recipient’s own setting decides whether they see it (“respecting the recipients setting for notification in their browser”). Drawn as a per-device Settings row, on by default.
  • HA-D4 Counted: items added and items used up (“Added and use up”). A plain removal is not.
  • HA-D5 Level 1 as in the issue: others only, one cursor per member, one summary per burst, 24-hour cue, no polling, History stays the durable record.
Choices the mockup made where the owner was silent (Proposal)
  • HA-P1 Phone: bell left of the profile button. Desktop: a rail item above the profile button.
  • HA-P2 Badge from the tokens (accent, on-accent), not the built red.
  • HA-P3 A tap opens a peek first (option B, recommended), then History; option A (straight to History, as built) is kept.
  • HA-P4 One row per person per kind within 30 minutes; up to four rows, then “and n more in History”; counts only, never item names; the shared address is “Someone”.
  • HA-P5 Two cursors: the bell moves when the peek or History opens; the catch-up moves only on its tick (H11); the tick also clears the bell.
  • HA-P6 The cue ends after 24 hours; a busy day shows 9+ and four rows, not a flood.
  • HA-P7 Hidden at zero; stays pressed while the peek is open.
  • HA-P8 Setting on by default, a check row, off hides the bell and (later) pushes on this device.
  • HA-P9 Only household-visible changes count; others’ personal records never do; recipe and plan changes not yet.
  • HA-P10 Desktop: Ctrl/Cmd+Enter closes the peek; no open key.
  • HA-P11 Level 2 card, its states, and a counts-only notification: idea only, not approved.
Open questions (recommended position, and what a wrong guess costs)
  • HA-Q1 What did “default household activity remove” mean? Recommended: the setting is on by default and plain removals are not counted. Cost: a filter line and a default flag.
  • HA-Q2 Can the journal tell “used up” from “removed”? Built Level 1 counts both. Recommended: if it cannot, say “used up or removed” until it can. Cost: wording.
  • HA-Q3 Is “the browser setting” the in-app setting (Level 1) or the browser’s notification permission (Level 2)? Recommended: Level 1 now; a push, if approved, needs both and never prompts before the person taps Turn on.
  • HA-Q4 Should opening the bell mark the catch-up caught up? Recommended: no (H11); the tick clears the bell.
  • HA-Q5 Peek first (B) or straight to History (A)? Recommended: B.
  • HA-Q6 Do recipe and plan changes count? Recommended: not yet.
  • HA-Q7 A desktop key for the bell, or the rail placement? Recommended: rail, no key.
  • HA-Q8 Level 2 (device push) needs the owner’s explicit approval: a VAPID secret, a subscription table, a new outbound path, a public endpoint to protect, iPhone only from the Home Screen (iOS 16.4+), a debounce check first. Recommended: keep it deferred.
  • HA-Q9 Is “Someone” right for the shared AI address? Recommended: yes until personal links are required.
How it fits the locked History decisions

The peek and the notification both lead to the catch-up (H4, H8). The bell’s 24 hours and counts are a different job from the catch-up’s three days with activity (H6). Opening the bell never marks the catch-up caught up (H11). An assistant on a personal link shows as that person (H10). Others’ personal entries stay private (D6, D7).

What loads, and what waits (DESIGN.md section 6)

Default path: the bell markup (about 0.4 KB, hidden at zero) and about 1.5 KB of CSS. The number and rows ride the existing /changes call; no polling. Deferred: the peek is drawn on tap from data already in the page, in place; one small request moves the cursor on open; History loads only if tapped. Level 2, if ever approved, loads nothing until Turn on. Full notes, the build differences and the proposals in detail: docs/knowledge/household-activity.md.
