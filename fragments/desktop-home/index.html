Desktop Home: Dashboard and Compact view

Decided direction (owner, voice, 11 and 12 Oct 2026)

Home on desktop has two swappable views, with one universal search above both. Dashboard is the side-panel layout (the product name; internally “side panel”). Compact view is the ask-first layout. The ideas card's highlighted slot is whatever is planned for today; if nothing is planned it is the top idea. Other ideas always show below. Tune ideas sits inline, collapsed behind a button, never a popup on desktop. Mobile Home stays as it is, except its ideas card borrows this highlight rule. Timeline, ideas-led and the plain dashboard grid are dropped. Sample data; Theme button for light and dark.
