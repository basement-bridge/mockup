Desktop Home, five directions

Proposal / options only. Nothing is decided or built into the real app. Owner ask (voice, 11 October 2026): desktop Home is "one of those underutilized places" today, basically a scaled-up phone screen with a greeting and a couple of tiles. He wants several creative directions that use what we now know about pantry, the cooking ideas pane and its signals, the weekly plan and shopping. Each frame is a 1180px desktop screen on the settled rail layout (the main area is wider than Pantry's 640px column because Home has no list to protect). Sample data only; use the Theme button for light and dark.
