Using things up, four directions

Fragment. The job: finding what will go off and making the most of it. Four ways to look when someone opens it, then what happens when they act on one thing.

Sample data only: spinach, milk, tomatoes, feta and bread. The days left are made up; how freshness is worked out and ranked is not decided here.

Open
  • How freshness is ranked (soonest first, or weighted by how likely it is to be wasted) is open and not shown. The badges here are made-up days.
  • How we know someone is in this job, rather than just browsing the pantry? Open. See the job file.
  • Which direction leads? 4 only appears when something is close, so it could sit above any of the others.
  • What are the ways out for an item: cook it, freeze it, used it, bin it? Is "bin it" shown at all, or does it hide behind a long press?
  • Do ideas come from saved recipes only, or can the AI suggest new ones? Shown: saved only.
  • Does cooking an idea tick the items off as used, or ask first?
  • Should "freeze it" move the item to the freezer and reset its date in one tap?
Performance note (DESIGN.md section 6)
  • Default path loads: this page, the shared theme and the small inline icon set. Emoji stand in for item pictures, so no image downloads.
  • Deferred until engaged: in the real app, the ideas (recipe matching against what is going off) are asked for after the list is on screen, or only when an item is tapped. Item pictures load for visible rows only.
  • Also deferred: freeze, used and bin actions need nothing extra until pressed. Other themes are separate files fetched on choice (theme.js).
  • Why reasonable now: the first thing wanted is the short list of what is close; matching ideas is the slower part, so it follows rather than blocks.
