What this option does

  • Two places, one per scope. Mine holds what you chose. Household holds what the kitchen chose. A value is never edited in the wrong place.
  • Mine shows the value in force and where it comes from. The sources are App, then Household, then You (D16), and a chip on each row says which one is in force. Open a row and its first choice reads Same as household (value), or Same as app default (value) when the household has set nothing, with the real value in brackets. A line under it shows the chain. Picking that choice drops your own value (the code clears it, so the next level applies).
  • Stock level checks (renamed, D17) ask whether the level Kitchie has recorded is still right; they are not about the amount. The group holds the switch, quiet hours, the daily limit, level and how often by place (D21), and the switched-off list. There is no time zone row (D18) and no separate “How proactive” dial.
  • Household is managed on its own tab, by household admins. An admin sees the rows as editors. A member who is not an admin sees the same rows read-only, with one line: Only household admins can change this. (D25 to D28; this replaces D5, which let any member edit.) Everything on Mine stays editable by everyone (D29).
  • Members and admins. A small view under Household. The founding member (the person who set the household up) is an admin by default and can make another member an admin, or take it back, one person at a time (D26, D27).
  • Things with one scope say so. Household name, categories, locations and Recipe stars and votes are only the household's, with no personal version to override, so they are on the Household tab only and not on Mine. Kitchen role and the assistant moved out of Preferences into your profile (D24); the sketch at the end of this page shows both.
  • Notes and food are added together, not swapped. Your note (chip You) and the household note (chip Household) sit side by side and are both read (D23). Your food statements and the household's are both used.
