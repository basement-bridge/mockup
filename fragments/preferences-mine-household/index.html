Mine and Household, two sections

One Preferences screen with two tabs. Mine is about you: every row shows the value in force and where it comes from. Household is the kitchen's own list, managed there and nowhere else, and any member can change it.

What this option does

  • Two places, one per scope. Mine holds what you chose. Household holds what the kitchen chose. A value is never edited in the wrong place.
  • Mine shows the value in force and where it comes from. A chip on each row says Yours, Household or Built-in. An overridden row also says what the household has. Pick Same as household to drop your own choice (the code clears the value, so the next level applies).
  • Household is managed on its own tab. Any member sees the same rows as editors (D5): there is no owner and no read-only list. A tenure-based gate was deliberately deferred by the owner, so none is drawn.
  • Things with one scope say so. My assistant and kitchen role are only yours. Household name, categories, locations and Recipe stars and votes are only the household's, with no personal version to override.
  • Notes and food are added together, not swapped. Your note and the household note are both read. Your food statements and the household's are both used.

Good for, and costs

Good for: "whose is this?" is answered by where you are, not by reading a chip. The household's rules can be looked at, and changed by any member, without any of your own choices in the way.

Costs: to see a household default while you are on Mine, you follow the link under the row or switch tab. Two tabs mean one more tap than a single list. The household tab repeats the five stock rows.

The owner explicitly deferred any earn-your-way, tenure-based gating to a later decision; deferred, not forgotten, and not built in. (Settles the owner-in-platform-mode question: there is no owner.)
  • D6 A member may set quiet hours to none for themselves, over the household's. No restriction.
  • D7 Most suggestions per day is 1 to 20, default 2, in the web form and in chat. (Settles the 0 to 20 against 1 to 10 question.)
  • D8 Household food statements are open to any member, as D5. Person-level statements stay unrestricted. (Settles the household-food-needs-an-owner question.)

Open questions

  • Recipe stars and votes are household-wide. There is no per-person star. If you want one, that is a change in Recipe's data (the token already says who is asking). A wrong guess costs a schema change.
  • No lock. A household value is a default; a member can always override it. Is there anything the household should be able to fix for everyone? (Quiet hours are decided: a member may set none for themselves, D6.)
  • Where it lives: as Settings > Preferences next to Stock checks, or replacing Stock checks.

Proposals Proposal

The owner did not say these. A wrong guess costs a redraw.

  • Two tabs, Mine first.
  • Rows save the moment you choose (no Save button), like the item fields. Kitchie's page today has Save buttons.
  • The chip words Yours, Household, Built-in, Only you, Everyone, Household only, This device.
  • "Founding member" (the People mockup's word). Superseded by D5: this screen has no owner, so it does not use the word.
  • A web list of food statements with "Stop remembering this". Today there is no web page for them; they are said to the assistant.
  • The household's value is never shown as a count of who overrides it: the code gives no way to ask, and another member's choices stay theirs.
