Mine and Household, two sections

One Preferences screen with two tabs. Mine is about you: every row shows the value in force and where it comes from. Household is the kitchen's own list, managed there and nowhere else, and only household admins can change it.

What this option does

  • Two places, one per scope. Mine holds what you chose. Household holds what the kitchen chose. A value is never edited in the wrong place.
  • Mine shows the value in force and where it comes from. A chip on each row says Yours, Household or Built-in. An overridden row also says what the household has. Pick Same as household to drop your own choice (the code clears the value, so the next level applies).
  • Household is managed on its own tab, by household admins. An admin sees the rows as editors. A member who is not an admin sees the same rows read-only, with one line: Only household admins can change this. (D25 to D28; this replaces D5, which let any member edit.) Everything on Mine stays editable by everyone (D29).
  • Members and admins. A small view under Household. The founding member (the person who set the household up) is an admin by default and can make another member an admin, or take it back, one person at a time (D26, D27).
  • Things with one scope say so. My assistant and kitchen role are only yours. Household name, categories, locations and Recipe stars and votes are only the household's, with no personal version to override.
  • Notes and food are added together, not swapped. Your note and the household note are both read. Your food statements and the household's are both used.

Good for, and costs

Good for: "whose is this?" is answered by where you are, not by reading a chip. The household's rules can be looked at by everyone, and changed by admins, without any of your own choices in the way.

Costs: to see a household default while you are on Mine, you follow the link under the row or switch tab. Two tabs mean one more tap than a single list. The household tab repeats the five stock rows.

Food rules Owner, voice, 10 Oct 2026

Decided as food F1 to F10 in docs/knowledge/user-preferences/food-preferences.md. What the Food card now draws:

  • Avoid is for good and a hard exclusion (no end date). "Never suggest" is gone as a separate kind; it was the same thing.
  • Likes and dislikes are soft weighting. They may end on their own ("for the next two weeks", then neutral again); with no end they stand. The time comes from what was said, so the screen has no date field, only a Keep it for good button.
  • Limit caps how often something may be suggested ("at most twice a week"), checked against recent meals.
  • A category (Spicy) holds the rule, what the person said, and recipes attached by reference. Titles are read from Recipe each time. The assistant creates it, notices recipes that look like a match and offers them: open Spicy to see it.
  • Ingredient level (peanuts) and category level are not headed on the screen; a row is just a rule.
  • Substitutions replace what used to be Usually (food F11 to F14). Open questions

  • Admin rights. Do admins have exactly the founding member's rights, or does the founding member keep some alone (granting and revoking admin, removing members)? The mockup gives grant and revoke to the founding member only.
  • The last admin. Can the last admin be removed or step down? What if the founding member leaves?
  • What a member sees. Read-only rows with Only household admins can change this is the reading drawn. Hidden rows would be the alternative.
  • Who can see who is admin. Drawn: every member. The alternative is admins only.
  • Values already set. What happens to household values that exist when roles arrive? Drawn: they stay as they are, now changed by admins only.
  • Quiet hours default. Drawn as admin only, as the owner listed it with the household-level things; confirm it needs admin.
  • Recipe stars and votes are household-wide. There is no per-person star. If you want one, that is a change in Recipe's data (the token already says who is asking). A wrong guess costs a schema change.
  • No lock. A household value is a default; a member can always override it. Is there anything the household should be able to fix for everyone? (Quiet hours are decided: a member may set none for themselves, D6.)
  • Where it lives: as Settings > Preferences next to Stock checks, or replacing Stock checks.
