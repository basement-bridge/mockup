Recipe: browse flyout to the full page

Fragment, mockup only, nothing is built. Owner (Arjan), voice, 12 October 2026. A recipe row on Recipes, or a recipe in a Home search result, opens a browse flyout in the right lane (job: casual browsing). From there Open full recipe takes over the whole recipe area as a page. On that page the notes and reactions flyout can be opened again. Sample data. Use the Theme button for light and dark. All frames are the 1224px desktop group: rail 88, main column 640 (736 with its gutters), right lane 400.

Decisions recorded (owner, voice, 12 Oct 2026)
  • RB-D1 The browse flyout is for casual browsing. Social proof leads: times cooked, last cooked, a thumb up or down per household member, and the notes. Ingredients follow, with All in or N missing.
  • RB-D2 Two primary actions: Cook now and Plan. Open full recipe is a secondary link.
  • RB-D3 Open full recipe is a full-page view that takes over the whole recipe lane (the main area). The owner is fine with a full takeover. Entered with a smooth transition, no jarring reload.
  • RB-D4 On the full page the notes and reactions flyout can be reached again: right-lane position, the same flyout conventions (one flyout at a time, Ctrl/Cmd Esc closes). How it is re-opened, and how the page lays out its top, are shown as options below.
  • RB-D5 Mobile has no flyouts. A mobile row opens the full page.

This replaces the flyout confirmed on 12 Oct in Recipes: the library (see Superseded at the foot): that one led with ingredients and had one primary button.

1. Browse flyout: two visual options

Both put social proof first and ingredients second, and both end in the same two primary buttons. They differ in how the proof reads. Option A is a stacked report: counts, then one row per member, then notes. Option B is a scoreboard of three tiles, then each person’s thumb and note merged into one voice card, with ingredients folded into chips so the voices keep the room. The same flyout opens from a Home search result (Home keeps its own lane rules; the content is identical).

A Stacked report Proposal: lead option, thumbs and notes are separate blocks

B Scoreboard and voices Proposal: thumb and note are one card per person

States both options must cover (flyout only, 400px). Never cooked: no counts, nothing to show, so the proof area says so and the three people are listed as not rated. N missing: the tag changes and the missing ingredients are marked in words, not colour alone.

2. Open full recipe: the transition

Same document, no reload (Proposal: the build uses the browser’s View Transitions API where it exists, and a plain 150 ms cross-fade where it does not). The browse flyout has already fetched the header, counts, reactions and ingredients, so the page paints those straight away and only the steps and photo load after. The URL changes to the recipe; Back returns to the list with the same row selected and the flyout open again.

  1. 0 ms. Click, or O. Focus moves to the page title.
  2. 0 to 140 ms. The browse flyout closes (one flyout at a time; it is remembered so Back reopens it). It slides 24px right and fades.
  3. 0 to 260 ms. The list column fades to nothing while the page fades in and rises 8px, parts staggered by 40 ms. The rail does not move. The recipe emoji tile and title are shared elements that glide from the row and flyout header into the page header.
  4. Reduced motion. No movement, instant swap. Back (button, Alt ←, or browser Back) plays the same in reverse and restores scroll and the selected row.

3. The full page: how notes are re-opened, and how the top is laid out

The page takes over the main area. Notes and reactions open in the right lane, the same 400px flyout as everywhere. Opening the browse flyout is not possible on this page (it was closed on the way in); there is exactly one flyout slot and notes use it. Two ways to re-open it and two layouts for the top are drawn; they combine in any pairing (use the switches above).

Page 1 Proof band, then actions with re-open A: a button in the page header

Re-open A. A persistent “Notes and reactions” button in the page header carries the thumb counts and note count, so you see the verdict without opening anything. It is a toggle (pressed while open) and has the key N. Always findable, uses header space. Page 1. Reading order: name, Cook now and Plan, a cook-history band (seven dots, last one marked; a pill per member with their thumb), ingredients, steps. One 640px column, left-anchored. Opening notes fills the free lane and the page does not move.

Page 2 Hero and two columns with re-open B: a docked handle on the right edge

Re-open B. A tab docked to the right edge of the page, with the thumbs and a note count on it. It costs the header nothing and is always at the edge where the flyout will appear; when notes are open the tab rides the flyout’s left edge and its arrow closes it. Easier to miss than a labelled button, and it takes a strip of the lane. Page 2. A hero card up top (picture slot, the three stat tiles, member pills, Cook now and Plan on the right), then ingredients and steps side by side, so a cook sees both without scrolling. Uses the whole main area (about 1136px); with notes open the page gives way and narrows to the 736px column, so the flyout pushes and never covers.

4. Mobile

No flyouts on mobile (RB-D5). A row opens the full page, pushed in from the right (native feel, 240 ms, edge swipe to go back). The top follows Page 1 so Cook now and Plan are in thumb reach. Notes and reactions are a row that opens a section on the page itself, not a sheet (Proposal; the owner only said no flyouts).

Notes: what the default path loads, what is deferred
  • Default path (this fragment). theme.css, theme.js (the two default themes only), one small styles.css and a short app.js for the prototype. No images, no fonts, no requests on load. The recipe emoji stand in for photos.
  • Recipes list in the build. Rows already carry readiness, cooked count and last cooked, so the list needs no extra call. The browse flyout fetches its own detail on engagement (a row click, or a Home search-result tap), one request for reactions, the first notes and ingredient stock. Intent-based prefetch on row hover or focus is a Proposal (DESIGN.md section 6).
  • Full page. Header, counts, reactions and ingredients reuse what the flyout fetched. Steps and photo are deferred until Open full recipe. The notes flyout loads the full notes list on first open only, and never before.
  • Deferred in this fragment. Edit, versions, photos, cook mode, the Plan day picker, empty search, more than five members, widths under 1224px, and the Home search-result entry (same flyout, not redrawn).
