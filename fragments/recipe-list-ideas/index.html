Recipes: ideas and list on one screen

Fragment. The owner saw two different ideas in one screen: “what to cook” (Cooking ideas) and “everything I have” (the list). Two ways to fix it, each at phone and desktop width. Sample data only. Use the theme button for light and dark.

Option A (recommended): one screen, one list

Recipes stays the place for the whole collection. The pane stays on top but is plainly a suggestion; the list below says what it is (“All recipes”, with a count) and uses the same words as the pane (“All in” everywhere, never “ready” in one place and “not a great fit” in another). Search or any chip folds the pane away, so a filtered list is just a list.

Option B: Ideas is its own view

A two-way switch at the top: Ideas (tonight, then more ideas) and Recipes (the plain list). Cleanest split, but one more tap to browse and ideas are no longer the first thing on the screen.
