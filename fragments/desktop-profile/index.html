Option A: Account card

The phone Profile page, widened into one small centred card. Settings, History, Me and Household drill in and back out.

Pros
The page the owner already knows; smallest build; one column.
Cons
Settings and Household feel cramped in 440px; every visit is drill in, drill out.
If we guess wrong
Low cost. One component, and the panes are reused if we move to B or C.

Option B: Two-pane settings dialog

One big window: sections down the left, the page on the right. Nothing drills in; Settings and Household really render.

Pros
Settings, History and Household are one click apart and have room; grows without a redesign.
Cons
Heaviest build; a lot of window to reach one action like Download CSV; furthest from the phone.
If we guess wrong
Highest cost. New layout, nav and focus handling; cheap to back out only before it is built.

Option C: Menu, then modal

The avatar opens a small menu beside the rail. Settings, History, Household and Me open a full modal over it; Download CSV just runs.

Pros
Quick actions need no window; a modal appears only for real work; the menu doubles as the shortcut cheat sheet.
Cons
Two layers to build and learn; the menu is not screen-blocking, so it bends the owner's rule.
If we guess wrong
Medium cost. The menu is cheap, but two patterns must be kept in step.

Notes for the owner

What you asked for (owner, 9 October 2026): the phone Profile page on desktop, with the dialogs as screen-blocking windows that block everything behind them, and a few mockup ideas to choose from. The avatar sits at the bottom of the left rail. Open it from the rail, or press G then P.

True modal in all three. The scrim covers the whole window including the rail, the page behind is inert (no clicking, no tabbing into it), focus is trapped and returned to where it was, Esc closes, a click on the scrim closes. In C the menu is the only thing that is not a modal.

Shortcuts. Every entry shows its key: G P profile, G M me, G S settings, G Y history, G H household, G D download inventory CSV. Press ? for the list. They work from the page and inside the open window.

Proposals (owner silent). The letters and the 1.5 second window after G. Window sizes: A 440px, B 900 by 620px, C 560px. Esc closes the whole window, not one level. In B, Me is the Profile pane and People is named Household (as on the phone). Sample data only: nothing is saved, downloaded or sent. The theme swatches in Settings are the real shared ones.

Open questions. Is a non-blocking menu acceptable in C, given the rule that dialogs block everything? Should Esc in A step back one level before closing? Should Download CSV also be reachable without opening anything (C does this)? A wrong guess on the first costs a rebuild of the entry point only.

Loads. Default path: shared theme tokens, this page's one CSS and one JS file, no images, no libraries. Window contents are built only when a window is opened; Settings, History and Household panes are drawn on first view.
