Notes for the owner

What you asked for (owner, 9 October 2026): the phone Profile page on desktop, as a screen-blocking window that blocks everything behind it. Decided the same day: the two-pane, settings-style window (sections down the left, the page on the right). Options A (account card) and C (menu, then modal) are removed. The avatar sits at the bottom of the left rail. Open it from the rail, or press G then P.

True modal. The scrim covers the whole window including the rail, the page behind is inert (no clicking, no tabbing into it), focus is trapped and returned to the opener, Ctrl or Cmd Enter, the × button and a click on the scrim close it.

Shortcuts. G P profile, G M me, G S settings, G Y history, G H household, G D my data. Press ? for the list. They work from the page and inside the open window. ↑ ↓ switch section when the section list has focus.

Proposals (owner silent). The letters and the 1.5 second window after G. Window size 900 by 620px. Me is the Profile section and People is named Household (as on the phone). Sample data only: nothing is saved, downloaded or sent. The theme swatches in Settings are the real shared ones.

Reuse. Any page can load profile.css and profile.js and call KProfile.open(opener) and KProfile.close(). opener is the element that gets focus back. This page also opens it at load with ?open=1 (or me, settings, history, household, data, shortcuts); the Pantry behind it is page.css and exists only here.

Open question. Esc closes the whole window, not one level. A wrong guess costs one key handler.

Loads. Default path: shared theme tokens, this page's CSS and one JS file, no images, no libraries. The window is built only when opened; its Settings, History and Household sections are drawn on first view.
