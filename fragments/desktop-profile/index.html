Notes for the owner

What you asked for (owner, 9 October 2026): the phone Profile page on desktop, as a screen-blocking window that blocks everything behind it. Decided the same day: the two-pane, settings-style window (sections down the left, the page on the right). Options A (account card) and C (menu, then modal) are removed. The avatar sits at the bottom of the left rail. Open it from the rail, or press G then P.

True modal. The scrim covers the whole window including the rail, the page behind is inert (no clicking, no tabbing into it), focus is trapped and returned to the opener, Ctrl Esc (Cmd Esc on a Mac, the same close key as everywhere on desktop), the × button (its tooltip says the key) and a click on the scrim close it. Plain Esc closes nothing. Ctrl or Cmd Enter still works as a silent alias, as in the built app, and is not shown anywhere.

Shortcuts. With the window open, 1 to 5 jump to a section (the footer says so): 1 Profile, 2 Settings, 3 Preferences, 4 Household, 5 My data (round 4, 11 October 2026: History left the list, so Household and My data moved up one; the built window still numbers 1 to 6). On the page, with no window open, G P opens the profile and G then M, S, H or D opens a section; Preferences has no letter. Press ? for the list. ↑ ↓ switch section when the section list has focus.

Preferences (owner, 11 October 2026). Preferences is its own section, in the gutter right after Settings, so the numbers run 1 to 6. It opens inside this window, including the /settings/preferences address; on desktop there is no separate full-page layout. The section shows the Preferences mockup (Option B) in a frame, loaded only the first time the section is shown. Phone and tablet keep Settings > Kitchen > Preferences. Plan the week is off the Settings > Kitchen list; it is still reached from Plan in the rail. Proposal: the number keys, because the built window already uses them; the old G-then-letter pairs stay for the page. The Settings section here still lists Stock checks and Categories under Kitchen; whether Stock checks now points to Preferences is open (owner silent).

Proposals (owner silent). The letters and the 1.5 second window after G. Me is the Profile section and People is named Household (as on the phone). Sample data only: nothing is saved, downloaded or sent. The theme swatches in Settings are the real shared ones.

Reuse. Any page can load profile.css and profile.js and call KProfile.open(opener) and KProfile.close(). opener is the element that gets focus back. This page also opens it at load with ?open=1 (or me, settings, preferences, history, household, data, shortcuts); the Pantry behind it is page.css and exists only here.

Full screen (owner, 11 October 2026, by voice). The window fills the whole screen, with the same 244px section list on the left; the translucent backdrop stays and shows as a 12px buffer around the edges (8px under 600px wide). The page content is capped at 60rem and sits left, so a wide screen is not one long line. Under 900px the section list becomes one scrolling strip across the top, the footer hints are left out (no keys on a touch screen), and the window still fills the screen. The built app draws the window only at 64rem and up; phone and tablet keep the full pages. The shortcuts list stays a small centred dialog on top. Inner scrolling: the section list, header and footer stay put, the page scrolls. Focus trap, inert page behind and focus return are unchanged. Notes: docs/knowledge/settings-window.md (SW-1 to SW-12).
