Planning the week, AI-led

Fragment. One phone, several states, all live: tap, drag a meal to another day, block a night. Sample data only. Nothing here is decided; every choice not spoken by the owner is marked Proposal below.

Owner's words (9 October 2026, voice)
  • Start phased: the first state is nothing, with a note saying speak to your AI assistant to lock something in and it shows up here. The note stays in every slot that is still empty.
  • Copy last week is a button on the screen and also something the assistant offers as an opening move.
  • Meals are dragged between days. Days can be blocked (night out); a Settings stencil sets the usual days, the grid overrides one week, with an offer to make it every week.
  • Locking a meal ties it to a recipe: what is on hand gets reserved (a soft label, not stock maths); what is missing is offered for the shopping list, and the same moment is where the assistant can be asked for a substitute.
  • A day holds several meal cards (one person on leftovers, another cooking). A whole-household meal is sized by the assistant for everyone; otherwise cards stack, told apart by avatar. Competing claims on one ingredient are made visible, not auto-resolved.
  • Nobody edits another person's plan. Anyone can view anyone's, or focus on their own. Blocking is per person.
  • A meal card shows what is missing at a glance, like "uses spinach" today.
Proposals (owner silent)
  • Week shown Monday to Sunday, dinner only (as in the four-direction mockup).
  • Everyone / Mine switch at the top of the grid; Mine is where empty prompts and blocking apply.
  • Competing claim shown as an orange "also Thu, Jane" tag on the on-hand ingredient, not a warning dialog.
  • Reserve is one tap for all on-hand ingredients of a meal, with per-ingredient undo left out.
  • Blocked days still show others' cards in Everyone view, with a small "Sam is out" mark.
  • Dropping a meal on a blocked day is refused with a short message; dropping on a day that already has a card stacks it.
  • Touch has no hover, so "Move to another day" also sits in the meal sheet for people who cannot drag.
  • Settings stencil: seven day buttons per person, "Usual days off cooking".
Open questions (and what a wrong guess costs: a redraw, nothing built)
  • Is "leftovers" its own kind of card (no recipe, no reservation) or a normal card? Drawn as a normal card with "Leftovers" in the name.
  • When a reserved meal is moved to another day, does the reservation move with it? Drawn: yes.
  • When someone else's meal uses an ingredient you reserved, who is told? Drawn: nobody is blocked; both cards show the tag.
  • Does a stencil day ever suppress a card you plant there by hand? Drawn: no, you are asked.
  • Should a whole-household card follow household size changes automatically?
