Planning the week, four directions

Fragment. The job: planning meals for a week. Four ways to start and then what the person sees once a week is taking shape.

Sample data only: five recipes from an imagined household. Which days count as the week, who is eating and how the shopping gap shows are not decided here.

Open
  • Which direction leads? 1 is manual, 2 and 4 hand the thinking to the AI, 3 splits choosing from scheduling.
  • Dinner only, or breakfast and lunch too? Every phone here shows dinner only.
  • Does the week start on Monday, or today and the next seven days?
  • Should the plan say what is missing from the pantry, or leave that to the shopping job? Shown only as a small "uses spinach" hint here.
  • Can a plan be saved and reused as a template, for example "busy week"?
  • Leftovers and cooking once for two nights: do they get their own slot?
  • Do saved recipes only count, or can the AI suggest new ones? Shown: saved only.
Performance note (DESIGN.md section 6)
  • Default path loads: this page, the shared theme and a small inline icon set. Emoji stand in for recipe pictures, so no image downloads.
  • Deferred until engaged: in the real app, recipe pictures load only for the meals on screen, and the recipe list behind "Add dinner" or "Pick what sounds good" is fetched when the person opens it, not on page load.
  • Also deferred: pantry matching ("uses spinach") is asked for after the week appears, so an empty week is instant.
  • Why reasonable now: most visits open an empty or existing week; searching the whole recipe collection is only needed when someone starts to fill a night.
