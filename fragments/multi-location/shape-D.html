Flaws in this option

  • Bowl is a lie by omission. The tracked amount is the cupboard bag; the bowl is prose. "Is there sugar on the bench?" cannot be answered by anything but reading the note.
  • Only two tiers. Sugar bowl + bag + sack: one lives in prose, so the note must be maintained by hand and goes stale ("refill from cupboard bag").
  • Second member finds the row under Pantry; the note might say kettle, but Counter area search and the Counter list never show it. This is the exact cost the owner may accept; it is real for anyone not the person who wrote the note.
  • Beef is well served: two amounts, two use-bys, thaw is a transfer between backup and main. Backup use by is a third field to design.
  • Level, swipe, Amount tile unchanged; level reads the working amount only. "Don't buy" is a rule: warn if backup > 0 and working is low.
  • Backup-only data on every item is dead weight for the 95% of items that have one place; fields must hide when empty (an "Add backup" affordance).
  • Wins: no new list, no rank, smallest mental model; tool change is three optional fields.
