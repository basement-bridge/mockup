Flaws in this option

  • Which is primary? The user must choose one at setup. Sugar: bowl or bag? Costco sauce 2 kitchen / 6 garage is clear; beef is not (the freezer is where most of it sits and where the shopper looks).
  • Three tiers need a rank, not a flag. The "refill order" list is a new concept a household must learn.
  • Primary empty, overflow full. Level says Out while 11 kg exists; the list row must override to "Refill". Easy to get subtly wrong in recipe matching: "can I cook now" must decide whether backup counts.
  • Backup is invisible to Pantry grouping and use-by sort. The freezer beef (14 Jan) never sorts as "eat first"; a Freezer area listing omits it unless overflow rows are also shown there, which re-creates duplicate rows.
  • Unit mismatch hidden, not solved: bowl in cups, bag in kg; "11.2 kg backup" on the chip only works because backup is not summed with the primary.
  • Second member finds the item at the primary place and sees the chip; finds the right container. Searching the garage gets a backup hit, good.
  • Wins: swipes, stepper, Amount tile, level all keep today's meaning. Gives the thaw/refill verb for free. Smallest tool change of B and C.
