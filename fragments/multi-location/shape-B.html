Flaws in this option

  • Row lies about where. A single row cannot say where to look; chips fix it but crowd the row at 390px with 3+ placements.
  • Amount tile becomes a list. The existing Amount tile (#288) and stepper cannot stay: edit target is ambiguous, two levels of tap to change a number.
  • Level from the sum hides the shortage that matters: 6 lb of beef is "Plenty" while only 1 lb is thawed and dinner needs thawing tonight. Sugar bowl empty, still Plenty.
  • Mixed units (cup vs kg) make the total a made-up number. Total as hero is wrong for sugar.
  • Swipe has no good answer: ask (slow), earliest use-by (wrong for sugar), first entry (arbitrary).
  • Pantry grouping by area and Use by sort: item is either under one area (hides freezer stock from a Freezer search) or in several (duplicate rows by another name).
  • Wins: true total is one tile; "don't buy" and recipe matching read the sum cleanly; no duplicate records. Heaviest migration of the four.
