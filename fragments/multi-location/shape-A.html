Flaws in this option

  • False alarm. Sugar bowl shows Running low and an accent pill while 11 kg is in the house. Same for the 1 lb thawed beef, which is correct as "one meal left" but looks like a shortage next to 5 lb frozen.
  • No transfer. Thawing is two unlinked edits; cooking 1 lb of the 3 meals is fine, restocking the fridge row from the freezer is not a move. Use by splits cleanly, which is the one thing A does well.
  • Swipe is honest but myopic: it reduces exactly the row you swiped. A person swiping the sugar sack when the bowl is empty gets a wrong total.
  • Recipe matching and "don't buy" need name-grouping that only shopping has. Cook-now and chat see 3 sugars. Chat "I put sugar in the garage" silently creates a 4th row.
  • Second member searching "sugar" gets 3 equal hits and no signal which is the everyday one. Best everyday-location story of all four, since each row names its place.
  • Duplicate-looking records is the thing the owner wants to avoid; A is the problem restated. Only a read-only hint (shown) is possible without schema change.
