Flaws in this option

  • Forgetting to link is silent. Unlinked rows behave exactly like today: a false Running low and a wrong purchase. The add-time prompt helps only for items added after this exists.
  • "Any row will do" is false. Beef, thawed chicken and an opened jar differ in use-by and state. The drain order helps, but cook and swipe act on one record today, so a server-side consume is needed.
  • Shopping groups by name, not by link. This is a code change, not a screen change. Until shopping and the low filter read the link, no mockup above is true.
  • Two truths on one screen. A row can be Low while the total is fine. The rule above keeps the words and colours apart, but people may still read a muted "Low here" as a bug.
  • Mixed units and level-only rows have no number, so the total is a list. Minimums on a group need a unit; per-row minimums conflict.
  • Thaw and decant are manual. No transfer operation carries amount, use-by and link in one step.
  • Wrong links look confident. A suggested pair like caster and brown sugar totals as one number.
  • Prompt fatigue. Every add of a known name asks a question. It must be one line, once per pair.
  • Wins: unlink loses nothing, each record keeps its own use-by, place and unit, housemates' private stock stays out of the shared total, and no existing item ids change.
