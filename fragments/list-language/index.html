One list language: Pantry, Recipes and Home, four ways

Phone only. Draft for the owner to judge. Mockup, nothing built in Kitchie.

The owner is not comfortable with how Pantry looks on a phone today: in his screenshot the rows are plain, a big bold name over a grey line, a red flag, a chevron, a floating copy button over the rows. This page draws four different directions for that list, and the same four on the Recipes list, so each can be judged on both. Inside one variant Pantry and Recipes use the same list language: the same controls for sort, filter, group, search, add and pick several, the same row anatomy, the same chips and flags. Home is a curated view and is allowed to lay itself out differently; it shares the colours, type and components.

Recommendation: A, Dense and sticky

Use A for Pantry and Recipes. Reasons, in the order they matter:

  1. It honours the space rule. The list screen's room is precious (owner). A spends 97px above the list (B and C: 144px, D: 138px) and fits 11 rows on a 390 x 844 screen (D 11, C 8, B 6). Today's row is 68px (.rowx in the household flow's styles), about 9 on the same screen by arithmetic.
  2. It improves the look without changing what the screen is. The screenshot's rows are plain. A keeps the row's anatomy, adds a tinted flag ("Past use by 1 day") and the three-square level where the number was missing, and drops the repeated group name from the grey line. The eye now lands on what needs attention.
  3. Sort is one tap from the list. The sort pill sits in the same sticky head as Filters, so the sort drive is the same on both screens and nothing is hidden in a sheet (C hides it; D uses the column heads, which is new for a phone).
  4. Recipes keep their pictures. A shows the 40px banner where a recipe has one (locked: no banner, no tile), plus Starred, Freezes and versions. D cannot give a recipe a real picture; B can, but at 6 rows a screen.
  5. Lowest build risk. Same markup and class names as the row today, no new gesture, swipe and the item sheet untouched. Mockup and build stay close (AGENTS.md).

What A costs: it is the least "pretty" of the four. If it still feels plain, C is the runner-up (calm sections, but one extra row and sort only in the sheet). Home is a separate choice: B's Home (tonight's idea as one hero card, two sideways strips) is the one place a card earns its space, and Home may drift from the lists, so you can take A's lists with B's Home. A's own Home is the denser, consistent one.

Assumptions

  • "Similar sort drive" means the same controls, positions and behaviour for sort, filter, group, search, add and pick several on Pantry and Recipes, not the same sort keys. Pantry sorts by Name, Use by, Added. Recipes sorts by Best match, Name, Time, Last cooked.
  • Mobile only. Drawn at 390 and checked at 360. Tablet, desktop, the desktop flyouts and the household flow are untouched.
  • Sample rows follow the screenshot (Spices++ and Veggies, "100 g · Spice bin", "Level: plenty", "200 g, old" with "Past use by 1 day"). The names and the third category ("Condiments", the screenshot cut it at "C...") are invented.
  • Unchanged and not redrawn: swipe on rows (left: use one or used up; right: add to the shopping list; on Recipes right: plan it), tapping a row (item sheet, recipe page), the shallow pull to search, receipt scan inside Add, the Out-items-hidden rule, the Custom view strip, the three-square level.
  • Home keeps today's three tiles (On the way out, Cooking ideas for you, Running low). The cooking-ideas pane stays on Home, not Recipes (locked 11 to 12 Oct); the lead button is "Plan" with "Make it" beside it.

Proposals (the owner has been silent; none approved)

  • LL-P1 Recipes gets the same top row as Pantry: Filters at the left, the assistant icon where Pantry has +, the magnifier at the far right. The always-open Recipes search field of 10 Oct (R-D4) becomes the same magnifier. Wrong guess: one more tap to search recipes; the fix is a per-screen exception.
  • LL-P2 Recipes gets a Filters sheet with Sort (Best match, Name, Time, Last cooked) and Show (Ready now, Starred, Freezes, Has versions). Best match is the locked ranking (ready first, then cooked most recently). Any other sort, or a Show filter, makes a Custom view as on Pantry.
  • LL-P3 Recipes stays one ranked list by default (locked). A button in the slot Pantry uses for Location or Category can group it by readiness (Ready now, One or two missing, Needs shopping), the same order with headers. Only C opens on that, because headers are its point.
  • LL-P4 The grey line drops the group the list is grouped by, so Spices++ is not repeated under Spices++. It shows the category when grouped by location, and in a Custom view.
  • LL-P5 C offers Use by as a third grouping: Past use by, Use soon (up to 3 days), Later, No date. A and D keep Use by as a sort only.
  • LL-P6 The chevron goes in A and D (the whole row is the target). B and C keep it.
  • LL-P7 Picking several: each variant has its own way in (above). While picking, a bar takes the tab bar's place: Add to list, Copy, Used up on Pantry; Plan, Add missing, Copy on Recipes. The Shopping badge is hidden meanwhile.
  • LL-P8 One flag component with four tones: danger (past use by, missing ingredients, as today's red "missing"), accent (use soon, running low, starred), sage (all in), muted (information). Wording: "Past use by 1 day", "Use tomorrow", "Use within 3 days"; D uses the short forms.
  • LL-P9 Level squares show only where an item has a level. A row shows one primary flag; D shows its chip, or the squares when there is no chip.
  • LL-P10 Chips carry counts ("Spices++ 6"); the All chip is the total, so the separate "18 items" line goes (kept in B, which has the room).

Open questions, and what a wrong guess costs

  • LL-Q1 Is Recipes losing its always-open search acceptable (LL-P1)? Wrong guess: an extra tap on the screen you search most.
  • LL-Q2 Does Recipes want a Sort at all, given "one ranked list" is locked? Wrong guess: a control nobody uses; removing it is one button.
  • LL-Q3 Should Home follow the list variant or be picked separately (A lists with B Home)? Costs nothing now; it decides how much Home CSS is shared.
  • LL-Q4 Is red right for "N missing" on a recipe? It is today's tone; here it is a quiet tint or outline, and only Past use by is drawn loud (B and D).

Locked decisions this respects

  • Pantry: Filters left, + and the magnifier at the right border, search over the whole row (pantry-search PS-D1 to D5); grouped A to Z by default until a sort or filter is chosen; Out items hidden; Use by stays a sort and filter; groups fold and carry the "added in 24 hours" marker; the Custom view strip; the three-square level; swipe; the half-height item sheet; receipt scan behind +.
  • Recipes: one ranked list; chips All, Ready now, Starred, Batch, Under 30, Added by me; a thumbnail only where a recipe has a banner; no in-app add (the assistant icon opens the person's AI app); versions shown, nothing deleted; the cooking-ideas pane lives on Home; "Make it" (not "Cook it").
  • App: tab order Home, Pantry, Plan, Recipes, Shopping with the badge; the production palette and type only (theme.css tokens, no raw colours); 44px touch targets; light and dark.
