What the owner said, and what is wrong today

The owner's screenshot (UAT, dark, a 2x display, cropped so the rail is not in it) shows the Shopping screen as a narrow column with a large empty area on its right, a dashed hand-off card, a touch hint and plain rows with plate-and-cutlery pictures. Measured against the app on uat v0.122.2: the rail and the 640px column are right, but no panel lane is drawn (46% of a 1440 window and 60% of a 1920 one is empty to the right of the column), there is no desktop header band, a row has no hover state and no visible Bought and Remove (they sit behind a touch-only "more" button), the keyboard-selected line is drawn twice (a bar and a focus ring), and a line with no picture draws the same plate a real emoji would. Details and the measurements: Kitchie #775.

Decisions (SD-1 to SD-12). All Proposals except where the owner is quoted.
  1. SD-1 Frame. Rail 88, column 640 plus 48px gutters, panel 400, one lane group anchored on the left, the panel one gutter from the column, exactly as Pantry, Plan and Home (desktop-layout.md). Owner: "design aligned with overall design language of the app". The lane widths are already settled; no new width.
  2. SD-2 Header band. A 1.5rem bold count, "8 to get", the Pantry's "8 items" treatment; no "Shopping" title (the rail says where you are). It carries the same number as the rail's badge and follows it as lines go; it is absent when the list is empty. Why: every other desktop screen opens with a header row; this is the smallest one that is true.
  3. SD-3 Panel content. The hand-off: the "Is someone else shopping?" card, the links that are out and the "still needs sorting" nudge. The same slot, the same markup, the same live patching; CSS makes it the panel. Absent when the slot is empty (no list, or a member who cannot change the list). Alternative: leave the card in the column (option B). Open question 1.
  4. SD-4 Keys line. One line at the foot of the hand-off, "J K move, D bought, S off the list, E amount, ? all keys", in the accent like the Pantry's hint line. Words only: the keys are the ones issue #594 built.
  5. SD-5 Row states. A hover fill (the Pantry's). The current line (J K or a click) stays the surface fill and 3px accent bar #594 built; the focus ring is left off it because it drew the line a second time.
  6. SD-6 Row actions. Bought and Remove, the row's own forms (one code path for a tap, a swipe and a key), drawn as the Pantry's hover buttons: right edge, 40px, a key cap (D, S), shown on hover, on focus within and on the current line; the member avatar steps aside while they show. The touch "more" button is not drawn at desktop width. Tick and swipe are unchanged (a 1024px tablet in landscape is a desktop width and still swipes).
  7. SD-7 Pictures. A 40px bordered tile with the emoji at 22px (Home's tile, scaled to the 68px row). A line with no picture keeps the slot so the names line up and draws nothing in it, never the plate: the Pantry draws nothing for an item with no emoji, and a plate looks like a real emoji. Alternatives: a dashed tile (the app's "not set yet"), or collapse the slot. The matching is untouched. Parity with the phone: open question 2.
  8. SD-8 Hint line. Kept as it is ("Tick as you drop things in the basket. Swipe right ... left ..."). It is touch wording on a desktop screen; the keys line carries the desktop wording. Open question 3.
  9. SD-9 Unchanged. The tick, the swipe, the scribble, the accent quantity, "N left at home", the receipt scan bar, the "How much did you buy?" question, and the promised and not-bought groups in the column.
  10. SD-10 Phone and tablet. Not drawn, not changed: every rule is inside the one desktop query. The phone page carries one hidden rule and one hidden element for the header count.
  11. SD-11 What loads. Nothing new on load: the rules are one block in the existing desktop.css (already media-gated and cached for a year); the count follows the rail badge in desktop-shopping.js, which the Shopping page already fetches at 64rem and up. No new request, file or dependency.
  12. SD-12 Option B. Drawn and not chosen (no lane for the empty area, the hand-off competes with the list for the top of the column).
Open questions (a wrong guess costs a CSS and markup move)
  1. Is the hand-off the right thing for the panel, or should a click on a line open that line's Pantry item in it? (That is new behaviour: its own issue.)
  2. The empty picture slot on desktop only, or the plate gone on the phone too? The design principles say a decision for one form factor is a decision for all unless the owner says otherwise.
  3. The hint line on desktop: keep, or replace with the keys?
  4. Tablet (600 to 1023px) keeps the phone list; not drawn for this layout, as for the rest of the app.
Notes

The anonymous shopper page (/list/<token>) is a different job (no account, no rail) and is not drawn here. Checked for the same drift and left alone: it is a centred 30rem column; it shares the plate fallback and a touch hint.
