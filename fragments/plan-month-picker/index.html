Plan it: a month behind Another day

Live, clickable mockup for Kitchie issue #826 (the follow-up to #757 option F). Mockup only, nothing is saved or sent. It is the desktop Home page with the Mandarin Mango Yogurt Bowl open on the right. Press Add to plan (or P), then Another day under the seven days. The sample today is Saturday 17 October 2026.

Owner, 17 October 2026 (voice)

“When people say another day, I actually want that monthly view to replace the current flyout … rather than making the seven days across as a horizontal Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, why don’t you do Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday vertically and stack the four columns of dates … colour code what’s open, what’s closed, what’s already got things planned. With hover, you can show what’s planned … The legend needs to be somewhere at the bottom. Get that mocked up first.”

A Another day as a header iconopenThe seven days get the whole list; a calendar icon is easier to miss than a labelled row.
States (a colour plus a shape or word each, so colour is never the only cue)
  • Open: dashed outline, plus sign, green tint. Planned: solid outline, plate, amber tint (yours, someone else’s and the household’s are one state; hover says whose). 2+ plans: stack icon and the count.
  • Usual day off: plain outline, outline moon (still pickable, not locked). Off this week only: filled, filled moon. A usual day off that is being cooked this week looks planned with a small moon.
  • Past: hatched, struck number, not pickable. Today: ring and underlined number. Hard avoid: a triangle where it asks first; a stop on every date when the meal is not for that person.
  • Hover or keyboard focus on a planned date shows what is planned in a read-only popover to the left; a line under the grid says what picking the date would do. Keys: ← → a week, ↑ ↓ a day, Home today, End the last day, Enter plans, Esc back.
Rules kept from #757 (owner, 16 October 2026)
  • A pick plans at once; a 3-second Planned or Replaced hold with an inline Undo, then the flyout slides out. Two steps only for a stacked night. A usual day off is not locked. Replace applies to the person’s own meal. The Hard avoid guard is asked live per date. This week and up to four weeks ahead.
Open questions (for the owner)
  • Column heads: week name over the date (recommended) or date only (L)?
  • Month change: step-rule and “1 Nov” (recommended) or a month band (M)?
  • Another day: a labelled row (recommended) or a header icon (A)?
Loads (DESIGN.md section 6)
