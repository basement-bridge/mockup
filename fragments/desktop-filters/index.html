Notes, proposals, open questions

Owner, 9 October 2026. On desktop, tapping Filters on Pantry "pops the controls out to the right where the item details are shown", and "the dialog will be a screen-blocking window that blocks everything else in the background. Give a few mockup ideas to choose from. Organise this content for desktop better."

Tension to settle. Those two sentences pull apart: a panel on the right, or a window that blocks everything. A and B/C are all true modals. A's "over the detail lane" setting is the one that does both at once.

Proposals (owner silent): Esc, the scrim and the close button discard unsaved changes; only Show applies. Statuses are drawn with a circle as asked, and combine with OR; groups combine with AND (as the phone sheet). Changing a status period switches that status on. Pills show how many items they would add, given the other choices; a pill with none is dashed, not disabled. Sort has Name, Location, Use by, Recently added, Quantity, each with a direction. "Use last filters" is seeded with a sample so you can try it.

Keyboard. Tab cycles inside the dialog. S, L, C, O jump to Status, Location, Category, Sort; then 1 to 9 toggles that section's options (the numbers show on them). Space toggles the focused control, Enter shows the result, R reverses the sort. Prototype only: [ and ] switch option, M changes the pill click rule, P moves A.

Pill click rule. As briefed, a plain click picks only that pill and Ctrl/Cmd-click adds more. The phone toggles on a plain click. The other setting above shows that. A wrong guess costs a lot of friction for people who expect two clicks to give two pills.

Open questions. Should Esc keep what you changed instead of discarding it? Which of the two pill click rules? Should a status keep a circle (one) or a box (several)? Is "Quantity" the right fourth sort, or should it be Level? Should the dialog open on the item panel's side when an item is selected?

Loads. Static HTML, one small CSS and one JS file, shared theme tokens, no images, no storage. Nothing is fetched on engagement; the dialog is built when it opens.
