## The divider (revision 4, replaces the catch-up card; wording human since revision 5)

- **What it is.** One quiet line inside the list, a rule with centred text, in words: "You were here on Saturday evening" (the visit before this one, Sat 17 Oct, 5:30 pm; `?last=wed` makes it Wed 14 Oct and the line "You were here on Wednesday evening"). The words come from a small dependency-free function, `humanVisit(lastIso, nowIso)` (Intl only, in the device's zone): the same day "this evening"; within the week "on Saturday evening"; the same weekday one week back "last Saturday evening"; the week after that "last Thursday"; two weeks to about ten weeks "a few weeks ago"; longer "a few months ago". Part of day: morning (5 to 12), afternoon (12 to 5), evening (5 to 9), night (9 to 5). **No clock times, no dates and no digits at all in it** (revision 4 printed the date and time). Changes newer than it sit above it and carry the existing quiet yellow dot (owner: keep) and a screen-reader "new". Changes older sit below it. No counts, no badge. Nothing else about the divider changed in revision 5. The sample's "now" is Sun 18 Oct, so the default visit was the day before and reads "on Saturday evening", not "last Saturday evening" (that wording appears for the same weekday a week back).
- **Where it is.** In the one plain recent list that every pattern opens on, between the day sections or inside the day that holds the visit time. It is a time marker, so it stays in a filtered list too (a tap on a handle, a tab change, a date window keep it), at the point where that list crosses the last visit. If nothing in the list is newer it is the first thing in the list and says "Nothing new since then." If everything in the list is newer it comes after the list.
- **Loaded by default, never pushed out.** In A, C and D the first load takes days (one calendar day with activity per call, as before) until it reaches the day with the divider, at most three days with activity (H6). In B (revision 5) the first load is one page of whole days, and never fewer days than the divider needs. Filters, tabs and Undo reload the same way, so the divider is in view whatever is on. The rest loads one day at a time as the end scrolls into view.
- **The cap (H6), kept in the part that still makes sense.** "New" reaches back at most three calendar days with activity. If the last visit is older than the third day (try Sam's tab with `?last=wed&tab=person`), the divider sits after the third day (in B the first load is a page, so more days than three may be loaded below it) and says "Only the last three days with activity are marked new." Older changes below it are not marked even if they are newer than the visit. With the last visit inside the three days (the default), the divider is exactly at the visit time.
- **No query parameter needed.** The screen compares `occurred_at` with the last-visit time on the rows it already has. It no longer sends `since` (the API still has it; the stand-in keeps it).
- **Gone with the card:** the tick (mark as caught up), the X (leave the catch-up), the person links in the summary and their way back, the "By Sam, you, Others" line, the catch-up in the lane, the filter button that opened the lane from the catch-up, the sheet that the X opened in A, and the keys C and T. **Proposal:** nothing marks the visit as seen on the screen; the app records the visit when History is left (Platform, the late-arrival caveat Q-H4 stays open). **Open:** should there be a quiet manual "mark as seen" on the divider? It was the tick's job; it was not asked for, so it is not drawn.
- **A (Cascade) on desktop** now always shows its filter panel in the lane (it used to show the catch-up there first); on a phone its quiet line opens the sheet as before.

## The two jobs (owner, voice, 23 Oct 2026)

1. **Catch up:** "what changed since I was last here". Since revision 4 this is a divider inside the plain list, not a card; "new" reaches back three calendar days with activity at most.
2. **Who did that, and when**, after something looks wrong (the milk vanished). The person often knows only roughly when and what, so **Topic and Date must carry this job**, and the tab says whose. The screen is kept quiet: one muted line on a phone, no big filter block, no counts, no badges.

## What the screen is

- **Entry (H4, H6, H8; revision 4, wording revision 5):** no card. The plain recent list opens with the divider "You were here on Saturday evening", newer changes above it marked quietly (the quiet yellow dot), three calendar days with activity at most (H6). **No counts.** The old card, its tick, its X and its person links are gone (see "The divider").
- **Tabs (person tabs):** My activity (`actor=me`), the other person when the household has two (`actor=<id>`), and **Others** (`actor=others`). With more people there is no per-person tab: My activity and Others. No counts on a tab.
- **Others (Q5, decided):** everyone who is not you, **and** events with no member (a shopper link, a shared assistant link, the system; read "Someone"), **and** past events by a member who has since been removed (read **"A former member"**; members are erased after a week and History is anonymised, so the name is gone). Nothing is hidden. In a two-person household the Sam tab and Others overlap on Sam's changes, because `actor=others` means "not me" (see open points).
- **The list (H7a, H8, H15):** recent history, one calendar day with activity per load (`days=1&tz=`), newest first, quiet days skipped, no ceiling, no button; in A, C and D the first load runs to the divider, in B (the default) it is one page of whole days (R5-3). In A the filter is one quiet component: on a phone, one muted line under the tabs (Anytime · All topics, a clear X when something is on) that opens a short bottom sheet; on desktop the same body in the lane. In B (default) the filter is the sentence line, "last 30 days · everything", and its two slots open light popups with More (R5-2). The list updates as you pick.
- **Date (the Pantry rotary, see below):** a status row, toggle on the left, rotary on the right, windows Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days; off reads Anytime. Sets `from`.
- **Topic (single):** the registry's areas, one combined feed across Pantry, Shopping and Recipes (area = first part of `kind`). Choosing an area with several kinds shows a finer row for that area ("In Pantry": Added, Removed, Moved...), which sets `kind`. This is how "the milk vanished" is found: Pantry, Removed, around then.
- **Removed from the filter:** the App (product) control (product stays on the event and is shown on the detail; it is only sent, hidden, with the in-context entity link because the API requires it), the Touches (`affects`) filter and the "Touches you" mark, the "N changes" summary, tab counts and the filter-button badge.
- **A row:** the skeleton the event always has (who, when, topic) plus the kind's template output (`HM.render`). No template published, or the evaluation fails: the generic row "Sam made a change in Recipe". Template being fetched: skeleton row. Platform down: shown as down, never as empty.
- **Open a result (H5):** a bottom sheet on phone, the lane on desktop. Content: who, when and app from the skeleton, then the template's output including its `detail` lines, and a link only where the template defines one to a route the product registered. A generic row opens a generic detail.
- **Preview control (mockup only):** **Filter pattern: A Cascade, B Sentence (default), C Quick answers, D Tap to filter (variation)**; **Search (Q1): A, filters only (default), or B, free text over the days loaded**; **Choices per group (Q2, undecided): one at a time (shown) or several (lists)**; person, household size, last visit (Sat, or Wed to show the three-day cap), D's filter choices (popover, or the gutter flyout on desktop), template registry, mockup notes.

## The Date rotary, and what Pantry actually does (verified in the code)

Source: Pantry's Filters sheet, `flows/household/app.js` ("status rotary", `srow`, `rotStep`) and `flows/household/*/styles.css` (`.strow .stt .dotc .rot .rar`); described in `README.md`. The History Date row copies the markup, class names and rules, so the build can use the app's own.

What exists in Pantry, and so here: each status is one long pill with a checkbox on the left (tap it or the name to turn the status on or off); the rotary on the right is plain text with up and down arrows; **drag up or down on it, scroll the wheel over it, or tap it to step through the windows** (a tap past the last wraps to the first, and a tap turns the status on); while dragging, the pill's label shows the window value live, and on release the label returns to the name while the rotary keeps the value; a light vibration ticks on each step where the device allows it.

**Press and hold on the rotary is not in Pantry** (pointerdown, move, up and wheel only; there is no timer on the rotary, and Pantry's press-and-hold is only on running-low rows and chips). Pantry also has no "Anytime" option: a status is off or on at a window. The owner believed a hold on "Anytime" opens the rotary directly. **Not invented here** (owner's rule: replicate what exists, flag it). The tap behaviour is built, and the History Date row shows "Anytime" when off. Question for the owner: should a press-and-hold open the rotary (or a picker) directly? If yes it belongs in Pantry too, so it should be one shared change.

Two small differences from Pantry, both marked: the first tap on an **off** row turns it on at its remembered window (Pantry's tap always advances, which would skip a window when the row is off); arrow keys and Enter also step it (Pantry's rotary is pointer-only). Time zone: the device's, undecided. **B (revision 4, changed in revision 5):** the same rotary and rules, but no Anytime, so its row has no toggle (the window is always on); it has a fourth, last stop All time, and it opens on Last 30 days. A day picker for specific dates sits beside it (R5-2, R5-4).

## Proposals (the owner was silent; marked in the mockup)

- **S-P3. Filter choices come from the registry** (`/api/history/kinds`, designed, not built; Q4): drawn as Pantry, Shopping and Recipes (the last two hypothetical: neither emits History yet and their kind names are placeholders; their rows are generic until a template exists). The labels belong to the producing product.
- **S-P6. The detail is the lane on desktop, a sheet on phone.**
- **S-P8. Topic has a finer step for an area with several kinds**, so "removed" can be picked after "Pantry" (the model's `kind`); the owner said a single Topic filter mapping to area or kind, so this is one filter in two steps, not two filters.
- **S-P9. Stand-in templates**: Kitchie's "wording today" column written as templates in `model.js`; Kitchie authors the real ones.
- **S-P10. Date windows are Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days** (the first version's); there is no Yesterday or custom range (the API has `to`, so a range is possible later). Off reads Anytime.
- **S-P11. Three tabs in a two-person household** (My activity, the other person, Others) so that Others is reachable without losing the person tab; with more people, My activity and Others.
- **S-P12. (Superseded, revision 4.)** The catch-up card and its "By Sam, you, Others" links are gone; the divider replaces them.

- **S-P13. Chips in C** are Mine today, Removed this week, Moved this week, By Sam, By others: five, chosen for the two jobs ("Removed" and "Moved" answer "the milk vanished" and "where did it go"). Each is built only from `actor`, `kind` and `from`. A chip that does not name a person keeps the current tab, so "Removed this week" under My activity is mine only.
- **S-P14. D's day label** sets one calendar day (`from` and `to`); the Date rotary has no such value in Pantry, so the rotary reads the day while it is on and a tap goes back to the remembered window.
- **S-P15. D's Undo** (a toast, 6 seconds) and the scroll to the top after a tap; D hides a handle that is already on.
- **S-P17. (Revision 5) The three action options are kinds only,** not a mix of areas and kinds; areas are reached through More. Wrong guess costs: a person who thinks in areas ("Shopping") needs one more tap.
- **S-P18. (Revision 5) The decay counts days, not events.** The summary says which kinds have activity and on which days, so a kind's score is the sum over those days of 0.5^(age / 2 days); ten removals on one day count once. HALF_LIFE stays a proposal.
- **S-P19. (Revision 5) The time options are nested windows,** so a person with activity today sees Today, Last 7 days and Last 30 days even though they overlap; each is offered only if it holds activity (day-level, from the summary).
- **S-P20. (Revision 5) The day picker** is a month grid (weeks start on Monday), days with nothing on record for that person are not selectable, and going to a month before the 30-day edge widens the summary.
- **S-P21. (Revision 5) `humanVisit` thresholds** (today, within the week, the same weekday one week back, the week after, a few weeks to ten, then months) and the part-of-day cut-offs (morning 5 to 12, afternoon to 5, evening to 9, night) are the designer's; the owner gave the shape and the examples.

## Open points and what a wrong guess costs

- **"Anyone".** The owner described the sentence as "Anyone · anything · anytime" (now "everything"). With the tabs left alone, every list is one person's (You, Sam, Others), so the first word is the tab's value and "Anyone" never shows. If the tabs go, "Anyone" is `actor` left out and D's person and item taps get much more useful. Wrong guess costs: building a person word that duplicates the tabs.
- **Item filter under a tab.** Tapping "Milk" in My activity shows only my milk changes; to see who removed it you must also look in Sam's tab or Others. Same cause.
- **Parallel work on mine and others.** Checked `origin/main` on 24 Oct: nothing after PR #290 touches `history/` or the tabs, and there are no open PRs in mockup, kitchie or platform. ## What the default path loads and what waits (`DESIGN.md` section 6, 6a)

Loads: the page, `theme.css`, `shared.css`, `option-d.css` (shared classes; 19 KB), `search.css` (about 8 KB), `model.js` (about 15 KB, the stand-in for Platform; the build replaces it with the calls) and `search.js` (about 40 KB). In B (default): one feed call per day for the open tab until one page is filled and the divider is settled (the divider is part of the first load, never behind a tap); the first load is not 30 days of entries; the cheap 30-day summary (kind and days only; the Platform call the owner approved) is read only when a slot is tapped. In A, C and D: one feed call per day, up to the divider (one to three days). Waits: the other tabs; older days (one per call, only when the end of the list scrolls into view, and in B past the 30-day edge the same, with the time turning to All time); the summary's widened form (only once the range goes past 30 days); the popups' options (drawn from the summary when a slot is tapped); the flyout, the day picker and the rotary (only on More); the filter choices (registry call on opening the filter); a template (fetched on first sight of a kind, cached); the detail (rendered from the row already loaded, no new request; the template link is only followed on tap). The rotary needs no call of its own: a step re-requests the newest day only. Everything lands in place: tabs, filter, days and the detail swap content without navigation. Phone and desktop share one script (CSS-first, DESIGN.md 7); the lane exists only from 1024px.
