Owner ask, 23 Oct 2026 (typed): "this section hasn't been looked at for a long time. Evaluate how the entire settings feature set has been implemented, in code, and create different mock-up ideas as to how to drive this outcome. Feel free to critically evaluate if all those knobs are really relevant or are there better ways. I'm very open. User experience is paramount. Less is more."

Kitchie issue for this work: see the link in the mockup page and REVIEW-QUEUE. Mockup: `fragments/settings-redesign/` (live: <https://basement-bridge.github.io/mockup/fragments/settings-redesign/>; the Pages URL could not be fetched from the authoring session, egress is blocked, so the live page is unchecked, the page itself was run in headless Chromium by `tools/settings-redesign-check.mjs`). Labels: **F** = fact (code read, git log, or measured by running the app); **I** = inference. Nothing here was tried on a real Android device.

## Job

Make Kitchie look and behave the way this person wants, once, without hunting. Today Settings tries to be five jobs at once: how it looks (theme, text, emoji, motion), what a Pantry row shows, how pushy the assistant is, the household's structure, and account actions. The person's real jobs are fewer: (1) pick a look, (2) tell the app who and what the household is, (3) connect an assistant, (4) decide how often the app asks questions, (5) get my data out. Text size, motion and dark at night are the phone's jobs.

## How we know the person is in this job

They tap the avatar (then Looks, Preferences, Household) or the desktop window's gutter. It is almost always a one-time visit after sign-up or after a specific annoyance. **I:** nobody visits Settings daily; no usage data exists for any knob (platform telemetry counts household-active days only).

## What leads

On the phone: the avatar menu is the real front door (11 rows). The Settings overview page adds nothing the menu does not already have except Sample items (F: `profile-menu.ts` and `app.ts renderSettingsPage` both call `appSharedRowsHtml`). Looks is the only screen with display knobs.

## What this job does not need

Per-person row-detail switches, a second reduce-motion switch, a size control that moves one list, a Reset button for them, a bell switch, a Settings overview page that repeats the menu. DESIGN.md section 1: "A job does not have to show everything we know about an item", the job decides, not a preference. Precedents for deleting a knob: the desktop shortcut-cue switch (12 Oct), the Light/Dark/Follow choice (9 to 10 Oct), the old time-zone setting (D18, inferred instead).

## Mockup

`fragments/settings-redesign/index.html`: Today (reproduces the Android report from the code) and four options, each with phone and desktop, a preview whose rows change when a control changes, and a ledger of every knob.

- **A. Just the essentials (recommended).** Settings stops existing; Looks has three controls: Theme, Light or dark (#1088), Emoji. Text size, motion, dark at night follow the phone. Row detail and the bell are not preferences. Gone: K02, K03, K05 to K11 (nine). Household items stay where the menu already has them.
- **B. Grouped by job, More for the rest.** A Settings front door of four jobs (How it looks, How it behaves, My household, Assistants), two or three controls each and the rest under More. Four row switches become one Rows choice; text size becomes a whole-app Larger; Preferences' stock-check dials get a front door unchanged. Closest to today, most new UI, one more tap to anything.
- **C. Search first.** One search box, six common things, type to reach all 27 entries. Every knob stays, including the Pantry-only size. Wins only if the set of settings grows.
- **D. In context, thin page.** `Aa` on the Pantry list (desktop `View` beside Sort) holds text size and row details, labelled "This list"; Theme, Light or dark and Emoji sit at the top of the avatar's profile menu; the Settings page left holds only Kitchen setup, Household, Assistants, Install, CSV.

**Recommendation: A.** It is the only option where the answer to "this knob does nothing on my phone" is that there is no such knob, and it needs mostly deletion. Use D's rule for anything the owner insists on keeping: it lives where it is used and says what it changes. B is the fallback if he wants one visible front door for Preferences and the household. C is not recommended now.

## Code audit

Settings in the app today is four surfaces: the **Looks** screen (device display knobs), the **Settings** overview (phone: `/settings`; desktop: the window's Settings pane), **Preferences** (Mine and Household: stock-check dials, food, notes, proposals; about 8,800 lines in `server/src/preferences/` plus 12 assets, owner-designed and out of scope except placement) and the **people pages** (Me, Household). UAT and production run in platform mode: household name and members are Platform's, and My assistant is on Profile > AI assistants (`assistantOnProfile = platformOn`); standalone mode keeps the Settings-page versions (F).

Facts that frame the table:
- **Age (F, git log).** The repo's history starts 29 Sep 2026. The display knobs K03 to K11 were added 1 Oct 2026 for issue #73 ("browser-only settings", stage 1 and 2), for a single list view. `settings.js` was last touched 10 Oct (themes). Since 13 Oct the repo has 558 commits; none changed what these knobs reach.
- **Where it is stored (F).** Display knobs: one localStorage key `kitchie.settings` (per device, per browser, per environment since #1056 on 22 Oct, so UAT started empty then). Two cookies leave the device so the server can paint first: `kitchie_theme`, `kitchie_emoji`. Member prefs and the assistant choice are in `stock_prefs`; `kitchie_assistant` and `kitchie_zone` are cookie mirrors. The Looks note says "Only your theme and the emoji setting also travel with you"; in code that means "sent to the server for first paint", not "follows you to another device" (I: the copy reads as cross-device; it is not).
- **Effect of Item name size, measured (F).** Test app (harness `test/web-harness.ts`) in headless Chromium at 390 px and 1366 px, four Pantry items. With `size: large`: Pantry list item names 16.96 px to 20.8 px (small 15.2 px), 11 text nodes change; Home 0 of 22, Shopping 0 of 14, Plan 0 of 157, Cooking 0 of 10, Preferences 0 of 53, menu 0 of 26 (phone). Desktop: Pantry 11 of 44, Home 0 of 37, Shopping 0 of 16. History had no rows in the fixture (I: its rows are `li .name` so the rule would reach them). The cause is one rule, `layout.ts:312-313`, `li .name`, used by the Pantry list rows (`renderRow`, `app.ts:741`) and History; Home, Shopping, Plan and Recipes draw names with other classes. The browser test asserts `data-size="large"` is set (`browser/settings-screens.browser.ts`), never a font size, so it cannot fail when the setting does nothing. The segment is labelled Normal where the report says Medium (F: `settings-mockup.test.ts`). Whether the Android report is this, or a stale UAT store after #1056, is #1089's to say.
- **Other effects measured (F).** Show the spot: Pantry meta "1 · Shelf · Meat" becomes "1"; Show the amount: becomes "Shelf"; use-by: visible `.due` 2 to 0 on the Pantry list; Compact rows: row height 70.4 px to 59.2 px, no font change. None of them reaches Home or Shopping.
- **Duplicates (F).** Phone: Looks, Categories, Locations and spots, Install and Sign out each appear on the Profile menu and on the Settings page. The household name is editable in Settings (standalone), on the Household page and in Preferences > Household. Categories and Locations are also in Preferences > Household. Install is a button, not a setting.
- **Vestigial (F).** `THEMES = ["auto"]` in `settings.js`: the saved `theme` field can only be "auto"; `apply()` and `layout.ts` still carry the `data-theme` branch. Kitchie #1088 asks about this.
- **Tests.** Display knobs: `test/settings.test.ts`, `test/settings-mockup.test.ts`, `browser/settings-screens.browser.ts` (attributes and saved values, never an effect on a screen other than the Looks screen). Theme: `test/theme.test.ts`, `browser/themes.browser.ts`. Assistant: `assistant-setting.test.ts`, `profile-assistant.test.ts`. Stock dials: `stock-check-settings.test.ts`, `browser/preferences-*.browser.ts`. Categories, locations, starters, household: their own tests (named in the table).

## Proposals (SR-P, all awaiting the owner; wrong guess cost in brackets)

- **SR-P1.** Delete the Settings overview page and the menu's Settings row; the menu already has Looks, Kitchen setup and Install. (Low: one fewer tap path; the desktop gutter's Settings is renamed Looks.)
- **SR-P2.** Looks has exactly three controls: Theme, Light or dark (per mockup#285, device cookie as #1088 option 1), Emoji. (Low.)
- **SR-P3.** Remove K03 to K11: size, motion, spot, amount, use-by, activity, compact, Reset. Stored values are ignored automatically, `clean()` drops unknown keys, no migration. (Medium for anyone who relied on Large; see SR-Q1.)
- **SR-P4.** Text size is the phone's: add one check that type is relative (rem or em, no fixed px for body text) so phone text scaling reaches every screen. (A separate small build item; also tested on a real device, which was not done here.)
- **SR-P5.** Remove the vestigial `theme` field and the `data-theme` branch (K02). (Code only.)
- **SR-P6.** Fix the Looks note: "These stay on this device; your theme and emoji choice are also sent so pages open in the right colours." (Copy only.)
- **SR-P7.** One home for the household name (the Household page), one for Categories and Locations (Kitchen setup on the menu), one Install, one Sign out. (Low.)
- **SR-P8.** Ask "default assistant" only with two or more connected; with one it is used automatically; drop None and Another from the up-front choice. (Low; standalone card goes.)
- **SR-P9.** Replace the attribute-only browser test with one that measures an effect, or delete it with the knob. (Test hygiene; the lesson of K03.)
- **SR-P10.** If any display knob is kept, put it where it is used and label its scope (Option D's `Aa` on the Pantry list). (Rule, not a build.)
- **SR-P11.** Sample items: keep the Pantry strip as the single door; Settings no longer shows a second one. (Needs SR-Q7.)

## Open questions (SR-Q, each with a recommendation; *How checked* says where the answer came from)

1. **Remove Item name size?** Recommend yes (SR-P3, P4). *How checked:* measured scope above; the 1 Oct #73 stage-1 brief asked for it on a single list; fact for the scope, trade-off for the decision. Closest earlier decision: #73, which predates Home, Plan and Shopping and does not settle it.
2. **Remove the four row-detail switches (spot, amount, use-by, compact)?** Recommend yes: the job decides what a row shows (DESIGN.md section 1). *How checked:* DESIGN.md read; measured Pantry-only effect; trade-off.
3. **Remove Reduce motion in favour of the OS setting?** Recommend yes, after a check that every animation path honours `prefers-reduced-motion`. *How checked:* 110 matches of the media query, global `data-motion` rule at `layout.ts:36`; not every path audited (I).
4. **Remove the bell switch?** Recommend yes; if the bell is too loud, fix the badge. *How checked:* `layout.ts:316`; the badge is hidden at zero already (F).
5. **Ten themes?** DESIGN.md already says narrow to three or five later. Recommend not here; ask when #1088 lands. *How checked:* `theme.ts`, DESIGN.md; trade-off.
6. **Delete the Settings overview page?** Recommend yes (SR-P1). *How checked:* both pages share one row builder; the only unique content is Sample items (F).
7. **Sample items: remove the Settings section?** Recommend yes, if the Pantry strip's "Not yet" and mute stay recoverable another way; otherwise keep a single "Clear sample items" row under Kitchen setup while any remain. *How checked:* `web-starters.ts`; whether the mute can be undone elsewhere was not traced.
8. **Ask for "My assistant" only with two or more connected?** Recommend yes. *How checked:* `profile-assistant.ts` already marks the only connected one used automatically (F).
9. **Quiet hours and Most suggestions per day: leave, or follow the phone's focus modes?** Recommend leave (decided D19, D20); revisit with usage data. *How checked:* `dials.ts` header cites owner decisions; collides with nothing today. Decided rules, not mine to change.
10. **Is device-level storage right for the display choices (theme, mode, emoji)?** Recommend yes: they are about a screen, not a person; fix the copy (SR-P6). *How checked:* `theme.ts` header; #1088 option 1; trade-off.
11. **Desktop: rename the window's Settings to Looks, and move Categories and Locations under Household?** Recommend yes under A. *How checked:* `profile.js` gutter and `settings-window-profile-settings.md`; trade-off.
12. **Is any in-app text scale wanted at all?** If the phone's own scale is not enough for someone, D's `Aa` (this list only, labelled) or B's whole-app Larger are the two honest forms. Recommend neither until a person asks. *How checked:* not testable here; needs one real device.
13. **Platform and Recipe settings.** Their own settings, if any, were not audited (the cookies are origin-wide). Recommend a separate audit. *How checked:* not read.
14. **Do not add telemetry to decide this.** Recommend decide by principle. *How checked:* `telemetry.ts` counts active households only; adding more is a cost and privacy decision.
