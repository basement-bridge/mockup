## Job

Today Pantry add is one item at a time. The person has a receipt and wants everything on it in the Pantry, matched against what they already hold, with the least typing.

Owner, voice, 12 October 2026 (tidied): since client-side OCR is now good, scan an image of a receipt and bulk-add the items. (1) Match each scanned line to an existing Pantry item **exactly** first. (2) An exact match gets a clear visual cue and must show whether the result is **add as new** or **just revise the quantity** of the existing item. (3) Close but not exact: a **confidence rating shown visually** (he floated one, three and five dots; "very acute, compact"; use best judgment), and the person confirms the suggested match or overrides it; an override becomes a new item. (4) Not recognisable: flagged distinctly, and the person must actively confirm it as a new item, "like a visual scan where on a given page they cross it out". Client-side OCR, no server round trip. Mockup first, in `mockup`; UI always lives in Kitchie; same look and feel.

## How we know the person is in this job

They chose it: Add, then a receipt (RS-1). On desktop, a photo dropped or pasted starts it too. Not inferred.

## What leads

The result of the read, not the photo: one review where what is certain is already done and what needs the person is obvious. Nothing is written until the person adds.

## What this job does not need

No account step, no stored photo (the live camera preview is now wanted: see RS-34; the earlier "no live camera preview" is superseded), no AI turn, no price field in the Pantry (shown to check only, RS-18), no barcode.

## Mockup

Four options (A Receipt order, B Sort the pile, C The paper page, D One at a time), each drawn through entry, capture, reading, review, per-line states, override, confirm-as-new, summary, done and undo, plus edge states (dark photo, first-use download, offline, camera blocked, low quality, nothing found, duplicates, quantity parsing, discount and bag lines). Phone, tablet and desktop. Recommended default (a proposal): A.

### How a line is scored against the Pantry (RS-66)

No model, no network. The code is `server/src/assets/pantry-receipt-match.js` in Kitchie (a layer on the Shopping scan's `receipt-match.js`), and the numbers below are what it gives (the same table is a test).

1. **Prepare the receipt line.** Repair letters the reader slipped, spell out a till's shortening from a short list (BTR butter, CHKN chicken, SLMN salmon, SMKD smoked, EVOO extra virgin olive oil), add the kind of thing a well-known brand is (Chobani adds yoghurt), drop sizes (500G, 1L) and filler words (large, fresh, pack), fold plurals. The Pantry name is read by the server's own name rule.
2. **Base score** (`scoreNames`): how much of the **Pantry item's** words the line covers. A word counts 1 when it is the same, 0.9 for a plural or a prefix, 0.85 for a till's shortening, 0.8 for one slip, else 0. The score is the average over the Pantry item's words, times a tightness factor from 0.9 to 1 (how many of the receipt's own words are the item's).
3. **Extra words cost.** Each receipt word that is not filler and is not in the Pantry name takes 0.15 off, at most 0.30.
4. **Same kind of thing.** If the line and the Pantry name share a family (pasta and penne; chicken and turkey; yoghurt and kefir; butter and margarine; the list is `FAMILIES`), the score is at least 0.50.
5. **The reader's doubt.** The score is multiplied by min(1, reader confidence / 0.9). In the dark (live) it is lowered a further 15%.
6. **State.** **Exact**: the line's own printed words (nothing spelled out, nothing slipped, fillers and sizes ignored) are exactly the Pantry item's words, the reader was at least 0.9 sure, and no second item is within 0.10. **Check**: otherwise, with a score of at least **0.45**. **Unsure**: under 0.45. The best item is the recommendation; the next three are the Pick another candidates.
7. **Dots** (Check only): **0.85 and up is 3, 0.65 to 0.84 is 2, 0.45 to 0.64 is 1.**
8. **Several Pantry records with the same words** count as one item; the most recently changed one stands for them (no batches, RS-56).
9. **Left out before matching** (RS-65): a negative price, or a savings, discount, coupon, voucher, points word, is a Discount; plastic or carrier bag, toilet, detergent, shampoo and the like are Not food.
