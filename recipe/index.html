Recipe tab, end to end

Draft for feedback. The whole Recipe tab for anyone arriving in recipe land: the list, a recipe, recipes arriving from the person's own AI assistant, moving between versions, cooking it, and adding photos and notes. Every screen is a live phone at phone width first. Sample data only. Open Mockup (bottom right on every screen) to switch who is using it and to flip between options.

What loads (DESIGN.md section 6)
  1. Default path, the list: theme, recipe.css, recipe.js (sample data, icons, bar), and the list's own script. Rows are thumbnails or emoji only; photos load lazily as rows come into view in the build.
  2. Deferred: the day picker (plan-sheet.js) builds its markup only when opened; in the build, fetch it on first swipe or first focus of Plan. The Add, Check it, Change it and Cook screens are separate routes, loaded only when reached (under R-O4 the build has no Add, Check it or Change it screens; the handoff's section 7 has the UI scope). The version tree, notes editor and photo picker are built on tap.
  3. Recipe page: the first photo only; the others on first swipe. Cooked history and Photos render below the fold; in the build they can be fetched when their section nears the viewport.
  4. Entitlement: the whole tab is the Recipes product boundary; a household without Recipes loads none of it (household flow's upgrade path).
  5. Stays close to the build: class names (.rr, .ing, .strip, .act, .sheet) and tokens are meant to be lifted. Sample names live only in recipe.js.
