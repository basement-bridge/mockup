Recipe tab, end to end

Draft for feedback. The whole Recipe tab for anyone arriving in recipe land: the list, a recipe, adding one by hand or with the assistant, changing it, moving between versions, and cooking it. Every screen is a live phone at phone width first. Sample data only. Open Mockup (bottom right on every screen) to switch who is using it and to flip between options.

Recipe-side gaps (solve in Recipe, Kitchie only renders)
  1. G1Notes. Recipe has no note on a recipe family. Needs an append-only note list (text, who, when), immutable like everything else.
  2. G2Cook history. record_usage keeps a count and a last date only. Cooked needs one entry per cook: when, who, which version, servings, an optional line and photo reference.
  3. G3Drafts. Recipe has active and disabled only. An assistant draft needs a third status (draft) that lists, search and ideas skip, or a draft must live outside Recipe until saved. Recommended: a draft status in Recipe, so the assistant writes once.
  4. G4Household default version. Not stored today. Needed so Plan and Cook open the right one.
  5. G5Who added it. Only free text in source.description. Needs a member id for "Added by me".
  6. G6Step timers. Steps are text only. A minutes value per step (optional, never invented) would drive the timer.
  7. G7Photos. Only source.image_refs. Dish photos, step photos and cook photos: see the photo-storage slice.
  8. G8Reading a photo or a link. Recipe has no parser. The reading is the caller's (the AI's) work; Kitchie needs a way to hand it the photo or link and get a draft back. Open: which side runs it.
  9. G9Batch facts. Freezes and portions fit in metadata.tags; agree the tag words (closed vocabulary where logic acts).
Other slices of the Recipe tab

Built by other agents at the same time; their folders sit next to this one (for example ../recipe-versions/, ../recipe-photos/, ../cooking-ideas/ if they used those names). Their sections are in docs/knowledge/jobs-to-be-done/recipe-tab.md. This slice draws versions, photos and ideas only as far as the flow needs them and defers to those slices.

What loads (DESIGN.md section 6)
  1. Default path, the list: theme, recipe.css, recipe.js (sample data, icons, bar), and the list's own script. Rows are thumbnails or emoji only; photos load lazily as rows come into view in the build.
  2. Deferred: the day picker (plan-sheet.js) builds its markup only when opened; in the build, fetch it on first swipe or first focus of Plan. The Add, Check it, Change it and Cook screens are separate routes, loaded only when reached. The version tree, notes editor and photo picker are built on tap.
  3. Recipe page: the first photo only; the others on first swipe. Cooked history and Photos render below the fold; in the build they can be fetched when their section nears the viewport.
  4. Entitlement: the whole tab is the Recipes product boundary; a household without Recipes loads none of it (household flow's upgrade path).
  5. Stays close to the build: class names (.rr, .ing, .strip, .act, .sheet) and tokens are meant to be lifted. Sample names live only in recipe.js.
