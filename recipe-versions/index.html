Recipe versions and lifecycle

This one answers: what is "the recipe" to a person, when a change fixes the version you have and when it makes a new one, how a try becomes a keeper, what stays as an audit trail, and what the assistant may change on its own. The model in one picture

Three layers. Only the bottom one is frozen; the two above it are allowed to move. That is the relaxation.

Recipe (a family). What a person means by "egg fried rice": one row in the list. It is named by, and opens, the household's usual version.
Version. One way of making it: "With peas", "Less soy, more garlic". Has a parent, a state (Suggested, Trying, Kept, Put away) and a current revision. Its name and state can change.
Revision. One saved edit of a version: only what changed (pick 6 B, git-like: a parent and a small change set, with a checkpoint every 10th), who saved it (and through which assistant), their words, when. Never changed, never deleted. Fixing a version adds a revision; going back or undoing one change adds one too.

6What a revision stores (backend) = B Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026)

Changes only, git-like (owner: "only the change / diff is kept… rich text, notes, images, links… storage efficient"). Content-addressed pieces, a change set per revision, a checkpoint every 10th, rich-text notes diffed per block, photos as references. About 27 MB for 200 recipes × 5 versions × 20 revisions, against 117 MB for whole copies.

Decisions in this slice (Proposal unless marked locked)

Numbered V-D so they do not clash with R-D (flow slice) or other slices. The same list is in docs/knowledge/jobs-to-be-done/recipe-tab.md.

  1. V-D1Three layers: recipe (family), version, revision. Immutability moves down from the version to the revision. A version can be fixed in place; what it said before is never lost. Proposal
  2. V-D2Four states for a version: Suggested, Trying, Kept, Put away. Suggested = made by an assistant and nobody has said yes (out of lists, plans, ideas and search). Trying = in use, not yet judged. Kept = a keeper. Put away = hidden, kept in full. "Favourite" is not a state: it is each member's star (existing set_preference, made per member). Proposal
  3. V-D3Transitions. Suggested → Trying (a person says yes) or dismissed. Trying → Kept (Keep it), → Put away (Not for us). Kept → Trying (Back to trying), → Put away. Put away → Kept (Bring back). Making a Trying version the usual also keeps it. A spin-off ends a version's life here with a pointer to its new recipe. Proposal
  4. V-D4The state says itself in one slim band at the top of a version, with the one action that moves it on (Keep it, Bring back, Open). Kept and the usual need no band: the "Our usual" pill under the title says it. Same slim component as R-D12. Proposal
  5. V-D5"The recipe" is the family, named by and opening its usual. The list shows one row per family; Plan, Cook and Ideas open the usual unless someone picks another version. Every family has exactly one usual at all times; the first version is it until someone changes it. This is gap G4, answered. Proposal
  6. V-D6Stars are per member and never change what the household opens. Only the usual does. A star is the member's own "liked" (V-D33). Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026) Replaces: "Open, cross-feature X5: preferences draws Recipe stars household-wide". X5 answered for Recipe's side as both, derived.
  7. V-D7A version row reads: name, what it changed from its parent, who and when, cooked count and revision, then usual and state pills. An assistant's work shows a dashed ✦ avatar and "Alex's assistant". Proposal
  8. V-D8Versions is a screen, grouped by state (pick 1 A). The R-D20 chip opens it. L3: there is no chip. A recipe with one version has no versions row; with several, the strip (its own row, docked above the jump row) is the quick switch and its last item, "All versions", opens this screen. The ⋯ sheet opens it too. The screen holds states, put-away versions, "To review" and compare. Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026)
  9. V-D9Every lifecycle action is in the version's ⋯ sheet, and only the ones that apply show: Make it our usual, New version from this, Make it its own recipe, History, Compare with our usual, Keep it, Back to trying, Put away. Proposal
  10. V-D10Three kinds of change, in the person's words (narrowed by L4: the person says them to their assistant, which writes them through patch_version's target): Fix this one (next revision of the same version), Keep both (new version, parent = this one, starts Trying), It is a different dish now (spin off: a new recipe, linked back). Proposal
  11. V-D11Where a change goes: pick 2 C. A try takes edits in place; a keeper or our usual asks: fix this one, keep both, or a different dish. Change in Recipes says where it will land, then opens the person's AI app (L4). Supersedes R-D16 Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026)
  12. V-D12A child version is pinned to the revision of its parent it was made from. A later fix to the parent is offered once on the child ("Original was fixed on 21 Sep · Bring it in"), never applied silently. Proposal
  13. V-D13History lists revisions newest first (plus "Who changed what", a per-line blame, from 6 B): what kind (Made, Fixed, Adjusted, Went back, Spun off), who (assistant marked), when, their words, and the diff in the R-D20 tint. Each older one has See it as it was and Go back to this. Going back saves a new revision; nothing is rewound. Proposal
  14. V-D14Compare is two columns with only what differs, the rest folded into one line ("5 ingredients the same, method the same"). Left is always the usual. Proposal
  15. V-D15Kept as an audit trail, never changed or deleted: revisions, the cook log (each cook points to a version and revision), the event journal, nutrition observations (attached to a revision), suggestions and their outcome, and spin-off links. Proposal
  16. V-D16Relaxed: a version's name, state, current revision and parent label; the family's name and usual; per-member stars; the version's cover_photo_id (P-D4); each version's notes and photos heads (their own revision lines, spec 9.4 and 9.5). Every one of these changes is a journal event with who and when. Proposal
  17. V-D17A person's own words stay required where an assistant acts (every content or state write; an assistant never resolves a review or accepts its own draft without the person's yes). In the app, tapping Save is the intent; "why" is optional. Each revision stores which it was. Proposal
  18. V-D18Promoting a try: pick 3 A, ask after every cook of a Trying version: Keep it, Needs work, Not for us. Keep it offers "Make it our usual" as a separate tick. Needs work can hand the change to the assistant. Not for us puts it away with the date, at once, from the first cook, with undo (answer 1, Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026); replaces "open: at once or after a second cook"). It sits after R-D19's pantry and freezer questions, in the same single end-of-cook ask. Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026)
  19. V-D19Any member who cooks it answers, and the first answer counts. A later cook can change it. Followers are not asked. L5: no followers; every member is asked alike. Locked L5
  20. V-D20Every revision and state change carries an author (a member), a channel (app or assistant) and, for an assistant, its client name. An assistant is never an author on its own: it acts for one member and shows as "Alex's assistant (Claude)". Kept for attribution even though all members are equal (L5). Answers gap G5. Proposal
  21. V-D21The assistant and kept versions: pick 4 B, edit and review after. An assistant may change any version, a keeper or the usual included, with the person's words. A change to a keeper or the usual is live at once and opens a review: a "To review" badge on the version, its strip item, Versions and History for every member; the review shows what changed, which assistant for whom, their words; Looks right accepts all, Undo reverts one change as a new revision. A cook meanwhile is logged against the revision it used and does not close the review. Tries are never reviewed. Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026)
  22. V-D22Reviews and drafts wait quietly: one slim line on the version and on Versions, and a To review screen. Never a pop-up, never on Home (R-D12). Suggestion answers Apply, Keep both, Not now, No thanks (void with 4 A). A whole new recipe from an assistant is still a family whose only version is Suggested (R-D12's draft, gap G3): Add to Recipes or Not for us. A dismissed draft is visible only in that member's assistant history (answer 4, Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026); replaces "a put-away draft shows under Put away"). Proposal
  23. V-D23Who may do what: everyone, equally (L5, L8). Admin rule for the usual and for putting away a version others cooked. Every member may keep, put away (whoever cooked it, no approval), change the usual, review an assistant's change and remove photos; content itself comes through each member's assistant (L4). Putting away the usual is blocked until another one is the usual (BR4). Authorship is still recorded on every write. Supersedes the admin part of this decision and the spec's old set_usual admin check; replaced by spec BR16. Locked L5, L8 Stays with the preferences owner: D25 to D30 themselves, PL3 and its flag, finer roles (X3, X6 closed for the Recipe side only)
  24. V-D24Two saves at once never overwrite each other. A save names the revision it started from. If someone saved first and the two touched different lines, the server applies the new change on top (6 B makes this safe: changes are by line id). If they touched the same line, nothing is saved and the person (or their assistant) is told who changed what. Proposal
  25. V-D25Spin off makes a new recipe whose first version is Trying, with "From Egg fried rice" on it; the old version stays, read-only, as "Spun off" with a link across. Proposal
  26. V-D26Word on screen is "version", never "variation". The API moves to "version" too, with the old tool names kept as aliases for a release (spec). Proposal
  27. V-D27Existing data migrates without loss: each recipe becomes a family with its "Original" version (Kept, usual) at revision 1; each variation becomes a version (Kept if active, Put away if disabled) whose revision 1 is its existing change set (variations are already sparse, so 6 B maps them one to one), pinned to its parent's revision 1; old ids keep working. Authors are "before members". Detail on the spec page. Proposal
  28. V-D28The cook log records version and revision (extends gap G2), so "what did we cook on the 8th" shows the recipe exactly as it was then, even after fixes. Proposal
  29. V-D29Nutrition belongs to a revision. A fix that changes ingredients marks the figures "for an earlier revision" until someone records new ones; nothing is recomputed by Recipe. Proposal
  30. V-D31Notes are a field of the version, inherited. A note shows on the version it was written on and every version made from it; a version stores a notes change only where its notes differ (per block, like content). Inherited notes say "From Original"; reworded ones "Reworded here"; hidden ones fold into one line with Show again. Add a note goes on the version in front of you, with "Every version of this recipe" offered (written on the first version). Live inheritance (spec 9.4, sub-option NA, Proposal). Refines R-D24: still the household's, now per lineage. A note after a cook goes on the cooked version with its tag. Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026) Replaces: "Open: notes on the family only, or also per version; default family only".
  31. V-D32Nothing is deleted; anything can be undone, any time. No delete for a try made by mistake: put it away, with undo. History has "Undo this change" on each revision and "Bring back" on a put-away; each undo is a new revision or event, so it can be undone too. Keeping a mistake costs about 1.8 KB (spec 9.10). Only photo files are ever removed (photos slice). Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026) Replaces: "Open: hard delete for a try made by mistake; default none".
  32. V-D33Each member reacts; the household's view is derived. After a cook each member who ate it can say liked or not for me, or skip; anyone can change theirs later on the recipe page. The household sees "Liked by Sam and Jane · 2 of 3 who ate it" and a Household favourite pill when its rule says so (Proposal: more than half of those who ate it, at least 2). A "not for me" is shown by name only to its giver; others see a count. Nothing is stored as a household vote. Locked (owner, typed, 10 Oct 2026) Replaces: "Open: stars per member or household-wide (X5); default household-wide".
  33. V-D30Loading: the version page loads the version's head only (from a head cache, so no change sets are applied). Versions, History, Compare and To review are separate routes, fetched on tap. The ⋯ sheet and the Keep sheet build on tap. One small stylesheet (versions.css) and one small script on top of the flow slice's. Proposal

  2. Deferred: Versions, History (with Who changed what), Compare and To review are routes, loaded on tap; history pages older revisions in on scroll (each older revision is rebuilt from a checkpoint on the server). Sheets are built on tap. Change opens the person's own AI app; nothing of it loads here.
  3. Stays close to the build: .st (with .rvw), .vr, .band, .rv, .df, .ch, .rvc, .rvrow, .verd, .cmp, the notes and reactions classes .note.vn, .nfrom, .nhid, .react, .ate, and the flow slice's .vstrip are meant to be lifted beside the flow slice's classes. Sample names live only in versions.js.
