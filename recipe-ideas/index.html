Cooking ideas

Draft for feedback, 10 October 2026. The "Cooking ideas" pane on Home was a title and a carousel sorted by "you have N of M". This slice decides what fills it, in what order, and why, how a person nudges it without a settings maze, what each idea says in one line, the empty and nothing-fits states, and how an idea becomes planned, held or cooked through Kitchie's existing tools. Sample data only; every phone is live. Open Mockup (bottom right) on any screen to switch person, option and state.

Reconciled 10 October 2026 with the other Recipe tab slices: the ranking's food inputs (avoid, cap, like, dislike) are read from the preferences service, not a second copy of the rules; after schema v6 an idea is the family's usual version and the end of a cook is record_cook; "Not for me" and the like/dislike arithmetic meet the food rules and stay open. Build handoff: recipe-tab-handoff.md. Nothing has been built or filed.

Options for yay, nay or combine

1The pane on Home

A Lead and two: one big idea with Cook now, Plan and Why, then two one-line rows. Decides for you, still shows a choice. B Swipe cards: today's carousel, each card a full lead card. Closest to what is built; hides ideas 2 to 5 behind a swipe. C Rows with tags: four compact rows with a reason tag. Densest, no big button. Recommended: A, because the job on Home is "what's for dinner", which wants one answer you can act on in one tap, with a way out.

ABC

2How a person tunes it

A Tune sheet: a sliders icon in the pane header opens one sheet: Lean towards (one pick of six) and Who is eating tonight. B Chips only: no icon on Home; the lean is the chip strip on See all, and who is eating comes only from the plan. Recommended: A; the icon costs no row, and "who is eating" changes the answer more than anything else.

AB

3The assistant

A Opens your AI app: a quiet "Ask your assistant for more" line; the assistant reads the same ranked ideas and reasons through Kitchie. Nothing it says appears in the pane. B It can pin ideas: the assistant may also leave up to one idea for today in the pane, labelled "From your assistant" with its one-line reason in quotes. Recommended: A now, B later if people ask the assistant and then want to find its answer in the app.

AB

4Where the ranking runs

A Kitchie ranks: it already holds stock, use-by, plan, cooking history and food rules, and asks Recipe for recipes and matches. B Recipe gets a "suggest" tool. C Only the AI ranks, nothing in the app. Recommended: A. B breaks Recipe's rule that the caller reasons and would make Recipe read Kitchie's food rules; C leaves the pane empty for anyone not in a chat. Not drawn; it is an architecture choice.

Decisions in this slice (all Proposal until the owner says)

Numbered I-D so they do not clash with R-D (recipe tab), D and F (preferences) or plan-week-ai's numbers. The same list is in docs/knowledge/jobs-to-be-done/recipe-tab.md.

  1. I-D1The pane answers one question: what to cook next. Tonight before 8pm, tomorrow after. Breakfast and lunch are out of scope until the plan has them. Proposal
  2. I-D2Ideas come from the household's own saved recipes only, plus portions already in the freezer. Nothing invented appears in the pane by default (the assistant is option 3). Proposal
  3. I-D3Kitchie ranks; Recipe answers; the AI explains nothing it did not do. Ranking is a small pure function in Kitchie (option 4 A), deterministic, so the Why sheet can say exactly why. Proposal
  4. I-D4Two passes: filter, then score. Filters never score, scores never exclude. Proposal
  5. I-D5Filters (left out tonight, always listed with the reason on See all): an avoid of anyone eating; a cap already reached; already planned this week; cooked in the last two days; "Not tonight" today; put away. Never hidden silently. Proposal Avoid and cap come from the food rules' evaluate() (preferences K9; cap counted as food Q-F3)
  6. I-D6Score, in this order of weight: ready (all in +3, one missing +1, two ≈0, three or more −2); use it up (+2 an item due in 3 days, +3 due tomorrow, at most +4); rotation (+2 a month or more, +1 two weeks, −2 cooked in the last 4 days, +1.5 never cooked); trying a version +1.5 for its maker; favourite +1; quick on a weeknight +1, over an hour −2; a dislike of someone eating −1.5, a like +1. Ties: fewer missing, then longest since cooked. Numbers are a starting point, tuned with real use. Proposal Open X4: food Q-F4 says a like and a dislike between eaters cancel; here they net −0.5
  7. I-D7One knob: Lean towards. Balanced (default), Use it up, Quick, Favourites, Something different, Batch. A lean doubles one family of reasons; nothing else changes. Batch cooker starts on Batch. Remembered per person (preferences, person level, P2: no gate). Proposal
  8. I-D8Who is eating comes from tonight's plan, or everyone in the household when nothing is planned. The person can change it for tonight only in the Tune sheet. Every filter and weight reads the people eating, not the viewer. Proposal
  9. I-D9A dish already in the freezer is offered as itself ("Have it tonight", reheat), not as cooking it again, and its recipe is not offered separately. Proposal Kitchie gap
  10. I-D10Each idea shows one line, built from its reasons: the strongest reason, then what is missing ("Needs feta, already on your list") or a caveat for someone eating ("Jane is not keen on mushrooms"), else "all in". Never two lines of reasons on the card. Proposal
  11. I-D11See all is the full ranked list with a reason tag per row, the lean as a chip strip (the shared strip, plan-week-ai 48), a "Possible, not a great fit" tail, and "Left out tonight (N)" folded at the bottom. Proposal
  12. I-D12Why is one tap (ⓘ), a sheet listing each reason as + or − with where it came from, and "Nothing here was guessed by an AI." Two quiet answers: Not tonight (off today's ideas) and Not for me (becomes a dislike for the viewer in Kitchie food rules, with Undo). Proposal Open X7: a rule made from a screen (food Q-F6 default: only the assistant makes one)
  13. I-D13Acting on an idea uses Kitchie's existing tools only. Cook now: start_cooking with the recipe id and resolved ingredients (cook mode, R-D18). Plan: the day picker (R-D28) then plan_meal (meal_source recipe), Hold on by default: plan_reserve. The cart: plan_add_missing_to_list after planning, or shopping_add for tonight. Have it tonight (freezer): plan_meal leftover. The end of a cook: cook_meal (stock out) and Recipe record_usage. Recipe holds no stock. Proposal After schema v6: the id is the usual's version id (V-D5); record_cook replaces record_usage (alias kept)
  14. I-D14When tonight is already planned, the plan leads ("Tonight: Egg fried rice"), with two "If plans change" rows under it. A follower sees one. Proposal
  15. I-D15Plan ahead (a chip on See all): pairs each empty night this week with the best idea left, use-it-up on the earliest night, skipping usual days off and nights with a meal. One Plan per night. Not an auto-planner. Proposal
  16. I-D16States. No recipes: "No recipes yet", Add a recipe and Ask your assistant. Under five recipes: ideas plus one line "Ideas get better with more". Pantry unreadable: ideas still ranked on everything else, one line says so, nothing is marked missing (Recipe's "unknown, not empty" rule). Nothing fits: the freezer if there is one, Plan ahead, Ask your assistant. After 8pm: Ideas for tomorrow. Morning: one line to take a freezer dish out. Proposal
  17. I-D17The assistant path is option 3. Either way it calls the same ranking (a read-only Kitchie tool, gap K1) so the chat and the pane never disagree, and anything it makes up is labelled "Made up by your assistant" (plan-week-ai 7c, 26). Proposal
  18. I-D18The pane shows three ideas, See all shows every one. No numbers, scores or percentages on the face; the Why sheet is the only place weights show, as + and −. Proposal
  19. I-D19Seasonality is left out until recipes carry a season tag; it is not cheap today. Nutrition is left out (another decision). Proposal
  20. I-D20Desktop: the pane is the Home side panel (desktop-layout.md), option A's lead card with all ideas below it, no carousel. Not drawn here. Proposal
Gaps (solve where the data lives; Kitchie only renders)
  1. K1Kitchie: one ideas read. A web route and a read-only MCP tool (cooking_ideas: for, lean, date) returning ranked ideas with reasons and left-out, so the pane and the assistant share one answer.
  2. K2Kitchie: link a freezer item to the recipe it came from (recipe id on the item at the end of a batch cook, R-D19). Today the item is a name only.
  3. K3Kitchie: "Not tonight" as a one-day per-person snooze. Small, no history needed.
  4. G10Recipe: variations in what_can_i_cook. It judges canonical recipes only; ideas should judge the household's default version (G4). Either a targets input, or Kitchie calls what_am_i_missing per default version (slower).
  5. G11Recipe: preferences on the list. Star, vote, version status and the default version come back with list_recipes or what_can_i_cook, so ranking needs no call per recipe. Today getPreference is internal and not a tool.
  6. G12Recipe: a "trying" version status (recipe-versions slice) readable in bulk.
  7. G13Recipe: matched name on each verdict. Already there (matched_as); Kitchie needs it to join use-by. No change, noted so it is not removed.
  8. G9Batch facts (existing gap): the closed tag words for freezes and portions.
What loads (DESIGN.md section 6)
  1. Default path, Home: the pane is drawn from one Kitchie call (K1) after the two stock tiles; it never blocks them. Only for a household with Recipes (product boundary); a household without it loads nothing of this.
  2. Deferred: the Why, Tune, Ask and day-picker sheets build on tap (plan-sheet.js is shared with the Recipe tab). See all is its own route. Photos only for the lead and visible rows.
  3. Cost: the ranking is one pass over the household's recipes (bounded by Recipe's 100 per what_can_i_cook), cached per household for a few minutes and dropped when stock, plan or food rules change.
  4. Close to the build: classes .ideas, .idl, .idr, .why, .rtag and the rank function in ideas.js are meant to be lifted; sample signals are only in ideas.js.
